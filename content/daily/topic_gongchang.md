      <h1 class="topic-hero-title">工厂</h1>
      <span class="topic-hero-title-py">gōngchǎng</span>
      <p class="topic-hero-desc">The working vocabulary of a Chinese factory floor, in the order a product moves through it: 打样, 封样, 开模, 大货, 验货, 出货.</p>
    </header>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="lifecycle"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">流程</span>
      <span class="sh-py">liúchéng</span>
      <span class="sh-en">The Product Lifecycle</span>

    <div class="pattern">
      <span class="label">从打样到出货 cóng dǎyàng dào chūhuò · From Sample to Shipment</span>
      <strong>打样 dǎyàng</strong> → make samples<br>
      <strong>确认样 quèrènyàng</strong> → the buyer approves a sample<br>
      <strong>开模 kāimú</strong> → cut production tooling<br>
      <strong>试模 shìmú</strong> → first shots off the new mold (T1, T2 ...)<br>
      <strong>产前样 chǎnqiányàng</strong> → pre-production sample from the real line<br>
      <strong>封样 fēngyàng</strong> → seal the approved sample as the standard<br>
      <strong>大货 dàhuò</strong> → bulk production<br>
      <strong>验货 yànhuò</strong> → inspection<br>
      <strong>出货 chūhuò</strong> → ship out
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="厂">
      <div class="scholar-label">厂里的话 chǎng lǐ de huà · Floor Language</div>
      <p>工厂 (gōngchǎng) is "work shed." The simplified form 厂 reuses an ancient character for a cliff overhang, a shelter with one wall; the traditional form is 廠. On the floor people say 厂里 (chǎng lǐ, "in the factory") and 厂家 (chǎngjiā) for the manufacturer as a business. The vocabulary below is the dialect of that world, much of it unknown to Chinese speakers outside manufacturing: 大货 for the bulk order, 丝 for a hundredth of a millimetre, 唛头 for a carton's shipping mark. Textbook business Chinese skips all of it, and it is what your merchandiser will write in every WeChat message.</p>
      <p>The sequence matters more than any single word. Each stage ends with a 确认 (confirmation), and each confirmation is the reference for the next stage: the approved sample is the standard for the mold, the sealed sample is the standard for bulk, and bulk is inspected against the sealed sample. Disputes later turn on which sample was confirmed, when, and by whom, so learn the stage names well enough to use them in a sentence.</p>
    </div>

          <span class="card-cn">打样</span>
          <span class="card-py">dǎyàng</span>

        <div class="card-def">The verb for producing samples before an order. 打样费 (dǎyàngfèi) is the sampling fee, often refunded against the first order; 打样周期 is how long sampling takes.</div>

            <div class="ex-en">About how many days does sampling take? Can the sampling fee be deducted from the bulk order?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-ochre">

          <span class="card-py">quèrènyàng</span>
          <span class="card-en">approval sample</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">The sample the buyer has approved, usually signed and dated. Everything after it is measured against it, so record exactly which version you approved and what you asked to change.</div>

            <div class="ex-py">Yánsè wǒmen quèrèn le, dànshì gàizi hái yào gǎi, zhège bù néng suàn quèrènyàng.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">We've approved the colour, but the lid still needs changes, so this doesn't count as the approval sample.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">封样</span>
          <span class="card-py">fēngyàng</span>
          <span class="card-en">sealed sample; golden sample</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">"Seal the sample": approved units are labelled, signed by both sides, and kept by each as the binding quality standard for bulk. Also called 标准样 (biāozhǔnyàng) or 金样 (jīnyàng, golden sample). Keep at least one yourself.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">封样一式三份，我们带走一份，你们留两份。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Fēngyàng yí shì sān fèn, wǒmen dàizǒu yí fèn, nǐmen liú liǎng fèn.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Three sealed samples: we take one with us, you keep two.</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">大货要以封样为准，颜色和手感都要一致。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Dàhuò yào yǐ fēngyàng wéi zhǔn, yánsè hé shǒugǎn dōu yào yízhì.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Bulk production must match the sealed sample; colour and feel must both be consistent.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-sienna">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">产前样</span>
          <span class="card-py">chǎnqiányàng</span>
          <span class="card-en">pre-production sample (PPS)</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Made on the production line with production materials, after tooling and before bulk starts. Hand samples can be perfect while line samples are not; insisting on a 产前样 catches the difference before thousands of units exist.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">大货开始前，请先寄五个产前样给我们确认。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Dàhuò kāishǐ qián, qǐng xiān jì wǔ ge chǎnqiányàng gěi wǒmen quèrèn.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Before bulk production starts, please send us five pre-production samples to approve.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="模">
      <div class="scholar-label">谁的模具 shéi de mújù · Who Owns the Mold</div>
      <p>模具 (mújù) covers every custom tool: injection molds, stamping dies, fixtures. 开模 (kāimú, "open a mold") is the act of commissioning one and is often the largest single cost in a new product. The question that matters most is not the price but the ownership: 模具归谁 (mújù guī shéi, "who does the mold belong to?"). A buyer who pays the 模具费 normally owns the tool, but unless the contract says so, and says the factory may not use it for anyone else, the factory holds it physically and can make your part for others. Ask the question in the meeting, then get the answer into the contract; the contract and IP page in this series covers the clauses.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">开模</span>
          <span class="card-py">kāimú</span>
          <span class="card-en">to make a mold; to commission tooling</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">The step that commits real money. 开新模 is new tooling; 共模 (gòngmú) or 公模 (gōngmú) is a shared or public mold the factory already owns, cheaper but not exclusive to you. 私模 (sīmú) is a private mold for one customer.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这个盖子要开新模还是可以用你们的公模？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhège gàizi yào kāi xīn mú háishi kěyǐ yòng nǐmen de gōngmú?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Does this lid need a new mold, or can you use one of your existing open molds?</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">开模到出样需要多长时间？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Kāimú dào chū yàng xūyào duō cháng shíjiān?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">How long from starting the mold to the first samples?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">模具费</span>
          <span class="card-py">mújùfèi</span>
          <span class="card-en">tooling fee</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">The one-off charge for the mold. Common arrangements: paid in full up front, split 50/50 with the balance at sample approval, or refunded (返还 fǎnhuán) once order volume passes an agreed number.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">模具费付清以后，模具归我们所有，对吧？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Mújùfèi fùqīng yǐhòu, mújù guī wǒmen suǒyǒu, duì ba?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Once the tooling fee is paid in full, the mold belongs to us, correct?</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">订单累计到五万个，模具费能不能返还？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Dìngdān lěijì dào wǔ wàn ge, mújùfèi néng bù néng fǎnhuán?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Once cumulative orders reach 50,000 units, can the tooling fee be refunded?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">试模</span>
          <span class="card-py">shìmú</span>
          <span class="card-en">mold trial; first shots</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Running the new tool for the first time. Rounds are numbered T1, T2, T3, said as letters plus Chinese numbers (T一, T二). Each round produces parts to measure; the goal is to stop modifying.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">T1试模的样品下周能寄出来吗？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">T-yī shìmú de yàngpǐn xià zhōu néng jì chūlái ma?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Can the T1 trial samples be sent out next week?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">改模</span>
          <span class="card-py">gǎimú</span>
          <span class="card-en">to modify a mold</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Changing the tool after a trial. The crucial question is who pays: changes caused by the factory's error are on them, changes caused by a new request from you are on you. Agree which is which in writing before work starts. 修模 (xiūmú) is repair or maintenance.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这次改模是你们的问题，费用应该由你们承担。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhè cì gǎimú shì nǐmen de wèntí, fèiyòng yīnggāi yóu nǐmen chéngdān.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">This modification is due to your error, so the cost should be yours.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

          <span class="card-py">túzhǐ</span>

            <div class="ex-en">Please work from the latest drawing; the old versions are void.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">公差</span>
          <span class="card-py">gōngchā</span>

        </div>

            <div class="ex-en">The tolerance at the thread must be held within plus or minus 0.05 mm.</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这批有几个尺寸超差了，装不上。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhè pī yǒu jǐ ge chǐcùn chāochā le, zhuāng bú shàng.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Several dimensions in this batch are out of tolerance; the parts won't fit together.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

        <div class="card-head">

        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Named after two old units of length, 尺 and 寸. 外径 (wàijìng) is outer diameter, 内径 (nèijìng) inner diameter, 壁厚 (bìhòu) wall thickness, 高度 height.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">壁厚能不能再薄一点？现在太重了。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Bìhòu néng bù néng zài báo yìdiǎn? Xiànzài tài zhòng le.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Can the wall be made a little thinner? It's too heavy right now.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">材质</span>
      <span class="sh-py">cáizhì</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Materials and Processes</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="钢">
      <div class="scholar-label">三零四和三一六 sān líng sì hé sān yī liù · Saying the Grade</div>
      <p>Steel grades are read digit by digit: 304 is 三零四 (sān líng sì), 316 is 三一六 (sān yī liù), and 316L adds the letter. 不锈钢 (bùxiùgāng) is "steel that does not rust." 食品级 (shípǐnjí, food grade) is the claim every supplier makes, so ask for the 材质报告 (cáizhì bàogào, material certificate) or a third-party test report rather than accepting the word. A vacuum bottle has an inner liner, 内胆 (nèidǎn, literally "inner gallbladder"), and an outer shell, 外壳 (wàiké); specify the grade for each separately, because a factory can meet "316" on the liner and use 304 or something cheaper on the shell.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">不锈钢</span>
          <span class="card-py">bùxiùgāng</span>
          <span class="card-en">stainless steel</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Grades by number: 201 is cheaper and less corrosion-resistant, 304 is the common food-contact grade, 316 adds molybdenum for better resistance to acids and salt. 材质 (cáizhì) is "material" in the sense of grade and composition.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">内胆用三一六，外壳用三零四，每批都要提供材质报告。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Nèidǎn yòng sān yī liù, wàiké yòng sān líng sì, měi pī dōu yào tígōng cáizhì bàogào.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">316 for the inner liner, 304 for the outer shell, with a material certificate for every batch.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">真空保温</span>
          <span class="card-py">zhēnkōng bǎowēn</span>
          <span class="card-en">vacuum insulation</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Double-wall insulation: the air between liner and shell is pumped out, 抽真空 (chōu zhēnkōng). 保温 is keeping warm, 保冷 keeping cold; 保温测试 is the thermal retention test, and 保温效果 the result.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">保温测试的条件是什么？装多少度的水，测几个小时？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Bǎowēn cèshì de tiáojiàn shì shénme? Zhuāng duōshao dù de shuǐ, cè jǐ ge xiǎoshí?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">What are the thermal test conditions? Water at what temperature, measured over how many hours?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-sienna">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">高硼硅玻璃</span>
          <span class="card-py">gāopéngguī bōli</span>
          <span class="card-en">borosilicate glass</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">"High boron-silicon glass," resistant to thermal shock. 耐热 (nàirè) is heat-resistant, 温差 (wēnchā) the temperature difference it can survive, 爆裂 (bàoliè) bursting or shattering.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这个玻璃能承受多大的温差？有没有冷热冲击测试？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhège bōli néng chéngshòu duō dà de wēnchā? Yǒu méiyǒu lěngrè chōngjī cèshì?</div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="货">
      <div class="scholar-label">出货 chūhuò · Out the Door</div>
      <p>出货 (chūhuò) is the last word of the sequence and the moment leverage changes hands. Before shipment the factory wants your balance payment and you hold it; after shipment the goods are in a container and the factory holds the money. That is why inspection happens before 出货, why the balance is usually due against the inspection report or the bill of lading (提单 tídān), and why a buyer's most useful sentence in the final week is 验货通过以后再付尾款 (yànhuò tōngguò yǐhòu zài fù wěikuǎn): the balance is paid after inspection passes. The price, payment, and contract pages in this series pick up from there.</p>
