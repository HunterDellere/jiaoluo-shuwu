      <h1 class="topic-hero-title">出行</h1>
      <span class="topic-hero-title-py">chūxíng</span>
      <p class="topic-hero-desc">The logistics of a factory trip: high-speed rail tickets on a passport, hailing a Didi to an industrial zone, paying by phone, and checking into a hotel.</p>
    </header>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="rail"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">高铁</span>
      <span class="sh-py">gāotiě</span>
      <span class="sh-en">High-Speed Rail</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">高铁</span>
          <span class="card-py">gāotiě</span>
          <span class="card-en">high-speed rail</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Trains are booked on the official 12306 app or site (it has an English version) or through travel apps, and your passport is the ticket: it is checked at the station entrance and at the gate. 动车 (dòngchē) are slightly slower bullet trains. Arrive early; large stations have security checks.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">我想买一张明天上午去宁波的高铁票，二等座。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yì zhāng míngtiān shàngwǔ qù Níngbō de gāotiě piào, èrděngzuò.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">I'd like one high-speed rail ticket to Ningbo tomorrow morning, second class.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">车次</span>
          <span class="card-py">chēcì</span>
          <span class="card-en">train number</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">G-trains are the fastest, D-trains next. 检票口 (jiǎnpiàokǒu) is the boarding gate, shown on the screens next to the train number; 车厢 (chēxiāng) is the carriage, 座位号 the seat number.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">请问G七五零五在几号检票口？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Qǐngwèn G qī wǔ líng wǔ zài jǐ hào jiǎnpiàokǒu?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Excuse me, which gate is train G7505 at?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="taxi"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">打车</span>
      <span class="sh-py">dǎchē</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Taxis and Didi</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">滴滴</span>
          <span class="card-py">Dīdī</span>
          <span class="card-en">Didi ride-hailing</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">The main ride app, also usable inside Alipay and WeChat. Industrial zones have confusing addresses, so ask the factory to send a 定位 (dìngwèi, location pin) and set that as the destination. 上车点 (shàngchēdiǎn) is the pickup point.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">麻烦发一个工厂的定位给我，我打车过去。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Máfan fā yí ge gōngchǎng de dìngwèi gěi wǒ, wǒ dǎchē guòqù.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Could you send me the factory's location pin? I'll take a car over.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-sienna">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">尾号</span>
          <span class="card-py">wěihào</span>
          <span class="card-en">last digits (of a phone number)</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Drivers confirm the passenger by the last four digits of the phone number: 手机尾号多少? Answer with the digits one by one.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">师傅您好，我尾号是三八二一。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Shīfu nín hǎo, wǒ wěihào shì sān bā èr yāo.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Hello, my number ends in 3821.</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">师傅，到前面那个大门口停就可以了。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Shīfu, dào qiánmiàn nàge dà ménkǒu tíng jiù kěyǐ le.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Driver, you can stop at that main gate ahead.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="pay"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">付款</span>
      <span class="sh-py">fùkuǎn</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Paying by Phone</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="码">
      <div class="scholar-label">扫码 sǎomǎ · Cash Is Rare</div>
      <p>China pays by phone. Foreign visitors can now link an international bank card to Alipay (支付宝 Zhīfùbǎo) and WeChat Pay (微信支付), with a fee on larger payments; set this up and test it before the trip. Two directions to know: you scan the merchant's code (扫码 sǎomǎ, 收款码 shōukuǎnmǎ is their receiving code), or the merchant scans yours (付款码 fùkuǎnmǎ, your payment code). Carry some cash anyway for the occasional place that cannot take foreign-linked cards. Note that 一 is often read 幺 (yāo) in phone and room numbers.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">扫码</span>
          <span class="card-py">sǎomǎ</span>
          <span class="card-en">to scan a code</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">您扫我还是我扫您? (Do you scan me or do I scan you?) is the everyday question at a till.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">我扫您吧，多少钱？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Wǒ sǎo nín ba, duōshao qián?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">I'll scan yours. How much is it?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">入住</span>
          <span class="card-py">rùzhù</span>
          <span class="card-en">to check in</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Hotels must register foreign guests' passports, and not every hotel is licensed to take foreigners, so book one that confirms it does. 押金 (yājīn) is the deposit, 退房 (tuìfáng) checkout. For a company invoice, give the 抬头 (táitóu, the invoice name).</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">您好，我在网上订了房，这是我的护照。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Nín hǎo, wǒ zài wǎngshang dìng le fáng, zhè shì wǒ de hùzhào.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Hello, I booked a room online; here is my passport.</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">退房的时候能开发票吗？抬头写我们公司的名字。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Tuìfáng de shíhou néng kāi fāpiào ma? Táitóu xiě wǒmen gōngsī de míngzi.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Can I get an invoice at checkout? Please make it out to our company.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
