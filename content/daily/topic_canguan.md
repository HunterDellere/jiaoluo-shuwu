      <h1 class="topic-hero-title">参观工厂</h1>
      <span class="topic-hero-title-py">cānguān gōngchǎng</span>
      <p class="topic-hero-desc">What you see on a Chinese factory tour and what to ask about it: workshops, lines, warehouses, inspection points, certifications, and the questions that show whether the factory really makes your product.</p>
    </header>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="floor"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">车间</span>
      <span class="sh-py">chējiān</span>
      <span class="sh-en">On the Floor</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="观">
      <div class="scholar-label">看什么 kàn shénme · What the Tour Is For</div>
      <p>参观 (cānguān) is "to visit and look," and the factory will show you its best line. The tour is still the one chance to see whether the company that quoted you makes the product itself, or sends parts of it out to other workshops (外发 wàifā, or 外包 wàibāo). Look for the processes your product needs on the floor: if a bottle needs deep drawing, welding, vacuum sealing, and powder coating, and you only see assembly and packing, ask where the rest happens. Ask permission before taking photos (可以拍照吗 kěyǐ pāizhào ma?); most factories allow it outside rooms with other customers' products.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">车间</span>
          <span class="card-py">chējiān</span>
          <span class="card-en">workshop, shop floor</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Named by process: 冲压车间 stamping, 焊接车间 welding, 注塑车间 injection molding, 喷涂车间 painting, 组装车间 assembly, 包装车间 packing. 无尘车间 (wúchén chējiān) is a clean room.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">抽真空是在这个车间做的吗？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Chōu zhēnkōng shì zài zhège chējiān zuò de ma?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Is the vacuum sealing done in this workshop?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">生产线</span>
          <span class="card-py">shēngchǎnxiàn</span>
          <span class="card-en">production line</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Also 流水线 (liúshuǐxiàn, "flowing-water line"), an assembly line. 工位 (gōngwèi) is a workstation, 工序 (gōngxù) a process step. 自动化 (zìdònghuà) is automation, 人工 manual work.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这条线一共有多少道工序？哪些是自动化的？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhè tiáo xiàn yígòng yǒu duōshao dào gōngxù? Nǎxiē shì zìdònghuà de?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">How many process steps does this line have in total? Which are automated?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">设备</span>
          <span class="card-py">shèbèi</span>
          <span class="card-en">equipment, machinery</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">机器 (jīqì) is a single machine. Ask whether equipment is owned and how old it is; 进口设备 (imported equipment) is a point of pride.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这些设备是你们自己的吗？用了几年了？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhèxiē shèbèi shì nǐmen zìjǐ de ma? Yòng le jǐ nián le?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Is this equipment yours? How many years has it been in use?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-sienna">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">仓库</span>
          <span class="card-py">cāngkù</span>
          <span class="card-en">warehouse</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">原材料仓 raw-material store, 成品仓 finished-goods store. A tidy warehouse with labelled batches (标识 biāoshí) is a good sign of traceability.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">原材料进来以后怎么标识？能追溯到批次吗？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Yuáncáiliào jìnlái yǐhòu zěnme biāoshí? Néng zhuīsù dào pīcì ma?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">How are raw materials labelled when they arrive? Can you trace them to the batch?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="qc"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">质检</span>
      <span class="sh-py">zhìjiǎn</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Inspection Points and Certificates</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="pattern">
      <span class="label">三道检验 sān dào jiǎnyàn · Three Checkpoints</span>
      <strong>来料检验 IQC láiliào jiǎnyàn</strong> → incoming materials<br>
      <strong>过程检验 IPQC guòchéng jiǎnyàn</strong> → during production, on the line<br>
      <strong>出货检验 OQC chūhuò jiǎnyàn</strong> → finished goods before shipment
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">质检</span>
          <span class="card-py">zhìjiǎn</span>
          <span class="card-en">quality inspection</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Short for 质量检验. 品管 (pǐnguǎn) or 品质部 is the quality department. Ask to see the inspection records (检验记录 jiǎnyàn jìlù) for a recent batch, not only the procedure.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">能看一下上周出货的检验记录吗？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Néng kàn yíxià shàng zhōu chūhuò de jiǎnyàn jìlù ma?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Could I see the inspection records for last week's shipment?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">认证</span>
          <span class="card-py">rènzhèng</span>
          <span class="card-en">certification</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">ISO 9001 is 质量管理体系认证 (the quality management system certificate). 验厂 (yànchǎng) is a factory audit, such as BSCI for social compliance. Check the certificate's 有效期 and that the company name matches the one you are contracting with.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">你们的ISO证书是哪个公司名下的？有效期到什么时候？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Nǐmen de ISO zhèngshū shì nǎge gōngsī míng xià de? Yǒuxiàoqī dào shénme shíhou?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Which company is your ISO certificate issued to, and until when is it valid?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="ask"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">提问</span>
      <span class="sh-py">tíwèn</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Questions to Ask on the Floor</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">外发</span>
          <span class="card-py">wàifā</span>
          <span class="card-en">to send out (to a subcontractor)</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">The question the tour exists to answer. A factory that sends out a step is not necessarily hiding anything, but you need to know, because quality on that step is out of its direct control.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">有没有哪道工序是外发的？外发的话，你们怎么管控质量？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Yǒu méiyǒu nǎ dào gōngxù shì wàifā de? Wàifā de huà, nǐmen zěnme guǎnkòng zhìliàng?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Is any process step sent out? If so, how do you control its quality?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">班</span>
          <span class="card-py">bān</span>
          <span class="card-en">shift</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">两班倒 (liǎng bān dǎo) is two rotating shifts; 加班 (jiābān) overtime. Shifts and headcount tell you real capacity better than a capacity figure does.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">你们现在是几班？一共多少工人？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Nǐmen xiànzài shì jǐ bān? Yígòng duōshao gōngrén?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">How many shifts are you running now, and how many workers in total?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-sienna">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">客户</span>
          <span class="card-py">kèhù</span>
          <span class="card-en">customer</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Factories like to name famous customers. The useful questions are which markets they ship to, and how much of the output the largest customer takes (占比 zhànbǐ, share), since a dominant customer gets the line first in peak season.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">你们主要出口哪些市场？最大的客户大概占多少比例？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Nǐmen zhǔyào chūkǒu nǎxiē shìchǎng? Zuì dà de kèhù dàgài zhàn duōshao bǐlì?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Which markets do you mainly export to? Roughly what share does your largest customer take?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
