      <span class="topic-hero-eyebrow">Everyday Life · 日常 rìcháng</span>
      <h1 class="topic-hero-title">合同</h1>
      <span class="topic-hero-title-py">hétong</span>
      <p class="topic-hero-desc">The contract vocabulary a buyer needs at a Chinese factory: which language governs, which seal binds, NNN agreements, patents and trademarks, tooling ownership, penalties, and arbitration.</p>
    </header>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="basics"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">合同</span>
      <span class="sh-py">hétong</span>
      <span class="sh-en">The Contract Itself</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="合">
      <div class="scholar-label">字面意思 zìmiàn yìsi · Agreeing Together</div>
      <p>合同 (hétong) is "join and match," from the old practice of writing an agreement twice and splitting the paper, so each side's half matched the other. A Chinese factory contract is usually short, and what is left out is decided later by whoever has the leverage. Three questions decide how much a contract protects you: which language version governs, whether it is sealed by the right legal entity, and where a dispute will be heard. This page covers the vocabulary for each. It is a vocabulary guide, not legal advice; have a lawyer who practises in China read anything that matters.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">以中文版为准</span>
          <span class="card-py">yǐ Zhōngwén bǎn wéi zhǔn</span>
          <span class="card-en">the Chinese version governs</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">A bilingual contract needs a clause saying which version prevails if they conflict (如有冲突 rú yǒu chōngtū). Chinese courts and arbitrators work in Chinese, so the Chinese text is the one that will be read. Have it translated back to you independently rather than trusting that the two versions match.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">本合同中英文各一份，如有冲突，以中文版为准。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Běn hétong Zhōng-Yīngwén gè yí fèn, rú yǒu chōngtū, yǐ Zhōngwén bǎn wéi zhǔn.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">This contract is in Chinese and English; in case of conflict, the Chinese version governs.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">条款</span>
          <span class="card-py">tiáokuǎn</span>
          <span class="card-en">clause, term</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">第五条 (dì-wǔ tiáo) is Article 5. 补充协议 (bǔchōng xiéyì) is a supplementary agreement, the usual way to add terms later. 附件 (fùjiàn) is an annex, where specifications and drawings belong.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">技术要求写在附件一里，附件和合同具有同等效力。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Jìshù yāoqiú xiě zài fùjiàn yī lǐ, fùjiàn hé hétong jùyǒu tóngděng xiàolì.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">The technical requirements are in Annex 1, which has the same force as the contract.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">公章</span>
          <span class="card-py">gōngzhāng</span>
          <span class="card-en">company seal</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">A Chinese company binds itself with its red seal more than with a signature. 合同专用章 (hétong zhuānyòngzhāng) is a seal used only for contracts and is equally valid. Check that the name on the seal matches the name on the 营业执照 (yíngyè zhízhào, business licence) and the 统一社会信用代码 (the 18-character company registration code). A seal of a different company, or a 部门章 (department seal), is a warning sign.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">合同要盖公司公章或者合同专用章，法人签字。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Hétong yào gài gōngsī gōngzhāng huòzhě hétong zhuānyòngzhāng, fǎrén qiānzì.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">The contract must carry the company seal or the contract seal, signed by the legal representative.</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">能发一下营业执照吗？我们核对一下公司名称。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Néng fā yíxià yíngyè zhízhào ma? Wǒmen héduì yíxià gōngsī míngchēng.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Could you send your business licence? We'd like to check the company name.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="ip"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">知识产权</span>
      <span class="sh-py">zhīshi chǎnquán</span>
      <span class="sh-en">IP and Confidentiality</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="权">
      <div class="scholar-label">先申请先得 xiān shēnqǐng xiān dé · First to File Wins</div>
      <p>China awards patents and trademarks to whoever files first, not to whoever used the design first. A registration in your home country gives you nothing in China. The defensive steps are to register your trademark (商标 shāngbiāo) in China, in Chinese characters as well as Latin letters, before you show a design around, and to sign a confidentiality agreement written for Chinese law. A Western-style NDA alone is weak; the common form for China is the NNN agreement: 不使用、不泄露、不规避 (no use, no disclosure, no circumvention), in Chinese, governed by Chinese law, enforceable in a Chinese court.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-sienna">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">保密协议</span>
          <span class="card-py">bǎomì xiéyì</span>
          <span class="card-en">confidentiality agreement</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">The general term for an NDA. NNN协议 is the China-specific form. Ask for it to be signed and sealed before you send drawings, not after.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">发图纸之前，我们需要先签一份保密协议。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Fā túzhǐ zhīqián, wǒmen xūyào xiān qiān yí fèn bǎomì xiéyì.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Before we send drawings, we need to sign a confidentiality agreement.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">专利</span>
          <span class="card-py">zhuānlì</span>
          <span class="card-en">patent</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">China has three kinds: 发明专利 (fāmíng zhuānlì) invention patents, 实用新型 (shíyòng xīnxíng) utility models for structural improvements, and 外观设计 (wàiguān shèjì) design patents for appearance. Design patents and utility models are faster and cheaper to obtain, and are the ones most often used against copies of consumer products.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这个外观我们已经在中国申请了外观设计专利。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhège wàiguān wǒmen yǐjīng zài Zhōngguó shēnqǐng le wàiguān shèjì zhuānlì.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">We've already filed a design patent in China for this appearance.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">模具归属</span>
          <span class="card-py">mújù guīshǔ</span>
          <span class="card-en">tooling ownership</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">The clause that states who owns the mold, that it may only be used for your orders, and that the factory must release it on request. 归 is "belongs to," 所有权 (suǒyǒuquán) ownership.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">模具所有权归买方，未经书面同意，不得为第三方生产。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Mújù suǒyǒuquán guī mǎifāng, wèi jīng shūmiàn tóngyì, bùdé wèi dì-sān fāng shēngchǎn.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">The buyer owns the tooling; without written consent it may not be used to produce for any third party.</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">合作结束的话，模具要无条件交还给我们。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Hézuò jiéshù de huà, mújù yào wú tiáojiàn jiāohuán gěi wǒmen.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">If the partnership ends, the mold is to be returned to us unconditionally.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="remedies"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">违约</span>
      <span class="sh-py">wéiyuē</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Breach and Disputes</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">违约金</span>
          <span class="card-py">wéiyuējīn</span>
          <span class="card-en">liquidated damages</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">A fixed sum payable on breach, such as late delivery. Under Chinese law a court or arbitrator can reduce it if it is far above the actual loss, so set it at a level you can justify. 赔偿 (péicháng) is compensation for actual loss.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">每延迟一天，按订单金额的千分之五支付违约金。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Měi yánchí yì tiān, àn dìngdān jīn'é de qiān fēn zhī wǔ zhīfù wéiyuējīn.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">For each day of delay, liquidated damages of 0.5% of the order value are payable.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">质保期</span>
          <span class="card-py">zhìbǎoqī</span>
          <span class="card-en">warranty period</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">How long the factory stands behind the goods. Pair it with 验收标准 (yànshōu biāozhǔn), the acceptance standard, normally the sealed sample and an AQL level.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">质保期为出货后十二个月，验收标准以封样为准。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhìbǎoqī wéi chūhuò hòu shí'èr ge yuè, yànshōu biāozhǔn yǐ fēngyàng wéi zhǔn.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">The warranty is twelve months from shipment; acceptance is judged against the sealed sample.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-sienna">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">仲裁</span>
          <span class="card-py">zhòngcái</span>
          <span class="card-en">arbitration</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">A foreign court judgment is hard to enforce in China, while an arbitration award can be enforced under the New York Convention. Many contracts name 中国国际经济贸易仲裁委员会 (CIETAC) or the Hong Kong (HKIAC) or Singapore (SIAC) centres. 管辖 (guǎnxiá) is jurisdiction; 适用法律 (shìyòng fǎlǜ) the governing law.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">因本合同发生的争议，提交中国国际经济贸易仲裁委员会仲裁。</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Yīn běn hétong fāshēng de zhēngyì, tíjiāo Zhōngguó Guójì Jīngjì Màoyì Zhòngcái Wěiyuánhuì zhòngcái.</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Disputes arising from this contract are submitted to CIETAC for arbitration.</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
