      <h1 class="topic-hero-title">水杯</h1>
      <span class="topic-hero-title-py">shuǐbēi</span>
      <p class="topic-hero-desc">The process vocabulary of a drinkware factory: forming and welding a stainless vacuum bottle, how the vacuum is made and tested, lids and plastic parts, glass, food-contact standards, and custom branding.</p>
    </header>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="anatomy"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">结构</span>
      <span class="sh-py">jiégòu</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Parts of a Bottle</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="杯">
      <div class="scholar-label">杯和瓶 bēi hé píng · Cup or Bottle</div>
      <p>Chinese sorts drinkware by shape before material. 杯 (bēi) is anything you drink from, so a vacuum bottle is a 保温杯 (bǎowēnbēi, "keep-warm cup") or 真空杯 even when it has a narrow mouth; 瓶 (píng) suggests a narrower neck, as in 水瓶 or 运动水壶 (yùndòng shuǐhú, sports bottle). In a factory the product is named by its parts, and a quote or a complaint is always about one of them, so learn the anatomy first. The general factory vocabulary (samples, molds, tolerances, defects, packing) is on the factory-words page; this page is the drinkware-specific layer.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="pattern">
      <span class="label">一个保温杯 yí ge bǎowēnbēi · Anatomy of a Vacuum Bottle</span>
      <strong>杯身 bēishēn</strong> → the body<br>
      <strong>内胆 nèidǎn</strong> → inner liner, the wall that touches the drink<br>
      <strong>外壳 wàiké</strong> → outer shell<br>
      <strong>杯口 / 瓶口 bēikǒu / píngkǒu</strong> → the mouth; 宽口 wide, 窄口 narrow<br>
      <strong>螺纹 luówén</strong> → the thread; 外螺纹 external, 内螺纹 internal<br>
      <strong>杯盖 bēigài</strong> → the lid; 内盖 inner stopper, 外盖 outer cap<br>
      <strong>密封圈 mìfēngquān</strong> → the seal ring, usually silicone<br>
      <strong>杯底 / 底盖 bēidǐ / dǐgài</strong> → the base and its cover, which hides the vacuum seal point<br>
      <strong>提手 tíshǒu</strong> → handle or carry loop
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">容量</span>
          <span class="card-py">róngliàng</span>
          <span class="card-en">capacity</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Two numbers can be meant: 标称容量 (biāochēng, nominal, the number on the box) and 满口容量 (mǎnkǒu, filled to the brim). Ask which one a drawing or quote uses. 克重 (kèzhòng) is the weight in grams.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">五百毫升是标称容量还是满口容量？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Wǔbǎi háoshēng shì biāochēng róngliàng háishi mǎnkǒu róngliàng?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Is 500 ml the nominal capacity or the brim-full capacity?</div>
          </div>
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">这款杯子空杯克重是多少？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Zhè kuǎn bēizi kōng bēi kèzhòng shì duōshao?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">What does this bottle weigh empty?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="forming"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">成型</span>
      <span class="sh-py">chéngxíng</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Forming and Welding Steel</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="pattern">
      <span class="label">不锈钢杯的工序 bùxiùgāng bēi de gōngxù · The Stainless Line, in Order</span>
      <strong>下料 xiàliào</strong> → cut the blank from sheet (板材) or tube (管材)<br>
      <strong>拉伸 lāshēn</strong> → deep-draw a cup shape from sheet<br>
      <strong>旋压 xuányā</strong> → spin-form the shoulder and neck<br>
      <strong>水胀 shuǐzhàng</strong> → hydroform: shape the body outward with water pressure<br>
      <strong>缩口 suōkǒu</strong> → neck down the mouth<br>
      <strong>滚牙 gǔnyá</strong> → roll the thread<br>
      <strong>焊接 hànjiē</strong> → weld liner and shell together at the mouth, and weld on the bases<br>
      <strong>抽真空 chōu zhēnkōng</strong> → evacuate the gap between the walls<br>
      <strong>测温 cèwēn</strong> → screen every unit for insulation<br>
      <strong>电解 / 抛光 diànjiě / pāoguāng</strong> → electropolish the liner, polish the shell<br>
      <strong>喷涂 pēntú</strong> → coat the outside, then print the logo<br>
      <strong>组装 / 包装 zǔzhuāng / bāozhuāng</strong> → assemble lids and pack
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">管材</span>
          <span class="card-py">guǎncái</span>
          <span class="card-en">tube stock</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Bodies are made either from welded tube (管材, cut to length) or drawn from sheet (板材 bǎncái). Tube needs a lengthwise seam weld; a drawn body has no side seam. Both work; the question is how the seam is finished and checked.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">内胆是管材做的还是板材拉伸的？有没有纵向焊缝？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Nèidǎn shì guǎncái zuò de háishi bǎncái lāshēn de? Yǒu méiyǒu zòngxiàng hànfèng?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Is the liner made from tube or drawn from sheet? Is there a lengthwise weld seam?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">激光焊</span>
          <span class="card-py">jīguānghàn</span>
          <span class="card-en">laser welding</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">The usual way to join liner and shell at the mouth; 氩弧焊 (yàhúhàn, TIG welding) is the older method. 焊缝 (hànfèng) is the weld seam. A weld that is not fully sealed (焊不牢 or 虚焊 xūhàn, a false weld) lets air back in and the bottle loses its insulation weeks later.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">口部用激光焊吗？焊缝怎么检测？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Kǒubù yòng jīguānghàn ma? Hànfèng zěnme jiǎncè?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Is the mouth laser-welded? How are the seams checked?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-sienna">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">电解</span>
          <span class="card-py">diànjiě</span>
          <span class="card-en">electropolishing</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Smooths the inside of the liner so it cleans easily and resists staining and odour. Ask whether every liner is electropolished or only polished mechanically.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">内胆都做电解处理吗？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Nèidǎn dōu zuò diànjiě chǔlǐ ma?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Are all the liners electropolished?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <span class="section-anchor" id="vacuum"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">真空</span>
      <span class="sh-py">zhēnkōng</span>
      <span class="sh-en">The Vacuum</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="空">
      <div class="scholar-label">无尾真空 wúwěi zhēnkōng · Sealing Without a Tail</div>
      <p>The insulation is the empty space between the two walls. Older bottles were evacuated through a small tube at the base, then pinched and sealed, leaving a "tail" (尾巴 wěiba). Most current production is 无尾 (wúwěi, tailless): bottles go base-down into a vacuum furnace (真空炉 zhēnkōnglú) with a solder pellet (钎料 qiānliào, or 焊料) over a hole in the base; the furnace draws out the air, then melts the solder to seal the hole, which is why 抽真空 is also called 真空钎焊 (zhēnkōng qiānhàn, vacuum brazing). A getter (吸气剂 xīqìjì) inside the gap absorbs gas that seeps out of the steel over time. Many factories also copper-plate the liner's outer surface (镀铜 dùtóng) to cut radiant heat loss. A vacuum that fails shows up as a bottle whose outside gets warm, 不保温 or 漏气 (lòuqì, leaking air).</p>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-red">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">测温</span>
          <span class="card-py">cèwēn</span>
          <span class="card-en">temperature screening</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">After vacuuming, each bottle is filled with hot water and its outer wall checked by hand or infrared (红外 hóngwài) to catch failed vacuums. It catches gross failures on the day, not slow leaks, which is why a sample is also held for a longer test (放置 fàngzhì, left to stand) before shipment.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">测温是全检还是抽检？放置几天以后再测一次吗？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Cèwēn shì quánjiǎn háishi chōujiǎn? Fàngzhì jǐ tiān yǐhòu zài cè yí cì ma?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Is temperature screening done on every unit or sampled? Do you test again after letting them stand a few days?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-teal">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">镀铜</span>
          <span class="card-py">dùtóng</span>
          <span class="card-en">copper plating</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">On the outside of the liner, inside the vacuum gap, where it reflects heat. It never touches the drink. Ask whether it is used, since it changes insulation performance at the same wall thickness.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">内胆外壁有没有镀铜？保温效果差多少？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Nèidǎn wàibì yǒu méiyǒu dùtóng? Bǎowēn xiàoguǒ chà duōshao?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">Is the liner's outer wall copper-plated? How much difference does it make to insulation?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-ochre">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">保温效果</span>
          <span class="card-py">bǎowēn xiàoguǒ</span>
          <span class="card-en">insulation performance</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">The Chinese national standard for stainless vacuum cups, GB/T 29606, defines a test with water at 95°C measured after a set number of hours. Specify the conditions when you compare claims: starting temperature, fill level, lid on, room temperature, hours.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">您说保温十二小时，是按国标测的吗？初始水温和环境温度是多少？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Nín shuō bǎowēn shí'èr xiǎoshí, shì àn guóbiāo cè de ma? Chūshǐ shuǐwēn hé huánjìng wēndù shì duōshao?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">You say it keeps warm for twelve hours. Was that tested to the national standard? What were the starting water temperature and the room temperature?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-violet">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">杯盖</span>
          <span class="card-py">bēigài</span>
          <span class="card-en">lid</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">Most leaks and most complaints are in the lid, not the steel. Types: 螺旋盖 (screw cap), 弹跳盖 (tántiào gài, flip or push-button lid), 吸管盖 (xīguǎn gài, straw lid), 茶隔 (chágé, tea strainer). Each moving part is another seal to test.</div>
        <div class="examples">
          <div class="example">
            <div class="ex-cn">弹跳盖的按钮能按多少次？做过寿命测试吗？</div>
            <div class="ex-py">Tántiào gài de ànniǔ néng àn duōshao cì? Zuò guo shòumìng cèshì ma?</div>
            <div class="ex-en">How many presses is the flip-lid button rated for? Has it had a lifespan test?</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card c-sienna">
        <div class="card-head">

    <span class="section-anchor" id="testing"></span>
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="sh-cn">测试</span>
      <span class="sh-py">cèshì</span>
      <span class="sh-en">Testing and Food Safety</span>
      <span class="sh-rule"></span>
    </div>

    <div class="scholar" data-glyph="检">
      <div class="scholar-label">食品级的证据 shípǐnjí de zhèngjù · Proof of Food Grade</div>
      <p>食品级 (food grade) is a claim, and the evidence for it is a migration test: a lab fills the part with food simulants and measures what comes out (迁移 qiānyí, migration), including heavy metals (重金属 zhòngjīnshǔ). China's food-contact standards are the GB 4806 series, with separate parts for metals, plastics, rubber, and glass; export markets add FDA (US) and LFGB (Germany). Ask for a report that names your material and your coating, is recent, and comes from an accredited lab. Steel grade is a separate check: a magnet cannot reliably tell 304 from cheaper 201, so ask for a 光谱仪 (guāngpǔyí, spectrometer) reading, which shows nickel and, for 316, molybdenum (钼 mù).</p>
    </div>

    <div class="cards">
      <div class="card c-sienna">
        <div class="card-head">
          <span class="card-cn">光谱仪</span>
          <span class="card-py">guāngpǔyí</span>
          <span class="card-en">spectrometer</span>
        </div>
        <div class="card-def">A handheld analyser gives a material reading in seconds on the floor. Ask to see it on a liner from your own production.</div>
        <div class="examples">
