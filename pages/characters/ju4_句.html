字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

句 jù pairs a wrapping element with 口 (kǒu, mouth). The curved stroke that bends over the top encloses the mouth below, and the early sense of the graph is to bend, hook, or wrap around. Speech wrapped and tied off into a finished unit is a 句: a stretch of words bundled together and brought to a stop. The 口 supplies the idea of the mouth and what it utters; the hooking element supplies the wrapping that closes the unit off.

句 carries a second, older reading gōu (to hook; crooked), preserved mainly in proper names such as 句践 Gōu Jiàn, the famous king of Yuè. In modern usage that reading has largely passed to the character 勾 gōu, while 句 itself settled onto jù and the field of speech. This page follows the jù reading throughout.

Three senses branch from the bundled utterance:

1. A sentence or clause. The complete grammatical unit: 句子 jùzi (sentence), 句号 jùhào (the full stop that closes it), 从句 cóngjù (subordinate clause).
2. A phrase or line. A shorter run of words, especially a line of poetry: 词句 cíjù (wording, phrasing), 诗句 shījù (a line of verse), 名句 míngjù (a famous line).
3. The measure word for utterances. 句 counts spoken remarks and lines: 一句话 yī jù huà (one remark), 说几句 shuō jǐ jù (to say a few words).

The thread through every sense is the same: a 句 is speech tied off into a unit you can count, write down, or quote.

句子 jùzi Sentences & Punctuation
句子 jùzi sentence
N 名词 míngcí
句 jù (sentence) + 子 zi (noun-suffix). The everyday word for a complete sentence as a grammatical unit: 一个句子 "one sentence," 简单的句子 "a simple sentence." The building block every grammar lesson works with.
这个句子太长了。
Zhège jùzi tài cháng le.
This sentence is too long.
请用这个词造一个句子。
Qǐng yòng zhège cí zào yí ge jùzi.
Please make a sentence with this word.
句号 jùhào full stop; period
N 名词 míngcí
句 jù (sentence) + 号 hào (mark; sign). The punctuation mark that closes a sentence, written in Chinese as a small hollow circle 。 rather than a dot. Idiomatically 画上句号 "to draw the full stop" means to bring something to a close.
别忘了在句子末尾加句号。
Bié wàng le zài jùzi mòwěi jiā jùhào.
Do not forget to add a full stop at the end of the sentence.
造句 zàojù to make a sentence; to compose a sentence
VO 动宾 dòngbīn
造 zào (to make; to build) + 句 jù (sentence). The classroom exercise of building a sentence to practice a new word, a verb-object compound: 用 "因为" 造句 "make a sentence with 'because'."
老师让我们用新词造句。
Lǎoshī ràng wǒmen yòng xīn cí zàojù.
The teacher had us make sentences with the new words.
辨析 biànxī · 句 vs. 词 vs. 字 Three nested units of written Chinese. 字 zì is a single character, the smallest written unit. 词 cí is a word, one or more characters that carry a meaning. 句 jù is a sentence, a string of words tied off into a complete thought. A 句 is built of 词, and each 词 is built of 字.
诗句 shījù Phrases & Lines of Verse
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Below the full sentence, 句 names the shorter run of words: the phrase, the wording, and above all the line of verse. Classical Chinese poetry is measured in 句, and a poem is admired or quoted line by line. A line that everyone knows by heart becomes a 名句 míngjù, a famous line, the kind of phrase that detaches from its poem and circulates on its own. To say someone speaks in 词句 cíjù is to comment on their choice of wording, the texture of the phrases they pick.

This is the 句 of the writer and the reader rather than the grammarian: the line on the page that can be weighed, recited, and remembered as a unit of craft.

诗句 shījù a line of verse; lines of poetry
N 名词 míngcí
诗 shī (poem) + 句 jù (line). A line or verse of poetry, the unit a poem is built and quoted in: 优美的诗句 "beautiful lines of verse." Often used in the plural sense, the lines of a poem taken together.
他能背很多古代诗句。
Tā néng bèi hěn duō gǔdài shījù.
He can recite many lines of classical verse.
名句 míngjù a famous line; a well-known quotation
N 名词 míngcí
名 míng (famous) + 句 jù (line). A celebrated line of verse or prose, so well known that it stands on its own apart from the work it came from. The pairing 千古名句 "a famous line for the ages" praises a line that has lasted.
这是一句流传千古的名句。
Zhè shì yí jù liúchuán qiāngǔ de míngjù.
This is a famous line that has been passed down through the ages.
词句 cíjù words and phrases; wording; phrasing
N 名词 míngcí
词 cí (word) + 句 jù (phrase). Words and phrases taken together, that is, the wording of a text: 推敲词句 "to weigh one's wording carefully." It frames language at the level of phrasing rather than whole sentences.
他对文章的词句很讲究。
Tā duì wénzhāng de cíjù hěn jiǎngjiu.
He is very particular about the wording of his writing.
一句话 yī jù huà The Measure Word for Remarks
语法洞见 yǔfǎ dòngjiàn · Grammatical Insight

The most frequent job 句 does in conversation is as a measure word: it counts spoken remarks, lines, and sentences. 一句话 yī jù huà is "one remark" or "a single sentence," 两句 liǎng jù is "a couple of lines," and 说几句 shuō jǐ jù is "to say a few words." The thing being counted is usually 话 huà (speech) or is left understood, so 我跟他说了几句 means "I exchanged a few words with him."

Because it counts whole utterances, 句 carries a faint sense of brevity and weight. 一句话 can mean "in a word," the gist boiled down to a single sentence, as in 一句话, 我同意 "in short, I agree." And 多嘴多舌 aside, even one well-placed 句 can matter: a single remark, 一句话, can decide a quarrel or settle a doubt.

一句话 yī jù huà one remark; a single sentence; in a word
MW 量词 liàngcí
一 yī (one) + 句 jù (measure word) + 话 huà (speech). A single spoken remark, counted with 句. Used adverbially, 一句话 means "in a word, to put it briefly."
他一句话就把我说服了。
Tā yí jù huà jiù bǎ wǒ shuōfú le.
He convinced me with a single remark.
说几句 shuō jǐ jù to say a few words
V 动词 dòngcí
说 shuō (to say) + 几 jǐ (a few) + 句 jù (measure word). To make a few brief remarks, the polite formula a speaker uses before a short toast or comment: 我想说几句 "I would like to say a few words."
请允许我说几句。
Qǐng yǔnxǔ wǒ shuō jǐ jù.
Please allow me to say a few words.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
一字一句 yī zì yī jù word by word, line by line: spoken or read with careful deliberation Lit: one-character-one-sentence. Describes speaking, reading, or copying out so slowly and clearly that each character and each phrase lands on its own. 一字一句地说 "to say it word by word."
字斟句酌 zì zhēn jù zhuó to weigh every word and phrase: to choose one's wording with great care Lit: character-weigh-sentence-ponder. 斟 and 酌 both mean to pour and measure out wine, here borrowed for measuring out language. Said of a writer or speaker who deliberates over every word.
三言两语 sān yán liǎng yǔ three words and two phrases: in just a few words, briefly Lit: three-words-two-phrases. A set phrase for saying something in very few words: 三言两语说不清 "it cannot be explained in a few words." A close cousin to counting things out in 句.
语重心长 yǔ zhòng xīn cháng weighty words from a deep heart: sincere and earnest counsel Lit: words-heavy-heart-long. Praises advice that is heartfelt and meant to last, the kind of remark an elder offers a younger person. Reminds us that a single 句 can carry great weight.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

See the 口 mouth at the bottom of 句, and a stroke curling over the top to wrap the speech and tie it off. That bundle of tied-off speech is the whole character: a unit you can count, write, or quote.

Make the bundle a complete thought and it is a sentence (句子), closed by the small circle of a 句号. Make it a line on the page and it is a 诗句, a verse worth remembering as a 名句. Hold up a finger and count, and 句 becomes the measure word for what the mouth says: 一句话, one remark. Whenever you meet 句, picture the mouth and the wrapping stroke, and ask which bundle of speech is being tied off.
