字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

定 dìng pairs the roof radical 宀 (mián) over a settling element below. The top is 宀, the pictograph of a roof with two side walls, which appears across the dwelling family (家 home, 室 room, 安 peace). Beneath it sits an element that the early forms show as something coming to rest, a thing brought down and made still within the house. The whole reads as a matter settled under a roof: brought indoors, set in place, and no longer moving. From that image of stillness fixed in a dwelling grows everything 定 means.

Three senses branch from the settled thing under the roof:

1. To decide, to set, to stipulate. Bringing an open question to rest by fixing it: 决定 juédìng (to decide), 规定 guīdìng (to stipulate), 确定 quèdìng (to confirm), 预定 yùdìng (to book in advance).
2. Fixed, settled, certain. The state of a thing that no longer wavers: 一定 yídìng (definitely), 固定 gùdìng (fixed), 肯定 kěndìng (certainly).
3. Calm, stable, composed. Inner and outer steadiness: 稳定 wěndìng (stable), 安定 āndìng (settled; peaceful), 镇定 zhèndìng (composed).

The thread running through every sense is the same: something brought to rest and held there. A decision is a question settled, certainty is a doubt settled, stability is a life or a state settled. Whether the thing fixed is a plan, a fact, or a frame of mind, 定 is the moment the wavering stops and the matter stands still.

决定 juédìng Decide & Stipulate
决定 juédìng to decide; a decision
V/N 动词/名词 dòngcí/míngcí
决 jué (to resolve; to break through) + 定 dìng (to settle). To bring a choice to rest, and also the decision itself. 做决定 zuò juédìng "to make a decision." The most common everyday word for deciding.
我决定明天去。
Wǒ juédìng míngtiān qù.
I decided to go tomorrow.
这是一个重要的决定。
Zhè shì yí ge zhòngyào de juédìng.
This is an important decision.
规定 guīdìng to stipulate; a rule; a regulation
V/N 动词/名词 dòngcí/míngcí
规 guī (rule; compass) + 定 dìng (to fix). To lay down a rule in advance, and the rule so laid down. 按照规定 ànzhào guīdìng "according to the regulations." The formal, institutional cousin of 决定.
公司规定不能迟到。
Gōngsī guīdìng bù néng chídào.
The company stipulates that you cannot be late.
确定 quèdìng to confirm; to be sure; certain
V/N 动词/名词 dòngcí/míngcí
确 què (sure; firm) + 定 dìng (to settle). To pin a thing down as fixed and beyond doubt, or to be sure of it oneself. 我不确定 "I'm not sure," 确定时间 "to fix the time."
你确定吗？
Nǐ quèdìng ma?
Are you sure?
预定 yùdìng to book in advance; to reserve; to schedule
V 动词 dòngcí
预 yù (beforehand) + 定 dìng (to settle). To settle something ahead of time: a table, a room, a plan. Often written 预订 when the matter booked is goods or tickets. The 预 fixes the settling in the future.
我们预定了一个房间。
Wǒmen yùdìng le yí ge fángjiān.
We booked a room in advance.
一定 yídìng Certainty & Surely
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Two of the most common certainty words in everyday Chinese both lean on 定: 一定 yídìng and 肯定 kěndìng. Both translate as "definitely," but they point in different directions. 一定 most often expresses the speaker's resolve or expectation about what will happen: 我一定去 "I will definitely go," 明天一定下雨 "it is bound to rain tomorrow." It is forward-looking, a promise or a confident forecast. 肯定 most often expresses confidence about what is already the case: 他肯定知道 "he definitely knows," 这肯定是真的 "this is certainly true." It affirms a present judgment.

The overlap is real, and in many sentences either will pass. The fine line is that 一定 carries the flavor of will and necessity ("it must be so, I will make it so"), while 肯定 carries the flavor of affirmation ("I am sure this is so"). 肯定 can also be a verb, "to affirm, to confirm," which 一定 cannot.

一定 yídìng definitely; surely; certainly; fixed
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
一 yī (one) + 定 dìng (fixed). Settled as one and unchanging, hence "for sure." As an adverb it voices resolve or confident forecast; as an adjective before a noun it means "a certain, a fixed" amount: 一定的程度 "a certain degree."
我一定会帮你。
Wǒ yídìng huì bāng nǐ.
I will definitely help you.
辨析 biànxī · 一定 vs. 肯定 一定 leans forward, voicing resolve or a confident forecast about what will happen: 我一定来 "I will surely come." 肯定 affirms a present judgment, "I am sure it is so": 他肯定走了 "he has certainly left." 肯定 can also be a verb (to affirm); 一定 cannot.
肯定 kěndìng certainly; to affirm; positive; sure
V/ADJ 动词/形容词 dòngcí/xíngróngcí
肯 kěn (to be willing; to assent) + 定 dìng (to settle). To settle a thing as yes: as an adverb, "certainly," and as a verb, "to affirm, to confirm." Its opposite is 否定 fǒudìng (to negate; negative).
他肯定已经知道了。
Tā kěndìng yǐjīng zhīdào le.
He certainly already knows.
固定 gùdìng fixed; to fix in place; regular
V/ADJ 动词/形容词 dòngcí/xíngróngcí
固 gù (solid; firm) + 定 dìng (fixed). Held firmly in one place or pattern, not movable or variable: 固定收入 "a fixed income," 把架子固定在墙上 "to fix the shelf to the wall."
他每天有固定的作息。
Tā měitiān yǒu gùdìng de zuòxī.
He keeps a fixed daily routine.
稳定 wěndìng Stable & Settled
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The third family of 定 turns the settled thing inward and outward into steadiness. 稳定 wěndìng describes a system that holds level: prices, a job, a government, a network connection. 安定 āndìng adds the warmth of 安 (peace) and describes a settled, secure way of life: 社会安定 "a settled society," 生活安定 "a stable life." 镇定 zhèndìng turns to the person, the composure that keeps a cool head in a crisis: 保持镇定 "to stay calm." All three share the core of 定, the wavering brought to rest, but they fix it at different scales: a system, a society, a self.

稳定 wěndìng stable; steady; to stabilize
V/ADJ 动词/形容词 dòngcí/xíngróngcí
稳 wěn (steady; firm) + 定 dìng (settled). Holding level without swinging, said of prices, jobs, moods, signals, and states. 稳定的工作 "a stable job," 局势稳定 "the situation is stable."
他的工作很稳定。
Tā de gōngzuò hěn wěndìng.
His job is very stable.
安定 āndìng settled; secure; to settle (a society, a life)
V/ADJ 动词/形容词 dòngcí/xíngróngcí
安 ān (peace; safe) + 定 dìng (settled). A secure, untroubled state of life or society, with the calm of 安 added to the fixity of 定: 安定的生活 "a settled life," 安定团结 "stability and unity."
人们渴望安定的生活。
Rénmen kěwàng āndìng de shēnghuó.
People long for a settled life.
镇定 zhèndìng composed; calm; to keep one's cool
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
镇 zhèn (to press down; to keep calm) + 定 dìng (settled). The steadiness of a person under pressure, a cool head when others panic: 保持镇定 "to stay composed," 镇定自若 "perfectly calm and self-possessed."
遇到危险要保持镇定。
Yùdào wēixiǎn yào bǎochí zhèndìng.
Stay calm when you meet danger.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
一言为定 yì yán wéi dìng one word and it is settled: a deal is a deal Lit: one-word-makes-settled. Said to seal an agreement on the spot: a single word given, and the matter is fixed, with no going back. The everyday way to say "deal, it's settled."
举棋不定 jǔ qí bù dìng to hold a chess piece without setting it down: to hesitate and waver Lit: raise-chesspiece-not-settle. The image of a player holding a piece in the air, unable to commit to a move. Said of anyone caught in indecision who cannot fix on a choice.
惊魂未定 jīng hún wèi dìng the startled spirit has not yet settled: still shaken after a fright Lit: startled-soul-not-yet-settled. Describes someone whose nerves are still rattled in the aftermath of a shock or narrow escape, the heart not yet returned to rest.
心神不定 xīn shén bù dìng heart and mind unsettled: distracted and uneasy Lit: heart-spirit-not-settled. Said of a restless, distracted state in which the mind cannot come to rest, often from worry or anticipation. The opposite of 镇定.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture a roof, 宀, and beneath it a thing that has finally come to rest and stopped moving. That settling under the roof is the whole character: the wavering ends, and the matter stands still.

From that one image read every sense. Settle an open question and you have decided it (决定). Settle a doubt and you are certain (一定, 肯定). Settle a price, a job, or a society and it is stable (稳定, 安定). Settle your own racing nerves and you are composed (镇定). Whenever you meet 定, ask what has just come to rest beneath the roof, and the meaning falls into place.
