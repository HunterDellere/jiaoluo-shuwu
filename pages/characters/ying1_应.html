字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

应 yīng is the simplified form of traditional 應. The traditional 應 carries 心 (xīn, heart) at its base: the older sense is something the heart answers to, a feeling or a duty that calls for a response. From that root grow both readings, one tilting toward what the heart ought to do and one toward how it responds. The upper portion of 應 supplies the sound.

The mainland simplification 应 keeps the 广 (guǎng, shelter) frame as its outer radical and reduces the dense interior of 應 to a compact base, dropping the explicit 心. The two forms are read identically; only the script differs.

应 is a polyphone, and the reading splits the senses cleanly:

1. yīng (level tone). Ought to, should: 应该 yīnggāi (should), 应当 yīngdāng (ought to), 理应 lǐyīng (by rights ought to). This is the modal of obligation, what one is duty-bound to do.
2. yìng (falling tone). To respond, to answer, to meet: 反应 fǎnyìng (a reaction), 答应 dāying (to agree, to promise), 适应 shìyìng (to adapt), 供应 gōngyìng (to supply), 应付 yìngfu (to cope, to handle). This is the active sense of answering a call or meeting a demand.

The thread through both readings is the same: a heart that answers. When the answer is the inner pull of duty, what one should do, it is yīng; when the answer is an outward act, a reply or a response to circumstance, it is yìng.

构词 gòucí Word-Formation Patterns
构词规律 gòucí guīlǜ · One Glyph, Two Readings yīng as "ought to / should" → 应该 should · 应当 ought to · 理应 by rights ought to
yìng as "to respond / answer" → 反应 reaction · 答应 to agree · 响应 to respond to · 回应 to reply
yìng as "to meet / adapt" → 适应 to adapt · 应对 to respond to · 应聘 to apply for a post
yìng as "to handle / supply" → 应付 to cope with · 供应 to supply · 应用 to apply, application
应该 yīnggāi Ought To & Should: the yīng Reading
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

In the level tone yīng, 应 is the modal of obligation. The most common word is 应该 yīnggāi, the everyday "should" or "ought to." Its close cousin 应当 yīngdāng means the same thing but is a touch more formal, frequent in writing, rules, and contracts. The bare 应 yīng also appears alone in compounds such as 理应 lǐyīng (by rights ought to), where it is unmistakably the obligation sense.

Hear the level tone and think duty: what the heart is bound to answer to. The same glyph in the falling tone yìng turns from "what I should do" to "how I respond," which is the subject of the next two sections.

应该 yīnggāi should; ought to; be supposed to
AUX 助动词 zhùdòngcí
应 yīng (ought) + 该 gāi (should). The everyday modal of obligation and expectation. 你应该休息 (you should rest), 应该没问题 (it should be fine). The most common way to say "should" in spoken Mandarin.
你应该多喝水。
Nǐ yīnggāi duō hē shuǐ.
You should drink more water.
他现在应该到家了。
Tā xiànzài yīnggāi dào jiā le.
He should be home by now.
应当 yīngdāng ought to; should; be obliged to
AUX 助动词 zhùdòngcí
应 yīng (ought) + 当 dāng (be fitting). Near-identical in meaning to 应该 but slightly more formal, so it appears often in regulations, contracts, and written prose.
我们应当遵守规则。
Wǒmen yīngdāng zūnshǒu guīzé.
We ought to abide by the rules.
辨析 biànxī · 应该 (yīng) vs. 答应 (yìng) 应该 yīnggāi uses the level tone yīng and means "should, ought to," a statement of obligation. 答应 dāying uses the falling tone yìng and means "to agree, to promise," an act of response. Same glyph, two tones, two worlds: one is duty, the other is reply.
反应 fǎnyìng Respond & React: the yìng Reading
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · The Second Reading

Let the tone fall from level yīng to falling yìng and the character turns from "what I ought to do" to "how I answer." 反应 fǎnyìng is a reaction, the way a body, mind, or system responds to a stimulus. 答应 dāying is to answer a request with a yes, to agree or promise. 响应 xiǎngyìng is to echo or respond to a call, often a public one. 回应 huíyìng is simply to reply. The common thread: something comes in, and 应 yìng is the answer that goes back out.

反应 fǎnyìng reaction; response; to react
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
反 fǎn (back) + 应 yìng (to respond). The response that comes back when something acts on you: a chemical reaction (化学反应), a person's reaction to news, a body's reaction to medicine. Both noun and verb.
他的反应很快。
Tā de fǎnyìng hěn kuài.
His reactions are very fast.
大家对这个消息反应不一。
Dàjiā duì zhège xiāoxi fǎnyìng bù yī.
People reacted to this news in different ways.
答应 dāying to agree; to promise; to answer (a call)
V 动词 dòngcí
答 dā (to answer) + 应 yìng (to respond). To answer a request with consent: to agree or promise. Also the literal "to answer" when called: 我叫了半天他都没答应 (I called for ages and he never answered). Note the neutral tone on 应 here.
他答应帮我搬家。
Tā dāying bāng wǒ bānjiā.
He agreed to help me move house.
响应 xiǎngyìng to respond to; to answer (a call); response
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
响 xiǎng (to sound, to echo) + 应 yìng (to respond). To answer a call or appeal, often a collective one: 响应号召 (respond to a call), 积极响应 (respond actively). The image is of an echo answering a sound.
很多人响应了这个活动。
Hěn duō rén xiǎngyìng le zhège huódòng.
Many people responded to this event.
适应 shìyìng Adapt, Handle & Supply
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Meeting the Situation

The falling-tone yìng widens from "to answer a call" to "to meet a situation." 适应 shìyìng is to adapt, to fit oneself to new conditions. 应付 yìngfu is to cope with or handle, sometimes with a hint of doing only the minimum to get by. 供应 gōngyìng is to supply, to meet a demand with goods. 应用 yìngyòng is to apply, to put to practical use. In each, 应 yìng is the act of rising to meet what the world presents.

适应 shìyìng to adapt; to get used to; to suit
V 动词 dòngcí
适 shì (to suit) + 应 yìng (to respond, to meet). To adjust oneself to new circumstances: 适应环境 (adapt to the environment), 适应新工作 (get used to a new job). The response here is the slow one of fitting in over time.
我还没适应这里的天气。
Wǒ hái méi shìyìng zhèlǐ de tiānqì.
I have not yet gotten used to the weather here.
应付 yìngfu to cope with; to handle; to deal with
V 动词 dòngcí
应 yìng (to meet) + 付 fù (to hand over, to deal out). To handle a task or person, often a difficult one. Can carry a tinge of just getting through it: 应付考试 (manage to get through the exam), 应付了事 (do something perfunctorily just to be done with it).
这么多工作我应付不过来。
Zhème duō gōngzuò wǒ yìngfu bù guòlái.
I cannot cope with this much work.
供应 gōngyìng to supply; to provide; supply
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
供 gōng (to provide) + 应 yìng (to meet a need). To supply goods or services to meet a demand: 供应商 (supplier), 供应链 (supply chain), 供不应求 (supply falls short of demand). The yìng here is the answering of a need with provision.
这家公司供应新鲜蔬菜。
Zhè jiā gōngsī gōngyìng xīnxiān shūcài.
This company supplies fresh vegetables.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
应有尽有 yīng yǒu jìn yǒu to have everything one could want: complete in every way Lit: whatever should be there, all of it is there. 应有 is "what ought to exist"; 尽有 is "all of it exists." Said of a place or collection that wants for nothing. Here 应 takes the yīng reading, "ought."
得心应手 dé xīn yìng shǒu handy and skillful: to do something with effortless mastery Lit: the hand answers the heart. What the mind intends, the hand at once carries out. Praise for a skill so practiced it feels automatic. Here 应 takes the yìng reading, "to respond."
应接不暇 yìng jiē bù xiá too busy to cope: more coming in than one can attend to Lit: responding and receiving without a spare moment. 应接 is "to attend to"; 不暇 is "no leisure." Said when guests, sights, or tasks arrive faster than one can handle them. The yìng reading, "to meet."
有求必应 yǒu qiú bì yìng to grant every request: to respond to whatever is asked Lit: where there is a request, there is sure to be a response. Often said of a generous person, or of a deity believed to answer every prayer. The yìng reading, "to respond."
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold one idea at the center of 应: a heart that answers. In the traditional 應 the 心 sits at the base, and everything the character means is some way the heart answers a call. When the call is the inner pull of duty, the answer is what you ought to do, and the tone is level yīng: 应该 (should), 应当 (ought to).

Then let the tone fall to yìng and the answer turns outward, into the world. A stimulus arrives and you give a 反应, a reaction. A request arrives and you 答应, you agree. A new situation arrives and you 适应, you adapt. A demand arrives and you 供应, you supply. Hear level yīng and think duty, what you should do; hear falling yìng and think response, how you meet what comes. The tone is the switch between the two halves of the character.
