字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

于 yú is a function word, a grammatical preposition rather than a picture of a thing. Its oracle-bone and bronze forms are usually read as a drawing of breath or sound carrying outward, and from very early on the character was borrowed to do purely grammatical work. Whatever the original image, 于 in classical and modern Chinese is essentially a relation-marker: it sits before a noun and says how the verb connects to it, in space, in time, in direction, or in degree. It is filed under the radical 二 èr (two) by stroke convention, not by meaning.

There is a second glyph to know. The traditional and classical texts often write this preposition as 於 yú, a separate character that overlapped 于 in many of its uses. In the simplified script the two were largely merged, so mainland writing uses 于 across the board, while 於 survives in traditional typesetting and in a handful of fixed expressions. Treat them as one word with two written shapes.

The senses that matter to a learner all flow from one idea, "the point a thing is located or measured against":

1. At, in, on (place and time). 于 + a location or a date: 生于北京 shēng yú Běijīng (born in Beijing), 始于 shǐ yú (to begin at), 位于 wèiyú (to be located at).
2. To, toward, for, about. The thing the action is directed at or concerned with: 对于 duìyú (regarding), 关于 guānyú (about), 属于 shǔyú (to belong to).
3. Than (in comparison). Placed after an adjective to mark the standard: 大于 dàyú (greater than), 高于 gāoyú (higher than), 优于 yōuyú (superior to).
4. Bound into disyllables. Many common two-syllable words end in 于 as a fossilized tail: 由于 yóuyú (because of), 终于 zhōngyú (finally), 等于 děngyú (equal to), 至于 zhìyú (as for).

The single thread is anchoring. 于 fixes a verb or a quality to a reference point: a place, a moment, a target, or a measuring line. Once you hear it as "with respect to," every use falls into place.

生于 shēng yú At, In, On: Place and Time
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The most classical job of 于 is to plant a verb at a place or a moment. After the verb, 于 introduces the location or the time: 出生于上海 chūshēng yú Shànghǎi (born in Shanghai), 成立于去年 chénglì yú qùnián (founded last year). This is the formal, written counterpart of the spoken locative 在 zài. In speech you would say 他住在北京 (he lives in Beijing); in a biography or a formal notice you meet the tighter 生于北京, 卒于南京 (born in Beijing, died in Nanjing). The 于 frame is compact and bookish, which is exactly why it dominates formal registers.

生于 shēng yú to be born in; to be born at
V 动词 dòngcí
生 shēng (to be born) + 于 yú (at, in). The set formal frame for stating birthplace or birth date, standard in biographies and records: 生于1980年 "born in 1980," 生于江南 "born south of the river."
他生于一个普通家庭。
Tā shēng yú yí ge pǔtōng jiātíng.
He was born into an ordinary family.
位于 wèiyú to be located at; to be situated in
V 动词 dòngcí
位 wèi (position) + 于 yú (at). The standard verb for stating where a place sits, common in geography and description: 北京位于中国北部 "Beijing is located in the north of China."
这家公司位于市中心。
Zhè jiā gōngsī wèiyú shì zhōngxīn.
This company is located in the city center.
在于 zàiyú to lie in; to rest with; to consist in
V 动词 dòngcí
在 zài (to be at) + 于 yú (at). Points to where the heart of a matter resides: 问题在于 "the problem lies in," 关键在于 "the key rests with." A staple of explanatory and argumentative prose.
成功在于坚持。
Chénggōng zàiyú jiānchí.
Success lies in perseverance.
辨析 biànxī · 于 vs. 在 于 is the written, formal locative, usually glued to the verb (生于, 位于). 在 zài is its everyday spoken equivalent, standing as its own word (住在, 放在). They cover the same ground; the choice is one of register, not meaning, with 于 reserved for formal and written Chinese.
对于 duìyú To, Toward, About: Direction and Topic
对于 duìyú regarding; with regard to; as for
PREP 介词 jiècí
对 duì (toward) + 于 yú (to). Introduces the thing an opinion or attitude is directed at: 对于这个问题 "regarding this question." It heads the topic of a sentence, much like English "as for."
对于这件事，我没有意见。
Duìyú zhè jiàn shì, wǒ méiyǒu yìjiàn.
As for this matter, I have no objection.
关于 guānyú about; concerning; with respect to
PREP 介词 jiècí
关 guān (to connect) + 于 yú (to). Marks the subject matter of a book, talk, or document: 关于历史的书 "a book about history." Where 对于 frames an attitude, 关于 frames a topic.
这是一本关于中国历史的书。
Zhè shì yì běn guānyú Zhōngguó lìshǐ de shū.
This is a book about Chinese history.
辨析 biànxī · 对于 vs. 关于 对于 points at the target of an attitude or response ("regarding X, I think..."). 关于 names the topic or scope of something ("a film about X"). When in doubt: 对于 takes a stance toward something, 关于 simply names what something is on.
属于 shǔyú to belong to; to be part of; to fall under
V 动词 dòngcí
属 shǔ (to be subordinate to) + 于 yú (to). States membership or ownership: 这本书属于我 "this book belongs to me," 鲸鱼属于哺乳动物 "whales belong to the mammals."
这片土地属于国家。
Zhè piàn tǔdì shǔyú guójiā.
This land belongs to the state.
大于 dàyú Than & the Bound Frames
语法洞见 yǔfǎ dòngjiàn · Grammatical Insight

Placed after an adjective, 于 marks the standard a thing is measured against, giving a tidy "greater than" frame: 大于 dàyú (greater than), 高于 gāoyú (higher than), 多于 duōyú (more than), 优于 yōuyú (better than). 三大于二 "three is greater than two" is how a Chinese maths text reads the symbol >. This is the formal cousin of the spoken 比 bǐ comparison (三比二大); 于 packs the same relation into a single bound word.

The other place a beginner constantly meets 于 is as the frozen tail of a common disyllable, where it no longer carries a separable meaning of its own. 由于 yóuyú (because of) opens a reason; 终于 zhōngyú (finally, at last) caps a long wait; 等于 děngyú (to equal) states an equation; 至于 zhìyú (as for) shifts to a new topic. You do not parse the 于 in these, you learn each as a unit. Recognizing that the same little particle is hiding inside all of them is what makes the list feel like one family rather than four unrelated words.

大于 dàyú to be greater than; larger than
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
大 dà (big) + 于 yú (than). The comparison frame "greater than," standard in maths and formal description: 收入大于支出 "income exceeds expenditure." Its mirror is 小于 xiǎoyú (less than).
五大于三。
Wǔ dàyú sān.
Five is greater than three.
由于 yóuyú because of; owing to; due to
CONJ 连词 liáncí
由 yóu (from) + 于 yú. A bound conjunction opening a cause, more formal than 因为 yīnwèi: 由于天气不好 "owing to the bad weather." It typically leads the clause and pairs with a result.
由于下雨，比赛取消了。
Yóuyú xià yǔ, bǐsài qǔxiāo le.
Because of the rain, the match was cancelled.
终于 zhōngyú finally; at last; in the end
ADV 副词 fùcí
终 zhōng (end) + 于 yú. A bound adverb marking the arrival of a long-awaited outcome: 我终于明白了 "I finally understood." It carries relief or fulfilment, and so attaches to results that were wished for or worked toward.
努力了三年，他终于成功了。
Nǔlì le sān nián, tā zhōngyú chénggōng le.
After three years of effort, he finally succeeded.
等于 děngyú to equal; to be equivalent to; to amount to
V 动词 dòngcí
等 děng (equal) + 于 yú. States equality, literal or figurative: 二加二等于四 "two plus two equals four," 不说话等于同意 "saying nothing amounts to agreeing."
一公里等于一千米。
Yì gōnglǐ děngyú yìqiān mǐ.
One kilometer equals one thousand meters.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
无济于事 wú jì yú shì of no help to the matter: useless, achieving nothing Lit: no-help-to-the-matter. Here 于 means "to, with respect to," so the phrase is literally "brings no benefit to the situation." Said of an effort or remedy that fails to change anything.
青出于蓝 qīng chū yú lán the blue comes from the indigo plant: the student surpasses the master Lit: blue-comes-from-indigo. From Xunzi: the dye 青 is drawn out of the 蓝 plant yet ends up bluer than its source. 于 marks the origin, "comes out of," and the saying praises a pupil who outgrows the teacher.
喜形于色 xǐ xíng yú sè joy takes shape on the face: visibly delighted Lit: joy-shows-on-the-complexion. 于 marks the place the feeling surfaces, "on the face." Said of someone whose happiness shows plainly in their expression, unable to hide it.
死于非命 sǐ yú fēi mìng to die other than by one's allotted fate: to meet an unnatural death Lit: die-by-not-fate. 于 marks the cause of death, and 非命 (not one's natural span) signals an accident, violence, or disaster rather than a peaceful end.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hear 于 as a single English phrase: "with respect to." It never travels alone; it always points at a reference point sitting just after it. Born with respect to a place is 生于; concerned with respect to a topic is 关于; greater with respect to a number is 大于; arriving with respect to the end is 终于.

So whenever 于 appears, look immediately to its right and ask what reference point it is anchoring the verb to: a location, a moment, a target, a measuring line, or the end of a long wait. Name that anchor and the meaning of 于 is already in hand. And remember its written twin 於, the same word wearing a fuller coat in traditional and classical texts.
