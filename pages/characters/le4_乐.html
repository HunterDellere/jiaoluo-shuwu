字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

The traditional 樂 is a picture of a musical instrument. The early forms show silk threads (the two 幺 elements) strung across a wooden stand (the 木 at the base), the image of a frame instrument like a zither or a rack of drums. The original meaning is therefore music, read yuè. The simplified 乐 collapses that whole structure into five quick strokes, but the two characters are one and the same word-family, simply written differently: 乐 is the mainland simplification of 樂.

From "music" the character grew a second reading. Because music gives delight, 樂 / 乐 came to also mean joy, happiness, and that sense took the reading lè. So one written sign carries two spoken words:

1. yuè · music. The original sense, the strung instrument: 音乐 yīnyuè (music), 乐器 yuèqì (musical instrument), 乐曲 yuèqǔ (a musical composition).
2. lè · happy, joyful. The derived sense, the gladness music brings: 快乐 kuàilè (happy), 欢乐 huānlè (joyous), 乐观 lèguān (optimistic), 乐意 lèyì (willing, glad to).

The link between them is intuitive: what is music for, if not joy? Read the character as yuè when it names sound and instruments, and as lè when it names the feeling. The thread of delight runs through both, the instrument on one side and the smile it draws on the other.

快乐 kuàilè Joy & Happiness (read lè)
快乐 kuàilè happy; joyful
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
快 kuài (quick; pleased) + 乐 lè (joy). The everyday word for happy, and the standard ingredient in greetings: 生日快乐 "happy birthday," 新年快乐 "happy new year."
祝你生日快乐！
Zhù nǐ shēngrì kuàilè!
Happy birthday to you!
孩子们玩得很快乐。
Háizimen wán de hěn kuàilè.
The children played very happily.
辨析 biànxī · 快乐 vs. 高兴 快乐 is the steadier, more lasting happiness, the kind you wish someone for a birthday or a whole year. 高兴 gāoxìng is the in-the-moment gladness over a specific thing: 见到你很高兴 "glad to see you." You can be 高兴 for a minute; you hope to be 快乐 for a lifetime.
欢乐 huānlè joyous; merry; festive joy
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
欢 huān (merry; happy) + 乐 lè (joy). A warmer, more public joy than 快乐, the shared gladness of a celebration or a crowd: 欢乐的气氛 "a joyous atmosphere."
节日里到处都是欢乐的气氛。
Jiérì lǐ dàochù dōu shì huānlè de qìfēn.
During the festival there is a joyous atmosphere everywhere.
乐观 lèguān optimistic; hopeful
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
乐 lè (joy) + 观 guān (to view; outlook). To take a bright view of things, to expect good outcomes. Its opposite is 悲观 bēiguān (pessimistic).
他对未来很乐观。
Tā duì wèilái hěn lèguān.
He is very optimistic about the future.
乐意 lèyì Willingness & Delight (read lè)
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The lè reading does more than name a mood. It also marks a glad willingness, the disposition of someone who takes pleasure in doing a thing rather than merely agreeing to it. 乐意 lèyì is "to be glad to," 乐于 lèyú is "to take pleasure in" some activity, and 乐趣 lèqù is the delight a pursuit gives. The common thread is that the doing itself is a source of joy, not a chore.

This is why 乐 sits at the heart of phrases about generosity and contentment. To help others 乐 is to find the helping itself pleasant; to be 乐 in one's lot is to be at peace with it. The reading lè turns joy from a passive feeling into an active leaning toward what one enjoys.

乐意 lèyì to be glad to; willing
V 动词 dòngcí
乐 lè (glad) + 意 yì (intention; will). To be happy to do something, with the nuance of genuine willingness: 我很乐意帮忙 "I'd be glad to help."
我很乐意帮你。
Wǒ hěn lèyì bāng nǐ.
I am very glad to help you.
乐趣 lèqù delight; pleasure; the fun of a thing
N 名词 míngcí
乐 lè (joy) + 趣 qù (interest; fun). The pleasure or enjoyment found in an activity: 读书的乐趣 "the pleasure of reading."
他从工作中找到了乐趣。
Tā cóng gōngzuò zhōng zhǎodào le lèqù.
He found delight in his work.
娱乐 yúlè entertainment; recreation; to amuse oneself
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
娱 yú (to amuse) + 乐 lè (joy). Entertainment or recreation, both the activities and the having of fun: 娱乐活动 "recreational activities," 娱乐圈 "the entertainment world."
周末我们需要一些娱乐。
Zhōumò wǒmen xūyào yìxiē yúlè.
On weekends we need a little entertainment.
音乐 yīnyuè Music: the Reading yuè
读音洞见 dúyīn dòngjiàn · Reading Insight

When 乐 names sound rather than feeling, it is read yuè, the original reading that recalls the strung instrument of the ancient glyph. The anchor word is 音乐 yīnyuè (music), where 音 yīn supplies "sound, tone" and 乐 yuè supplies the organized art of it. From there the yuè reading runs through the whole vocabulary of music: 乐器 yuèqì (instrument), 乐曲 yuèqǔ (composition), 乐队 yuèduì (band, orchestra), 乐谱 yuèpǔ (sheet music).

The rule of thumb is straightforward. If the word is about sound, instruments, or compositions, the character is yuè. If it is about gladness, willingness, or fun, it is lè. The same five strokes, two spoken words, told apart by what they point at: the music, or the joy it makes.

音乐 yīnyuè music
N 名词 míngcí
音 yīn (sound; tone) + 乐 yuè (music). The general word for music as an art: 古典音乐 "classical music," 听音乐 "to listen to music." Note the yuè reading, not lè.
我喜欢听音乐。
Wǒ xǐhuan tīng yīnyuè.
I like listening to music.
乐器 yuèqì musical instrument
N 名词 míngcí
乐 yuè (music) + 器 qì (implement; vessel). Any musical instrument: 中国乐器 "Chinese instruments," 弹乐器 "to play an instrument." The word echoes the strung frame of the original glyph.
你会弹什么乐器？
Nǐ huì tán shénme yuèqì?
What instrument can you play?
乐曲 yuèqǔ a musical composition; a piece of music
N 名词 míngcí
乐 yuè (music) + 曲 qǔ (tune; melody). A composed piece of music: 一首乐曲 "a piece of music." The 曲 names the melody, the 乐 the musical art it belongs to.
这首乐曲很优美。
Zhè shǒu yuèqǔ hěn yōuměi.
This piece of music is very beautiful.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
乐不思蜀 lè bù sī shǔ so happy as to forget Shu: too content to think of home Lit: happy-not-miss-Shu. From the deposed ruler of the kingdom of Shu, who, kept in comfort by his conquerors, said he no longer missed his homeland. Now said of anyone so absorbed in present pleasure that they forget where they came from.
助人为乐 zhù rén wéi lè to take joy in helping others: to find pleasure in kindness Lit: help-others-as-joy. A praise for the person who genuinely enjoys lending a hand, treating the helping itself as a source of happiness rather than a duty.
自得其乐 zì dé qí lè to find one's own pleasure: content in one's own way Lit: self-obtain-its-joy. Said of someone happily absorbed in a quiet pursuit of their own, needing no audience or approval, perfectly content alone.
安居乐业 ān jū lè yè to live in peace and work with contentment: a settled, contented life Lit: peaceful-dwelling-joyful-work. The classic image of a flourishing society in which people have stable homes and take satisfaction in their work, a standing political ideal.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold the traditional 樂 in your mind first: silk threads strung across a wooden stand, an instrument waiting to be played. That picture is the original meaning, music, read yuè. Now play it, and watch the joy rise off the strings. That joy is the second meaning, happiness, read lè.

The reading tells you which face you are looking at. Sound, instruments, and compositions take yuè: 音乐, 乐器, 乐曲. Gladness, willingness, and fun take lè: 快乐, 乐意, 乐趣. One instrument, two gifts. Ask whether the word means the music or the smile, and you will know whether to say yuè or lè.
