字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

情 qíng is a pictophonetic compound: 忄 (the heart radical) carries the meaning, and 青 qīng supplies the sound. The left element 忄 is the squeezed, side-standing form of 心 xīn (heart), the same radical that marks 怕 pà (fear), 忙 máng (busy), and a whole field of inner states. On the right, 青 qīng (the blue-green of growing things) lends its pronunciation and nudges qīng toward qíng. Together they name what stirs inside: the movements of the heart.

From that core image the character spreads into three senses that anchor a great many words:

1. Feeling, emotion, mood. The general inner state: 感情 gǎnqíng (feelings; affection), 心情 xīnqíng (mood), 情绪 qíngxù (emotions; emotional state), 热情 rèqíng (warmth; enthusiasm).
2. Affection, love. Feeling directed at another person: 爱情 àiqíng (romantic love), 友情 yǒuqíng (friendship), 亲情 qīnqíng (family affection), 情人 qíngrén (lover; sweetheart).
3. Situation, circumstance. The shape of how things stand: 情况 qíngkuàng (situation), 情形 qíngxíng (state of affairs), 事情 shìqíng (matter; affair), 情节 qíngjié (plot; circumstances of a case).

The thread running through all three is the heart facing the world. Turned inward it is mood and feeling; turned toward a person it is affection; turned toward events it is the felt shape of a situation. Chinese keeps these close together on purpose: a 情况 is not a cold set of facts but the way matters actually stand, the circumstances as the heart reads them.

感情 gǎnqíng Feeling & Mood
感情 gǎnqíng feelings; affection; emotional bond
N 名词 míngcí
感 gǎn (to feel) + 情 qíng (feeling). The broad word for emotional feeling, and especially the affection that builds up between people: 他们感情很好 "they have a close bond." Also "to develop feelings": 日久生情 "feelings grow with time."
他们之间很有感情。
Tāmen zhījiān hěn yǒu gǎnqíng.
There is real affection between them.
她对这座城市有很深的感情。
Tā duì zhè zuò chéngshì yǒu hěn shēn de gǎnqíng.
She has a deep attachment to this city.
心情 xīnqíng mood; state of mind; frame of mind
N 名词 míngcí
心 xīn (heart) + 情 qíng (feeling). The mood you are in at a given time: 心情好 "in a good mood," 心情不好 "in a bad mood." A passing state of the heart rather than a settled trait.
今天天气好，我的心情也很好。
Jīntiān tiānqì hǎo, wǒ de xīnqíng yě hěn hǎo.
The weather is nice today, so my mood is good too.
辨析 biànxī · 心情 vs. 情绪 心情 is your overall mood, the general weather of how you feel: good, bad, relaxed, heavy. 情绪 qíngxù points more at the raw emotional state and its swings, often the agitated or negative side: 闹情绪 "to sulk; to throw a fit," 情绪不稳定 "emotionally unstable." You can be in a fine 心情 while 情绪 names the specific feeling moving through you.
情绪 qíngxù emotion; emotional state; mood (often the volatile side)
N 名词 míngcí
情 qíng (feeling) + 绪 xù (threads; strands). The tangled threads of feeling, an emotional state that can run high or low: 控制情绪 "to control one's emotions," 闹情绪 "to be in a mood; to sulk."
他努力控制自己的情绪。
Tā nǔlì kòngzhì zìjǐ de qíngxù.
He tried hard to control his emotions.
热情 rèqíng enthusiasm; warmth; warmly hospitable
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
热 rè (hot) + 情 qíng (feeling). Warm feeling, used both as a noun (enthusiasm, passion) and as an adjective for a warm, welcoming person: 他很热情 "he is very warm and friendly."
主人很热情地招待我们。
Zhǔrén hěn rèqíng de zhāodài wǒmen.
The host received us very warmly.
爱情 àiqíng Affection & Love
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

When 情 turns toward another person it becomes the language of bonds. Chinese sorts these bonds by who they are with. 爱情 àiqíng is romantic love, the love between partners. 友情 yǒuqíng is the feeling between friends, friendship. 亲情 qīnqíng is the tie of blood, the affection within a family. Each pairs 情 with a different first character, and together they map the heart's attachments.

This is why 情 alone can stand for romance: 情人 qíngrén is a lover or sweetheart, 情书 qíngshū is a love letter, and 情人节 Qíngrénjié is the name for Valentine's Day. The single glyph carries enough of the heart that, in the right company, it reads as love itself.

爱情 àiqíng romantic love; love (between partners)
N 名词 míngcí
爱 ài (to love) + 情 qíng (feeling). Specifically romantic love, the bond between lovers, distinct from 爱 ài alone, which covers love of any kind. 爱情故事 "a love story."
这是一个美丽的爱情故事。
Zhè shì yí ge měilì de àiqíng gùshì.
This is a beautiful love story.
友情 yǒuqíng friendship; the feeling between friends
N 名词 míngcí
友 yǒu (friend) + 情 qíng (feeling). The bond of friendship. Close to 友谊 yǒuyì in meaning, but 友情 leans on the felt warmth, while 友谊 leans on the formal relationship.
他们的友情持续了几十年。
Tāmen de yǒuqíng chíxù le jǐshí nián.
Their friendship lasted for decades.
亲情 qīnqíng family affection; the bond of kinship
N 名词 míngcí
亲 qīn (kin; close) + 情 qíng (feeling). The affection that runs between family members, the love of parent and child or among siblings. It completes the trio with 爱情 (love) and 友情 (friendship).
血浓于水，亲情最珍贵。
Xuè nóng yú shuǐ, qīnqíng zuì zhēnguì.
Blood is thicker than water; family affection is most precious.
情况 qíngkuàng Situation & Circumstance
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The third great sense of 情 steps away from private feeling toward the wider shape of events. Here 情 means the situation, the circumstances, the way things stand. 情况 qíngkuàng is the everyday word for "situation" or "circumstances": 什么情况 "what is going on," 情况紧急 "the situation is urgent." Its close neighbor 情形 qíngxíng describes the state of affairs as a scene one observes.

This is also where 情 meets the very common word 事情 shìqíng, a matter or affair, where 事 shì (matter) and 情 sit side by side: 有件事情 "there is a matter (to discuss)." The link is worth holding onto: in Chinese a "matter" is not a bare event but a thing with a felt shape, which is exactly what 情 contributes.

情况 qíngkuàng situation; circumstances; condition
N 名词 míngcí
情 qíng (circumstance) + 况 kuàng (condition). The standard word for a situation or set of circumstances: 了解情况 "to understand the situation," 紧急情况 "an emergency."
请你介绍一下这里的情况。
Qǐng nǐ jièshào yíxià zhèlǐ de qíngkuàng.
Please give us a rundown of the situation here.
情形 qíngxíng state of affairs; situation; how things stand
N 名词 míngcí
情 qíng (circumstance) + 形 xíng (shape; form). The shape a situation takes, often as something witnessed or described: 当时的情形 "the way things were at the time." Close to 情况 but with a touch more of the observed scene.
他向我描述了当时的情形。
Tā xiàng wǒ miáoshù le dāngshí de qíngxíng.
He described to me how things were at the time.
事情 shìqíng matter; affair; thing (to be dealt with)
N 名词 míngcí
事 shì (matter) + 情 qíng (circumstance). The everyday word for a matter or affair, something that happens or needs handling: 一件事情 "a matter," 出事情了 "something has happened."
我有一件重要的事情要告诉你。
Wǒ yǒu yí jiàn zhòngyào de shìqíng yào gàosu nǐ.
I have an important matter to tell you about.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
情不自禁 qíng bù zì jīn unable to restrain one's feelings: cannot help oneself Lit: feeling-not-self-restrain. When emotion wells up too strongly to hold back: 她情不自禁地哭了 "she could not help crying." 禁 here is jīn, "to bear; to restrain."
一见钟情 yí jiàn zhōng qíng to fall in love at first sight Lit: one-look-concentrate-feeling. 钟 zhōng here means to gather or focus one's feeling on someone. The standard phrase for instant romantic attraction on a first meeting.
通情达理 tōng qíng dá lǐ to understand feelings and reason: sensible and reasonable Lit: grasp-feeling-reach-reason. Said of a person who weighs both human feeling (情) and good sense (理), and so deals with others fairly and with understanding.
手足之情 shǒu zú zhī qíng the affection of hands and feet: the bond between brothers and sisters Lit: hand-foot's-feeling. 手足 (hands and feet) is a classical image for siblings, who are as inseparable as one's own limbs. The phrase names the deep tie between brothers and sisters.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Put your finger on the heart radical 忄 at the left of 情 and feel it as a heart standing on its side, leaning toward the world. The phonetic 青 qīng, the fresh blue-green of growing things, gives the sound and a hint of something alive and stirring. 情 is the heart in motion.

Then watch which way the heart turns. Turned inward, it is mood and feeling (心情, 情绪, 感情). Turned toward a person, it is affection and love (爱情, 友情, 亲情). Turned toward events, it is the felt shape of a situation (情况, 事情). Whenever you meet 情, ask where the heart is pointing, and the sense will follow.
