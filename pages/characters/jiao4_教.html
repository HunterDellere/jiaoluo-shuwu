字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

教 jiào pictures a lesson: a hand with a rod beside a child. The right element is 攵 (pū), the radical that began as a hand 又 holding a stick or staff, the sign of striking, rapping, or directing. The left side carries the child: the oracle-bone and bronze forms show 子 (zǐ, child) with the marks of the counting-tablet above it, the same elements seen in 孝 (xiào, filial). Put together, the scene is an elder guiding a child through a lesson, rod in hand, the original image of disciplined instruction.

From that one scene the character spreads across a wide field of meaning:

1. To teach, to instruct (jiào). The formal reading, the root of the whole vocabulary of schooling: 教育 jiàoyù (education), 教师 jiàoshī (teacher), 教室 jiàoshì (classroom), 教训 jiàoxun (a lesson, often a hard one).
2. To teach a skill (jiāo). The colloquial reading, used for the everyday act of showing someone how: 教书 jiāoshū (to teach, as a profession), 教中文 jiāo Zhōngwén (to teach Chinese).
3. Teaching, doctrine, religion (jiào). A body of teaching handed down: 宗教 zōngjiào (religion), 佛教 Fójiào (Buddhism), 基督教 Jīdūjiào (Christianity).

The thread running through all of it is transmission: knowledge, a craft, or a faith passed from one who holds it to one who does not. Note the two readings. As a verb of the bare act of teaching it is often jiāo; as a noun for instruction, education, or religion it is jiào.

教育 jiàoyù Teach & Educate
教育 jiàoyù education; to educate
N/V 名词/动词 míngcí/dòngcí
教 jiào (to teach) + 育 yù (to raise; to nurture). The whole enterprise of teaching and bringing up, as both a noun and a verb: 高等教育 "higher education," 教育孩子 "to bring up a child." This is the formal jiào reading.
教育很重要。
Jiàoyù hěn zhòngyào.
Education is very important.
父母要好好教育孩子。
Fùmǔ yào hǎohǎo jiàoyù háizi.
Parents should bring up their children well.
教师 jiàoshī teacher (formal); educator
N 名词 míngcí
教 jiào (to teach) + 师 shī (master; expert). The formal word for a teacher as a profession, common in writing and titles. In direct address the warmer 老师 lǎoshī is used instead.
她是一名中学教师。
Tā shì yì míng zhōngxué jiàoshī.
She is a secondary-school teacher.
辨析 biànxī · 教师 vs. 老师 教师 names the occupation: it appears on forms, in statistics, and in formal prose. 老师 is how you address or refer to a teacher in daily life, and it extends as a respectful term to many skilled people. You would say 王老师 "Teacher Wang," never 王教师.
教室 jiàoshì classroom
N 名词 míngcí
教 jiào (to teach) + 室 shì (room). The room where teaching happens. Watch the tone of 室 (shì), and do not confuse it with 教师 jiàoshī (teacher); the two words differ only in the second syllable.
学生们在教室里上课。
Xuéshengmen zài jiàoshì lǐ shàngkè.
The students are having class in the classroom.
教训 jiàoxun a lesson (learned the hard way); to reprimand
N/V 名词/动词 míngcí/dòngcí
教 jiào (to teach) + 训 xùn (to admonish). As a noun, the lesson drawn from a mistake or setback: 吸取教训 "to learn the lesson." As a verb, to scold or take someone to task.
这次失败给了我一个教训。
Zhè cì shībài gěi le wǒ yí ge jiàoxun.
This failure taught me a lesson.
教书 jiāoshū The jiāo Reading: To Teach a Skill
语音洞见 yǔyīn dòngjiàn · Reading Insight

教 is a polyphone, a character with two readings, and learners meet both early. The split is not random: it tracks how the word behaves. When 教 is the plain verb "to teach," the bare act of showing someone how to do something, it usually takes the first-tone reading jiāo: 我教你 wǒ jiāo nǐ "I'll teach you," 他教数学 tā jiāo shùxué "he teaches math," 教书 jiāoshū "to teach for a living."

When 教 is a noun, or part of a compound naming instruction, education, or religion, it takes the fourth-tone reading jiào: 教育 jiàoyù (education), 教师 jiàoshī (teacher), 宗教 zōngjiào (religion). A handy rule of thumb: if you can put an object after it and mean simply "teach X," reach for jiāo; if it sits inside a settled noun, reach for jiào.

教书 jiāoshū to teach (as a profession); to be a teacher
V 动词 dòngcí
教 jiāo (to teach) + 书 shū (books). Literally "to teach books," the standard way to say one teaches for a living. Note the first-tone jiāo here, the colloquial verb reading.
他在大学教书。
Tā zài dàxué jiāoshū.
He teaches at a university.
教 jiāo to teach (someone something)
V 动词 dòngcí
The bare verb, first tone, taking both a person and a thing: 教我中文 "teach me Chinese." This is the everyday "show me how" sense, distinct from the institutional jiào of 教育.
请你教我写汉字。
Qǐng nǐ jiāo wǒ xiě hànzì.
Please teach me to write Chinese characters.
妈妈教我做菜。
Māma jiāo wǒ zuò cài.
My mom is teaching me to cook.
请教 qǐngjiào to consult; to ask for advice (politely)
V 动词 dòngcí
请 qǐng (to request) + 教 jiào (instruction). To respectfully ask someone to teach you, the polite way to seek advice from a senior or an expert: 向您请教 "may I ask your guidance." Here 教 returns to the fourth tone.
我想向您请教一个问题。
Wǒ xiǎng xiàng nín qǐngjiào yí ge wèntí.
I would like to ask your advice on a question.
宗教 zōngjiào Religion: the Teaching as Faith
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Chinese names religion as 宗教 zōngjiào, literally "ancestral teaching" or "lineage doctrine." The logic is the same thread that runs through the whole character: a religion is a body of teaching handed down. So the name of nearly every faith ends in 教: 佛教 Fójiào (Buddhism, the "Buddha teaching"), 道教 Dàojiào (Daoism), 基督教 Jīdūjiào (Christianity, the "Christ teaching"), 伊斯兰教 Yīsīlánjiào (Islam), 儒教 Rújiào (the Confucian teaching).

The same 教 builds the words for religious places and roles: 教堂 jiàotáng (a church), 教会 jiàohuì (a church as a body of believers), 主教 zhǔjiào (a bishop, "chief teacher"). Throughout, 教 is read jiào and means the teaching itself, the doctrine that a community gathers around and passes to the next generation.

宗教 zōngjiào religion
N 名词 míngcí
宗 zōng (ancestor; lineage) + 教 jiào (teaching). The general word for religion, the handed-down teaching that a community is built around: 宗教信仰 "religious belief."
他对东方宗教很感兴趣。
Tā duì dōngfāng zōngjiào hěn gǎn xìngqù.
He is very interested in Eastern religions.
佛教 Fójiào Buddhism
N 名词 míngcí
佛 Fó (Buddha) + 教 jiào (teaching). The "Buddha teaching." A model for how faiths are named in Chinese: the founder or central figure plus 教.
佛教起源于古印度。
Fójiào qǐyuán yú gǔ Yìndù.
Buddhism originated in ancient India.
教堂 jiàotáng church (the building)
N 名词 míngcí
教 jiào (religion) + 堂 táng (hall). The hall of worship, a church building. The community of believers, by contrast, is 教会 jiàohuì.
这座教堂很有名。
Zhè zuò jiàotáng hěn yǒumíng.
This church is very famous.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
因材施教 yīn cái shī jiào to teach according to aptitude: tailor instruction to each learner Lit: based-on-talent-apply-teaching. A principle traced to Confucius, who answered the same question differently for different students. The enduring ideal of the teacher who meets each pupil where they are rather than drilling all alike.
言传身教 yán chuán shēn jiào teaching by word and by example: instruct in speech and in conduct alike Lit: words-transmit-body-teach. To teach not only by what you say but by how you live, so that your own conduct is the lesson. Praise for a teacher or parent whose example matches their words.
教学相长 jiào xué xiāng zhǎng teaching and learning grow each other: the teacher learns by teaching Lit: teach-learn-mutually-grow. From the Liji (Record of Rites). Teaching exposes what the teacher does not yet understand, so instructing others deepens one's own knowledge; the two advance together.
不教而诛 bú jiào ér zhū to punish without having taught: to penalize people never instructed in the rule Lit: not-teach-yet-punish. From the Analects, a criticism of the ruler who punishes failings the people were never taught to avoid. To hold someone to a standard you never showed them.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture the old scene inside 教: a child on the left, and on the right a hand holding a rod, 攵, raised to direct the lesson. Hold that image of an elder guiding a child, and every sense of the character follows from it. The act itself is to teach (教书, 教中文). Gather it into an institution and it is education (教育, 教师, 教室). Hand it down as a doctrine a whole community lives by and it is religion (宗教, 佛教).

The two readings sort by job. When 教 is the bare verb with an object, "teach me this," it is usually first tone, jiāo. When it locks into a noun for instruction or faith, it is fourth tone, jiào. Ask whether you are doing the teaching or naming the teaching, and the tone follows.
