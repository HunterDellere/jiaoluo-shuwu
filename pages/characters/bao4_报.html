字源zìyuánEtymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

报 decomposes into the hand radical 扌 on the left and, on the right, the seal-script element 卩 with a 又 hand tucked beneath it (the IDS structure is ⿰扌⿸卩又). The picture is a scene of judgment: 卩 is a person bent at the knees, a kneeling figure, and the two hands seize that figure and hold it down. The original glyph staged an arrest and a sentence being passed.

Shuōwén places 报 under the 㚔 group and glosses it as 当罪人也, to pass judgment on an offender. The seal form shows the kneeling criminal pinned by a hand from behind while another hand presses the verdict home. The earliest sense is therefore not news at all but a legal ruling delivered, an authority answering a crime with its due.

From that courtroom act the meaning fanned outward along one logic: a 报 is a response that comes back. A verdict answers a crime, so 报 came to mean to repay, to requite, whether the requital is revenge or gratitude. To answer a question with information gave to report, to announce; the thing announced, set in print and answered to the public daily, gave 报纸 (bàozhǐ), the newspaper.

Japanese keeps the character as 報 (the unsimplified form) reading it hō in 情報 (jōhō, information) and 報告 (hōkoku, a report), preserving the same arc from judgment to message. The mainland simplification trimmed the seal-script 㚔 element on the left to the cleaner 扌, which is why the modern glyph reads so plainly as a hand.

新闻xīnwénThe Press & Printed News
报纸bàozhǐnewspaper
N 名词 míngcí
报 (the thing announced) plus 纸 (zhǐ, paper): the daily report rendered on paper. The everyday word for a printed newspaper, and the head noun behind titles like 《人民日报》.
爷爷每天早上看报纸。
Yéye měitiān zǎoshang kàn bàozhǐ.
Grandpa reads the newspaper every morning.
日报rìbàodaily paper
N 名词 míngcí
日 (rì, day) plus 报: a report issued every day. The suffix that names mastheads, as in 《人民日报》 (Rénmín Rìbào, the People's Daily) and 《广州日报》.
这条新闻登在今天的日报上。
Zhè tiáo xīnwén dēng zài jīntiān de rìbào shàng.
This piece of news ran in today's daily paper.
海报hǎibàoposter
N 名词 míngcí
海 (hǎi, sea) plus 报: a public announcement spread far and wide. The word for a poster or playbill, the kind pasted on a wall to advertise a film or a show.
墙上贴着一张电影海报。
Qiáng shàng tiē zhe yì zhāng diànyǐng hǎibào.
A film poster is pasted on the wall.
陈述chénshùTo Report & Register
报告bàogàoto report; a report
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
报 (to announce) plus 告 (gào, to tell): to deliver an account formally, and the document that delivers it. Works as both the act of reporting and the written or spoken report itself.
他向经理报告了这件事。
Tā xiàng jīnglǐ bàogào le zhè jiàn shì.
He reported this matter to the manager.
请大家看这份调查报告。
Qǐng dàjiā kàn zhè fèn diàochá bàogào.
Please everyone look at this survey report.
报名bàomíngto sign up, register
V 动词 dòngcí
报 (to report) plus 名 (míng, name): to report your name onto a list. The standard verb for signing up or enrolling for a class, a contest, or an exam.
我想报名参加这次比赛。
Wǒ xiǎng bàomíng cānjiā zhè cì bǐsài.
I want to sign up to take part in this competition.
辨析 biànxī · 报名 vs. 报到 报名 (bàomíng) is registering in advance to join; 报到 (bàodào) is checking in once you arrive, reporting for duty on the first day.
报道bàodàoto report (news); coverage
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
报 (to announce) plus 道 (dào, to state): to report an event through the media, and the news coverage produced. The journalist's verb, distinct from 报告 in that 报道 is broadcast to the public.
电视台报道了这场比赛。
Diànshìtái bàodào le zhè chǎng bǐsài.
The TV station covered this match.
回还huíhuánRequital: Payback & Reward
语义脉络 yǔyì màiluò · Semantic Thread

Here the courtroom origin surfaces most plainly. A verdict is the state answering a crime, so 报 holds the sense of a return that matches what was given. Aim it at a kindness and it means to repay (回报); aim it at a wrong and it means to avenge (报仇). Both ride the same moral arithmetic: every act calls back its like.

回报huíbàoto repay; a return, reward
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
回 (huí, to return) plus 报 (to requite): to give back in kind. Used for repaying kindness and, in finance, for the return on an investment.
他用努力工作来回报父母。
Tā yòng nǔlì gōngzuò lái huíbào fùmǔ.
He repays his parents by working hard.
这笔投资的回报很高。
Zhè bǐ tóuzī de huíbào hěn gāo.
The return on this investment is very high.
报答bàodáto repay (a kindness)
V 动词 dòngcí
报 (to requite) plus 答 (dá, to answer): to answer a kindness with thanks made real. Reserved for repaying gratitude, a warmer word than the neutral 回报.
我一定会报答你的恩情。
Wǒ yídìng huì bàodá nǐ de ēnqíng.
I will surely repay your kindness.
报仇bàochóuto avenge, take revenge
V 动词 dòngcí
报 (to requite) plus 仇 (chóu, enmity): to pay back a wrong. The dark twin of 报答, turning the same logic of return toward vengeance.
他发誓要为父亲报仇。
Tā fāshì yào wèi fùqīn bàochóu.
He swore to avenge his father.
成语chéngyǔIdioms & Set Phrases
恩将仇报ēn jiāng chóu bàoto repay kindness with enmity: to bite the hand that feeds you恩 (kindness) is met with 仇 (enmity) as its 报 (return). A sharp condemnation of ingratitude, common in everyday speech to call out a betrayer.
报仇雪恨bào chóu xuě hènavenge a wrong and wipe out a grudge报仇 (take revenge) paired with 雪恨 (wash away the hatred). The standard four-character phrase for settling a blood debt, frequent in martial-arts fiction and historical drama.
投桃报李tóu táo bào lǐthrow a peach, repay with a plum: return one good turn with anotherFrom the Shījīng (Book of Songs): 投我以桃，报之以李. The image of trading fruit stands for reciprocal courtesy between friends.
以怨报德yǐ yuàn bào déto repay virtue with resentmentAn inversion of the Confucian ideal: 德 (virtue, kindness) is answered with 怨 (resentment). The phrase appears in the Analects' discussion of how to respond to good and to harm.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture a courtroom from the seal-script era. A figure kneels, knees folded to the stone floor, and a bailiff's hand 扌 clamps the shoulder while a second hand 又 presses the warrant down from behind. The judge speaks the verdict aloud. That spoken answer, the state's reply to a crime, is the whole character: a hand and a sentence delivered back.

Now let the verdict echo outward. The same hand that delivered judgment can deliver any answer that comes back. It hands you the morning 报纸 (bàozhǐ), the day's events answered in print. It stamps your name onto a list when you 报名 (bàomíng). It returns a kindness when you 回报 (huíbào), or a wound when you 报仇 (bàochóu). Every time you meet 报, hear the courtroom verdict behind it: something given calls back its answer.
