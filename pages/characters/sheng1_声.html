字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

声 shēng began as a picture of a sound being made and heard. The full traditional form is 聲, which gathers three things: 殸 (a hanging chime-stone struck by a hand holding a mallet), 耳 (ěr, an ear) below it, and the sound that travels between them. The chime is sounded, the ear receives it, and the character names exactly that event: a sound produced and perceived. It is the classic way Chinese builds an abstract idea, by drawing the concrete scene that contains it.

The simplified 声 keeps only the upper-left corner of 殸, dropping both the ear and the striking hand. What survives looks like 士 (shì) over a few strokes, which is why the modern dictionary radical is 士. The radical here is a matter of stroke shape, not meaning: nothing about a scholar lives inside the word for sound. When you meet 声, read the whole traditional 聲 behind it, the struck chime and the listening ear, and the modern senses fall into place.

Three senses branch from the struck chime:

1. Sound, voice, noise. The literal hearing: 声音 shēngyīn (sound), 大声 dàshēng (loud), 小声 xiǎoshēng (quiet, softly), 笑声 xiàoshēng (the sound of laughter).
2. Tone of the voice. The pitch a syllable is spoken on: 声调 shēngdiào (tone), 四声 sìshēng (the four tones of Mandarin), 第一声 dì-yī shēng (the first tone).
3. To make known, reputation. What is sounded out to the world: 声明 shēngmíng (to declare; a statement), 名声 míngshēng (reputation), 声誉 shēngyù (prestige).

The thread is the sound that leaves a source and reaches others. Whether it is a chime, a laugh, the pitch of a word, or a name carried on the air, 声 is something sounded out and received.

声音 shēngyīn Sound & Voice
声音 shēngyīn sound; voice
N 名词 míngcí
声 shēng (sound) + 音 yīn (tone; note). The everyday word for any sound the ear picks up, and also for a person's voice. The two-syllable word is the natural noun; bare 声 mostly lives inside compounds.
我听到了奇怪的声音。
Wǒ tīngdào le qíguài de shēngyīn.
I heard a strange sound.
她的声音很好听。
Tā de shēngyīn hěn hǎotīng.
Her voice is very pleasant.
辨析 biànxī · 声音 vs. 音 声音 is the ordinary noun for a sound or voice you can hear. Bare 音 yīn is more technical and bound, leaning toward a tone, a note, or the abstract phonetic value: 音乐 yīnyuè (music), 发音 fāyīn (pronunciation). You hear a 声音; you analyze an 音.
大声 dàshēng loud; loudly; in a loud voice
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
大 dà (big) + 声 shēng (voice). A big voice, used adverbially: 大声说话 "to speak loudly," 大声叫 "to shout." Its pair is 小声 xiǎoshēng, softly.
请大声一点，我听不清。
Qǐng dàshēng yīdiǎn, wǒ tīng bù qīng.
Please speak up a little, I can't hear clearly.
小声 xiǎoshēng quiet; softly; in a low voice
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
小 xiǎo (small) + 声 shēng (voice). A small voice, the opposite of 大声: 小声说 "to speak quietly," often so others do not overhear.
他小声地告诉我一个秘密。
Tā xiǎoshēng de gàosu wǒ yī ge mìmì.
He quietly told me a secret.
声调 shēngdiào The Tones: Pitch of Speech
语音洞见 yǔyīn dòngjiàn · Phonological Insight

Mandarin is a tonal language: the pitch contour a syllable is spoken on is part of the word, not decoration. 声 is the character that names this. 声调 shēngdiào is "tone," literally the sound's pitch-shape, and 四声 sìshēng is the set of four: 第一声 high and level, 第二声 rising, 第三声 dipping then rising, 第四声 falling. A fifth, the toneless 轻声 qīngshēng (neutral tone), is short and unstressed. Change the tone and you change the word: 妈 mā (mother), 麻 má (hemp), 马 mǎ (horse), 骂 mà (to scold).

声 also names the consonant that opens a syllable. 声母 shēngmǔ is the "initial," the onset consonant such as the b in bā, paired with the 韵母 yùnmǔ, the "final" that carries the vowel and tone. So 声 sits at the heart of how a Chinese syllable is described: an initial 声母, a final, and a tone 声调 riding over the whole.

声调 shēngdiào tone (of a syllable); intonation
N 名词 míngcí
声 shēng (sound) + 调 diào (pitch; tune). The pitch contour that distinguishes one Mandarin syllable from another. Getting the 声调 wrong can turn one word into a completely different one.
汉语的声调很重要。
Hànyǔ de shēngdiào hěn zhòngyào.
Tones are very important in Chinese.
四声 sìshēng the four tones (of Mandarin)
N 名词 míngcí
四 sì (four) + 声 shēng (tone). The four-way tone system of standard Mandarin: high-level, rising, dipping, and falling. The neutral tone 轻声 is counted separately.
普通话有四声。
Pǔtōnghuà yǒu sìshēng.
Standard Mandarin has four tones.
声母 shēngmǔ initial consonant (of a syllable)
N 名词 míngcí
声 shēng (sound) + 母 mǔ (mother; base). The consonant that opens a syllable, such as the m in mā. Paired with the 韵母 yùnmǔ, the final that carries the vowel and tone.
这个字的声母是 b。
Zhège zì de shēngmǔ shì b.
The initial of this character is b.
名声 míngshēng Reputation & Declaration
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

A sound that travels far enough becomes a name. From the literal "sound" 声 extends to mean what is sounded out about a person or a position: a declaration, and the reputation that lingers after it. 声明 shēngmíng is to state something openly, to put it on record; the same two characters as a noun mean an official statement. 名声 míngshēng is the reputation a person carries, the sound of their name in others' mouths, which can be good 好名声 or bad 坏名声.

The wider family keeps this sense of a voice raised in public. 声誉 shēngyù is prestige or standing; 呼声 hūshēng is a public call or clamor for something; 声援 shēngyuán is to back a cause with one's voice, to give vocal support. In every case 声 is no longer the bare sound but the sound aimed outward, meant to be heard and to leave a mark.

名声 míngshēng reputation; repute; name (one has in the world)
N 名词 míngcí
名 míng (name) + 声 shēng (sound). The sound of one's name as it travels among others: the reputation you hold. Can be qualified as 好名声 (good name) or 坏名声 (bad name).
这家餐厅名声很好。
Zhè jiā cāntīng míngshēng hěn hǎo.
This restaurant has a very good reputation.
声明 shēngmíng to declare; to state publicly; a statement
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
声 shēng (to sound out) + 明 míng (to make clear). To state something openly and on the record, or the formal statement itself: 发表声明 "to issue a statement."
公司发表了一份声明。
Gōngsī fābiǎo le yī fèn shēngmíng.
The company issued a statement.
声誉 shēngyù prestige; reputation; standing
N 名词 míngcí
声 shēng (name; repute) + 誉 yù (praise; honor). A more formal, weightier word than 名声, leaning toward esteem and public standing: 国际声誉 "international prestige."
这所大学享有很高的声誉。
Zhè suǒ dàxué xiǎngyǒu hěn gāo de shēngyù.
This university enjoys a high reputation.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
异口同声 yì kǒu tóng shēng different mouths, the same voice: to say in unison, to agree unanimously Lit: different-mouths-same-sound. Many people voicing the very same thing at once. Used when a group answers or insists with one voice, leaving no doubt of their agreement.
声东击西 shēng dōng jī xī make a noise in the east, strike in the west: a feint to mislead the enemy Lit: sound-east-strike-west. A classic stratagem: make a show of attacking one place to draw attention there, then hit somewhere else entirely. The 声 is the deliberate noise raised as a decoy.
鸦雀无声 yā què wú shēng not a crow or sparrow makes a sound: utterly silent, dead quiet Lit: crow-sparrow-no-sound. So still that not even the noisiest birds are heard. Said of a hushed room or crowd, often one frozen by tension or rapt attention.
绘声绘色 huì shēng huì sè painting both sound and color: vivid and lifelike (of a description) Lit: paint-sound-paint-color. Of telling or describing something so vividly that the listener seems to hear and see it. High praise for a gripping, animated account.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture the full traditional 聲: a hand lifts a mallet and strikes a hanging chime-stone, and just below sits an ear, catching the ring. That single scene, a sound made and a sound heard, is the whole character. The simplified 声 is only the top corner of that chime left standing, with the ear and the mallet worn away, so when you see it, restore the chime and the ear in your mind.

From that ring, follow the sound outward. Close up it is a plain noise or voice (声音, 大声). Shaped into pitch it becomes the tone of a word (声调, 四声). Carried far enough on the air it becomes a name in the world, a declaration or a reputation (声明, 名声). Whenever you meet 声, ask how far the sound is traveling, and you will know which sense is ringing.
