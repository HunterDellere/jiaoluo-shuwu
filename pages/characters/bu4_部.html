字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

部 bù is a pictophonetic compound: the right-side element 阝 (the city-mound radical, written 邑 yì when free-standing) carries the meaning, marking a settlement or an administrative division of territory, while the left-side element supplies the sound. When 阝 sits on the right of a character it almost always points to a place, a town, or a region under one authority; the same radical appears in 都 dū (capital city), 郡 jùn (commandery), and 邦 bāng (state). So at root 部 is a piece of land set under a single command, a district.

From that founding image of a marked-off division the modern senses fan out:

1. Part, section. A portion of a larger whole: 部分 bùfen (a part), 全部 quánbù (the whole of, every part), 局部 júbù (a local part).
2. Ministry, department, unit. A branch of an organization or army: 部门 bùmén (department), 部队 bùduì (military unit), 教育部 jiàoyùbù (the Ministry of Education).
3. Measure word. The classifier counted out for self-contained machines and works: 一部电影 yí bù diànyǐng (a film), 一部手机 yí bù shǒujī (a phone), 一部小说 yí bù xiǎoshuō (a novel).

The thread through every sense is division into bounded units. Whether the unit is a slice of a report, a wing of a government, a column of soldiers, or a single complete film, 部 frames it as one demarcated piece, the way the old city-mound framed one parcel of governed land.

部分 bùfen Part & Whole
部分 bùfen part; section; portion
N 名词 míngcí
部 bù (section) + 分 fen (a share). A part of something larger, the everyday word for a portion: 大部分 dàbùfen "the greater part, most of," 一部分 yí bùfen "a portion." Note the second syllable goes neutral.
大部分学生都来了。
Dàbùfen xuésheng dōu lái le.
Most of the students came.
这只是问题的一部分。
Zhè zhǐshì wèntí de yí bùfen.
This is only part of the problem.
全部 quánbù the whole; all of it; the entirety
N 名词 míngcí
全 quán (complete; whole) + 部 bù (parts). Every part taken together, the total: 全部完成 "all finished," 全部家当 "one's entire belongings." It is the natural counterweight to 部分.
这是我全部的钱。
Zhè shì wǒ quánbù de qián.
This is all the money I have.
辨析 biànxī · 部分 vs. 全部 部分 names a piece of a thing, leaving the rest unmentioned: 一部分人同意 "some of the people agree." 全部 names the thing without remainder, every piece together: 全部人都同意 "all of the people agree." They sit at opposite ends of the same scale, the slice against the whole.
内部 nèibù internal; the interior; inside (of an organization)
N 名词 míngcí
内 nèi (inside) + 部 bù (section). The inner part of a body, machine, or group, often the confidential inside of an organization: 内部消息 "inside information," 内部会议 "an internal meeting." Its pair is 外部 wàibù (external).
这是公司内部的事。
Zhè shì gōngsī nèibù de shì.
This is an internal company matter.
部门 bùmén Departments & Units
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The administrative root of 部 surfaces most plainly when it names a branch of an organization or army. A 部门 bùmén is a department, one demarcated wing of a company or institution. Scaled up to the level of the state, 部 is the formal word for a government ministry: 教育部 jiàoyùbù (the Ministry of Education), 外交部 wàijiāobù (the Ministry of Foreign Affairs), 财政部 cáizhèngbù (the Ministry of Finance). The pattern is fixed: a domain plus 部 gives the ministry that governs it.

The same logic runs through the military. A 部队 bùduì is a unit or formation of troops, a body of soldiers under one command, and 部 marks the column or detachment as a bounded fighting body. A senior officer commands his 部下 bùxià, the subordinates under him. Across civilian and military uses alike, 部 keeps its oldest sense: a piece of a larger whole, set apart and placed under a single authority.

部门 bùmén department; division; branch
N 名词 míngcí
部 bù (section) + 门 mén (gate; category). One demarcated wing of a company or institution: 销售部门 "the sales department," 政府部门 "government departments." The standard word for an organizational unit.
他在哪个部门工作？
Tā zài nǎge bùmén gōngzuò?
Which department does he work in?
部队 bùduì military unit; troops; armed forces
N 名词 míngcí
部 bù (unit) + 队 duì (team; formation). A body of troops under one command, from a single unit up to the armed forces in general: 参军入部队 "to join the army." Soldiers say 在部队 "in the service."
他的哥哥在部队当兵。
Tā de gēge zài bùduì dāngbīng.
His older brother serves in the army.
部长 bùzhǎng minister; head of a department
N 名词 míngcí
部 bù (ministry) + 长 zhǎng (chief; head). The head of a ministry or department: 外交部长 "the Foreign Minister." The 长 here is the leader-sense, read zhǎng, not the cháng of "long."
教育部长发表了讲话。
Jiàoyù bùzhǎng fābiǎo le jiǎnghuà.
The Minister of Education gave a speech.
量词 liàngcí The Measure Word: Films & Devices
语法洞见 yǔfǎ dòngjiàn · Grammatical Insight

Besides its noun life, 部 serves as a measure word, the classifier slotted between a number and certain nouns. It counts two families of things: large self-contained works and substantial machines. For works, 部 is the classifier for films, novels, dictionaries, and long compositions: 一部电影 yí bù diànyǐng (a film), 一部小说 yí bù xiǎoshuō (a novel), 一部词典 yí bù cídiǎn (a dictionary). For machines, it counts phones, cars, and pieces of equipment treated as complete units: 一部手机 yí bù shǒujī (a phone), 一部机器 yí bù jīqì (a machine).

The logic mirrors the noun. A 部-counted thing is a bounded whole made of many parts, a film of many scenes, a novel of many chapters, a machine of many components, complete in itself. For a single short film clip or a casual phone you will also hear the all-purpose 个 gè, but 部 is the formal, fitting classifier when the work or device is regarded as one finished, self-contained piece.

一部电影 yí bù diànyǐng a film; one movie
N 名词 míngcí
一 yī (one) + 部 bù (measure word) + 电影 diànyǐng (film). The standard way to count a film, treating it as a single complete work. The same 部 counts a 小说 (novel) or a 电视剧 (TV series).
我昨天看了一部电影。
Wǒ zuótiān kàn le yí bù diànyǐng.
I watched a film yesterday.
一部手机 yí bù shǒujī a mobile phone; one cellphone
N 名词 míngcí
一 yī (one) + 部 bù (measure word) + 手机 shǒujī (phone). 部 counts a phone as a self-contained device. You will also hear 一个手机 in casual speech, but 一部手机 is the proper classifier.
他买了一部新手机。
Tā mǎi le yí bù xīn shǒujī.
He bought a new phone.
一部小说 yí bù xiǎoshuō a novel; one work of fiction
N 名词 míngcí
一 yī (one) + 部 bù (measure word) + 小说 xiǎoshuō (novel). 部 counts a full-length novel as one finished work. For a short story you would more likely use 篇 piān, the classifier for shorter writings.
这是一部很有名的小说。
Zhè shì yí bù hěn yǒumíng de xiǎoshuō.
This is a very famous novel.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
按部就班 àn bù jiù bān to follow the proper sequence: to do things step by step in due order Lit: according-to-section, settle-into-rank. Originally a writer's image of arranging material into its right divisions and lines; now it means to proceed methodically by the established order, neither rushing nor skipping steps. Here 部 keeps its old sense of an ordered division.
部署周密 bù shǔ zhōu mì deployed with careful thoroughness: arranged in close and complete order 部署 bùshǔ is to lay out forces or tasks in their assigned positions, the military sense of 部; 周密 zhōumì means thorough and tightly planned. Said of a plan or operation set up with every detail in place.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture the old city-mound on the right of 部, one parcel of land fenced off and placed under a single command. That bounded division is the whole character. Everything 部 means is a piece set apart as one unit.

Slice a thing and you have its 部分, a part; gather every slice and you have the 全部, the whole. Set apart a wing of an office and you have a 部门; set apart a body of troops and you have a 部队. And when you count out 一部电影 or 一部手机, you are tallying complete, self-contained units, a film whole in itself, a device whole in itself. Whenever you meet 部, look for the bounded unit, the parcel marked off from the rest.
