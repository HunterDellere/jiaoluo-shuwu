字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

理 lǐ is a pictophonetic compound: the jade radical 王/玉 carries the meaning, and the right-hand element 里 lǐ carries the sound. The left side is 王, the standing form of 玉 yù (jade), the same element that runs down the side of 玛 and 珠. Its meaning here is the natural veining inside a piece of jade, the streaks and grain that a carver reads and follows when deciding where to cut. So the earliest sense of 理 is concrete: the texture or grain of a stone, the lines already there to be worked along.

From "the grain you cut along" the character climbs steadily into the abstract:

1. Texture, grain. The literal veins of a material: 纹理 wénlǐ (texture; grain), 肌理 jīlǐ (the texture of flesh or a surface).
2. Principle, reason, the inner pattern. The grain extended to the order running through any thing or argument: 道理 dàoli (sense; the way of a thing), 理由 lǐyóu (a reason), 真理 zhēnlǐ (truth).
3. To put in order, to manage. To work along the grain rather than against it, hence to tidy and to govern: 管理 guǎnlǐ (to manage), 整理 zhěnglǐ (to sort; to tidy), 处理 chǔlǐ (to handle).
4. To heed, to attend to. Often in the negative: 不理 bù lǐ (to ignore; to pay no mind).

In later Chinese thought 理 became one of the great philosophical words. The Neo-Confucian thinkers raised it to mean cosmic principle, the rational pattern underlying all things, paired against 气 qì, the matter and energy that principle organizes. To "investigate the principle of things" (穷理 qióng lǐ) was the scholar's path to understanding. The thread from jade to cosmos holds throughout: 理 is the order already in a thing, the grain you find rather than the shape you impose.

道理 dàoli Reason & Principle
道理 dàoli sense; reason; the way a thing works; principle
N 名词 míngcí
道 dào (way) + 理 li (principle). The sense or logic behind something, the principle that makes it hold together. 有道理 "that makes sense," 讲道理 "to be reasonable, to argue by reason rather than force."
你说的很有道理。
Nǐ shuō de hěn yǒu dàoli.
What you said makes a lot of sense.
我们应该讲道理。
Wǒmen yīnggāi jiǎng dàoli.
We should be reasonable.
理由 lǐyóu a reason; grounds; justification
N 名词 míngcí
理 lǐ (principle) + 由 yóu (cause; from). The specific reason or grounds given for an action or claim: 没有理由 "there is no reason," 充分的理由 "ample justification."
他没有理由生气。
Tā méiyǒu lǐyóu shēngqì.
He has no reason to be angry.
辨析 biànxī · 道理 vs. 理由 道理 is the general sense or principle that makes something true or right: it lives in the matter itself ("that argument has 道理"). 理由 is a particular reason a person offers to justify a choice ("give me one 理由 to stay"). You can have plenty of 理由 with no 道理 at all, that is, excuses without real sense.
合理 hélǐ reasonable; rational; fair
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
合 hé (to fit; to accord with) + 理 lǐ (principle). Fitting the principle, in accord with reason: 合理的价格 "a reasonable price," 合理安排 "a sensible arrangement." Its opposite is 不合理.
这个价格很合理。
Zhège jiàgé hěn hélǐ.
This price is very reasonable.
管理 guǎnlǐ Manage & Handle
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The same 理 that names a thing's inner grain also names the act of working with that grain. To 理 something is to bring it into order along its natural lines, hence the whole family of verbs of managing, sorting, and handling. 整理 zhěnglǐ is to tidy and arrange, putting scattered things back into their places. 处理 chǔlǐ is to deal with or dispose of a matter, to handle a problem to its end. 管理 guǎnlǐ is to administer and run, the word behind every "manager" and "management" in modern Chinese. Even a haircut is 理发 lǐfà, literally to put the hair in order.

The image underneath is the carver again: you do not force the jade, you read its veins and cut along them. Good management, in the logic of the word, is less about imposing a shape than about finding the order already latent in things and following it.

管理 guǎnlǐ to manage; to administer; management
V/N 动词/名词
管 guǎn (to govern; to be in charge of) + 理 lǐ (to put in order). To run and oversee an organization, system, or resource: 管理公司 "to manage a company," 时间管理 "time management."
她管理着一个很大的团队。
Tā guǎnlǐ zhe yī ge hěn dà de tuánduì.
She manages a very large team.
处理 chǔlǐ to handle; to deal with; to process
V 动词 dòngcí
处 chǔ (to manage; to dispose of) + 理 lǐ (to put in order). To take a matter in hand and see it through: 处理问题 "to deal with a problem," 处理数据 "to process data."
这件事我会处理。
Zhè jiàn shì wǒ huì chǔlǐ.
I will handle this matter.
整理 zhěnglǐ to tidy up; to sort; to put in order
V 动词 dòngcí
整 zhěng (whole; to straighten) + 理 lǐ (to put in order). To arrange scattered things back into order: 整理房间 "to tidy the room," 整理笔记 "to organize one's notes."
他正在整理自己的房间。
Tā zhèngzài zhěnglǐ zìjǐ de fángjiān.
He is tidying up his room.
理论 lǐlùn Theory & Truth
理论 lǐlùn theory; theoretical principle
N 名词 míngcí
理 lǐ (principle) + 论 lùn (discourse; argument). A reasoned, systematic account of how something works, set against 实践 shíjiàn (practice): 科学理论 "scientific theory," 理论上 "in theory."
理论和实践要结合。
Lǐlùn hé shíjiàn yào jiéhé.
Theory and practice must be combined.
真理 zhēnlǐ truth; the verified principle
N 名词 míngcí
真 zhēn (true; real) + 理 lǐ (principle). Truth as the principle that genuinely holds, not mere opinion: 追求真理 "to pursue truth," 真理只有一个 "there is only one truth."
科学家一生追求真理。
Kēxuéjiā yīshēng zhuīqiú zhēnlǐ.
Scientists pursue truth all their lives.
物理 wùlǐ physics; the principles of matter
N 名词 míngcí
物 wù (thing; matter) + 理 lǐ (principle). Literally "the principles of things," the modern name for physics. A clear case of 理 meaning the lawful pattern running through the physical world.
他物理学得很好。
Tā wùlǐ xué de hěn hǎo.
He is very good at physics.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
理所当然 lǐ suǒ dāng rán only natural: just as principle dictates it should be Lit: by-principle-it-ought-thus-to-be. Said of something taken as a matter of course, the obvious and proper outcome that reason demands. 把帮助别人看作理所当然 "to see helping others as only natural."
理直气壮 lǐ zhí qì zhuàng bold with right on your side: speaking firmly because the principle is clearly yours Lit: principle-straight-spirit-strong. When the 理 is on your side, your 气 (spirit, breath) is strong, so you can speak up with full confidence and no guilt.
通情达理 tōng qíng dá lǐ reasonable and understanding: alive to feeling and clear on principle Lit: penetrate-feeling-reach-principle. Praise for a sensible, fair-minded person who weighs both human feeling (情) and reason (理), neither coldly rigid nor blindly sentimental.
据理力争 jù lǐ lì zhēng to argue strongly on principle: to stand on reason and contend hard for it Lit: rely-on-principle-strongly-contend. To take one's stand on what is right and fight for it in argument, not backing down so long as reason is on your side.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold a piece of jade up to the light and look at the veins running through it. Those lines are 理, the grain. The radical 王 on the left is the jade itself, and the carver's whole art is to read that grain and cut along it, never against it.

Now let the image widen. Follow the grain in a stone and you get texture (纹理). Follow the grain in an argument and you get its sense and reason (道理). Follow the grain in a tangled affair and you set it in order (整理, 处理, 管理). Follow the grain of the whole world and you reach the cosmic principle the philosophers called 理. Wherever you meet 理, look for the pattern already there, the line you are meant to find and follow rather than force.
