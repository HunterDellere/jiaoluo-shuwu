字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

性 xìng is a pictophonetic compound: the heart radical 忄 carries the meaning, while 生 (shēng) supplies the sound. The 忄 on the left is the upright form of 心 (heart, mind), and it marks the character as something that lives in the inner self. The phonetic 生, meaning to be born, to grow, lends its pronunciation; yet here the two halves rhyme in sense as well, for 性 is precisely what one is born with in the heart. The simplified and traditional forms are identical, eight strokes unchanged across the script.

The core idea is innate nature: the disposition a thing carries from birth, what it cannot help being. From that single root the senses spread along four lines:

1. Nature, character, disposition. The temperament of a person: 性格 xìnggé (character, personality), 个性 gèxìng (individuality), 本性 běnxìng (innate nature), 性情 xìngqíng (temperament).
2. Sex, gender. The born category of male or female: 性别 xìngbié (sex, gender), 男性 nánxìng (male), 女性 nǚxìng (female), 异性 yìxìng (the opposite sex).
3. Property, quality. The inherent character of a thing: 性能 xìngnéng (performance, properties), 弹性 tánxìng (elasticity), 毒性 dúxìng (toxicity).
4. The abstract -ness / -ity suffix. Attached to a word, 性 turns it into the quality of being so: 可能性 kěnéngxìng (possibility), 重要性 zhòngyàoxìng (importance), 安全性 ānquánxìng (safety, safeness).

The thread is what lies inborn. A person's temperament is the nature of their heart; the male or female category is the nature of the body; the elasticity or toxicity of a substance is the nature of the material; and as a suffix, 性 simply names the abstract nature, the -ness, of whatever it follows.

性格 xìnggé Nature & Character
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The oldest and richest face of 性 is the inner disposition of a person. 性格 xìnggé is the everyday word for character or personality, the settled set of traits by which we know someone. 个性 gèxìng narrows to what makes a person distinct from everyone else, their individuality. 本性 běnxìng reaches deeper still, to the nature one is born with and rarely sheds. 性情 xìngqíng colors the whole with feeling, the temperament as it shows in mood and reaction. Together they map the heart from its deepest root to its outward grain.

性格 xìnggé character; personality; disposition
N 名词 míngcí
性 xìng (nature) + 格 gé (standard, frame). The settled set of traits that make up who a person is: 性格开朗 "an open, cheerful character," 性格内向 "an introverted personality." The standard word for someone's overall character, often the answer to "what kind of person are they."
他的性格很开朗。
Tā de xìnggé hěn kāilǎng.
He has a very cheerful character.
他们俩性格完全不同。
Tāmen liǎ xìnggé wánquán bùtóng.
The two of them have completely different personalities.
辨析 biànxī · 性格 vs. 个性 性格 xìnggé is a person's whole character, the full set of traits, neutral and describable for anyone. 个性 gèxìng stresses what sets a person apart, their distinctiveness: a person with strong 个性 stands out from the crowd, while everyone has some 性格. The first asks "what is their character," the second "what makes them their own."
个性 gèxìng individuality; individual character; distinctiveness
N 名词 míngcí
个 gè (individual) + 性 xìng (nature). The traits that distinguish one person from another, the stamp of the individual: 有个性 "to have a strong personality, to be distinctive," 个性化 "personalized, customized." Often praised as a mark of a person who is not interchangeable with the crowd.
这个年轻人很有个性。
Zhège niánqīngrén hěn yǒu gèxìng.
This young person has a strong, distinctive personality.
她喜欢有个性的设计。
Tā xǐhuan yǒu gèxìng de shèjì.
She likes designs with individual character.
本性 běnxìng innate nature; one's true nature
N 名词 míngcí
本 běn (root, origin) + 性 xìng (nature). The nature one is born with, deep and hard to change: 本性善良 "good-natured at the core." Often invoked when surface behavior gives way to what a person really is, as in 本性难移, that one's nature is hard to alter.
他的本性其实很善良。
Tā de běnxìng qíshí hěn shànliáng.
His true nature is actually very kind.
性情 xìngqíng temperament; disposition; nature and feeling
N 名词 míngcí
性 xìng (nature) + 情 qíng (feeling). Temperament considered through the lens of mood and emotional reaction: 性情温和 "of a gentle temperament," 性情大变 "a sharp change in temperament." Warmer and more feeling-laden than the neutral 性格, closer to the heart's weather than its frame.
老人性情温和，很受邻居喜爱。
Lǎorén xìngqíng wēnhé, hěn shòu línjū xǐ'ài.
The old man has a gentle temperament and is much loved by his neighbors.
性别 xìngbié Sex & Gender
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Because sex is a category one is born into, 性 also names it. 性别 xìngbié is the neutral term for sex or gender, the box on a form, the category itself. From it the language builds the everyday pair 男性 nánxìng (male) and 女性 nǚxìng (female), more formal than 男人 and 女人 and common in writing about people in the aggregate. 异性 yìxìng names the opposite sex, the counterpart category. Here 性 has narrowed from the whole inner nature to one fixed dimension of it, the dimension of male and female.

性别 xìngbié sex; gender
N 名词 míngcí
性 xìng (sex) + 别 bié (distinction, category). The category of male or female: 性别平等 "gender equality," the 性别 field on an application form. The standard, neutral word in both everyday and formal contexts for the sex or gender of a person.
请在表格上填写您的性别。
Qǐng zài biǎogé shang tiánxiě nín de xìngbié.
Please fill in your gender on the form.
公司提倡性别平等。
Gōngsī tíchàng xìngbié píngděng.
The company promotes gender equality.
女性 nǚxìng female; women (as a category)
N 名词 míngcí
女 nǚ (woman) + 性 xìng (sex). The female sex, women considered as a category or class: 职业女性 "professional women," 女性用品 "products for women." More formal and collective than 女人, the way one refers to women in writing, statistics, and public discourse.
越来越多的女性进入科技行业。
Yuè lái yuè duō de nǚxìng jìnrù kējì hángyè.
More and more women are entering the tech industry.
构词 gòucí · The X + 性 sex pattern A sex-marking word before 性 names the category itself: 男性 nánxìng (male), 女性 nǚxìng (female), 异性 yìxìng (the opposite sex), 同性 tóngxìng (the same sex). These forms are more formal and collective than 男人 / 女人 and are the standard register for writing, forms, and public discussion.
男性 nánxìng male; men (as a category)
N 名词 míngcí
男 nán (man) + 性 xìng (sex). The male sex, men considered as a category: 成年男性 "adult males," 男性朋友 "a male friend." The formal counterpart to 女性, used wherever one speaks of men as a group rather than addressing a particular man.
这种病在男性中更常见。
Zhè zhǒng bìng zài nánxìng zhōng gèng chángjiàn.
This illness is more common among men.
性能 xìngnéng Property & the -ity Suffix
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Extend "nature" from people to things and 性 names the inherent property of a material or system: 性能 xìngnéng (the performance or capabilities of a machine), 弹性 tánxìng (elasticity, the quality of springing back), 毒性 dúxìng (toxicity). Push the same logic one step further and 性 becomes a pure suffix, the workhorse that turns an adjective or verb into an abstract noun, the equivalent of English -ness and -ity: 可能性 kěnéngxìng (possibility), 重要性 zhòngyàoxìng (importance), 安全性 ānquánxìng (safety). This is one of the most productive word-building tools in modern Chinese, the standard way to name a quality in the abstract.

性能 xìngnéng performance; capabilities; properties (of a machine)
N 名词 míngcí
性 xìng (property) + 能 néng (capability). The functional properties of a machine, device, or material: 性能稳定 "stable performance," 高性能 "high-performance." How well a thing does what it is built to do, the practical measure of its inherent quality.
这台电脑的性能很强。
Zhè tái diànnǎo de xìngnéng hěn qiáng.
This computer has very strong performance.
弹性 tánxìng elasticity; flexibility; springiness
N 名词 míngcí
弹 tán (to spring, to bounce) + 性 xìng (property). The quality of stretching and springing back, literal in materials and figurative in arrangements: 弹性材料 "an elastic material," 弹性工作时间 "flexible working hours." Names a built-in capacity to flex without breaking.
这种材料很有弹性。
Zhè zhǒng cáiliào hěn yǒu tánxìng.
This material is very elastic.
可能性 kěnéngxìng possibility; likelihood
N 名词 míngcí
可能 kěnéng (possible) + 性 xìng (-ness). The abstract noun "possibility," built by attaching the -ity suffix to the adjective 可能: 有可能性 "there is a possibility," 很大的可能性 "a strong likelihood." A model case of how 性 turns a quality into a countable, discussable thing.
这件事成功的可能性很大。
Zhè jiàn shì chénggōng de kěnéngxìng hěn dà.
There is a strong possibility that this will succeed.
构词 gòucí · The X + 性 abstract-noun pattern Attach 性 to an adjective or verb and you get the quality of being so, English -ness or -ity: 重要性 importance (from 重要 important), 安全性 safety (from 安全 safe), 积极性 enthusiasm, drive (from 积极 active), 可靠性 reliability (from 可靠 reliable). The pattern is enormously productive: once you know a quality word, you can usually name its abstraction by adding 性.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
江山易改，本性难移 jiāng shān yì gǎi, běn xìng nán yí rivers and mountains are easier to move than ones nature: a persons deep character rarely changes Literally "rivers and mountains may change, but innate nature is hard to shift." A folk maxim contrasting the upheaval of whole landscapes with the stubbornness of human character: even great outward change leaves a person's deep disposition largely as it was. Often shortened to 本性难移 alone.
率性而为 shuài xìng ér wéi to act following ones nature: to do as one's disposition prompts Literally "to follow nature and act." To behave according to one's true temperament rather than calculation or constraint. It can praise an honest, unaffected spirit or, in a cooler reading, describe someone who simply acts on impulse without weighing the consequences.
性命攸关 xìng mìng yōu guān a matter of life and death: bearing directly on survival Literally "bearing on life itself." Here 性命 means life, the vital nature that animates the body. The phrase marks something as gravely serious, a matter on which survival hangs, as in 性命攸关的决定, a life-or-death decision.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold one picture at the center of 性: a heart (忄) that was born (生) a certain way. That is innate nature, what a thing carries from birth and cannot easily shed, and it is the seed of every sense.

Turn the heart toward a person and you get character: 性格 personality, 个性 individuality, 本性 the deep true self, 性情 temperament. Narrow that born nature to one fixed dimension, male or female, and you get sex and gender: 性别, 男性, 女性, 异性. Carry "nature" over to things and you get inherent property: 性能 performance, 弹性 elasticity, 毒性 toxicity. Finally, strip the word down to a pure tool and 性 becomes the -ness suffix that names any quality in the abstract: 可能性 possibility, 重要性 importance, 安全性 safety. Whenever you meet 性, ask whose nature this is, the heart's, the body's, the material's, or simply the abstract nature of some quality.
