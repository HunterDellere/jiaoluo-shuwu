字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

社 shè is a meaning compound: the altar-and-spirit radical 礻 (the squeezed form of 示, the sacrificial altar) stands beside 土 tǔ, earth. The picture is an altar raised to the god of the soil. The 礻 marks the character as something sacred and ritual, while 土 names exactly which spirit is being honoured, the power of the ground itself. The simplified and traditional forms are identical, seven strokes that have stayed stable through the script's history.

In the oldest sense 社 is that soil-god and the shrine where it was served. Every settlement had its own altar of the soil, and the people of a place were bound together by the worship they shared there. From that root, the word's whole modern reach unfolds, because a 社 was never just a god but the community gathered around it.

Three faces grow from this core of a people gathered around a shared altar:

1. Society, community. The human collective considered as a whole or as a local circle: 社会 shèhuì (society), 社区 shèqū (community, neighbourhood), 社交 shèjiāo (social interaction).
2. An association, an organization, an agency. A formally gathered body of people: 社团 shètuán (a club or society), 报社 bàoshè (a newspaper office), 通讯社 tōngxùnshè (a news agency), 旅行社 lǚxíngshè (a travel agency), 出版社 chūbǎnshè (a publishing house).
3. The god of the soil, the shrine. The original ritual sense, surviving chiefly in 社稷 shèjì, the altars of soil and grain that once stood for the whole state, and in the village 土地 land-and-soil cult.

The thread is the gathering. Honour a soil-god and a community forms around its altar; widen that community and you have society; formalise a slice of it for a shared purpose and you have an association; and behind it all stands the old shrine where the binding first took place.

社会 shèhuì Society & Community
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The broadest face of 社 is the human collective itself. 社会 shèhuì is society at its widest: the whole web of people and institutions in which a life is lived. Narrow the lens to where people actually live side by side and you reach 社区 shèqū, the local community or neighbourhood. Turn from the structure to the act of mixing within it and you get 社交 shèjiāo, the business of meeting and dealing with other people. The three move from the vast to the near to the personal, but all rest on the same idea of people bound together.

社会 shèhuì society; the social world
N 名词 míngcí
社 shè (the gathered community) + 会 huì (to assemble, an assembly). The whole body of people and institutions taken together: 社会问题 "social problems," 社会制度 "the social system," 走向社会 "to go out into the world (after school)." The default word for society in the large, abstract sense.
我们生活在一个信息社会。
Wǒmen shēnghuó zài yí ge xìnxī shèhuì.
We live in an information society.
毕业以后他就走向了社会。
Bìyè yǐhòu tā jiù zǒuxiàng le shèhuì.
After graduating he went out into the wider world.
社区 shèqū community; residential neighbourhood
N 名词 míngcí
社 shè (community) + 区 qū (district, zone). A bounded local community: the people of a single neighbourhood, plus the services and administration that serve them. 社区服务 "community services," 社区医院 "a community clinic," 社区活动 "neighbourhood activities." Smaller and more concrete than 社会.
我们社区有一个新的图书馆。
Wǒmen shèqū yǒu yí ge xīn de túshūguǎn.
Our community has a new library.
辨析 biànxī · 社会 vs. 社区 社会 shèhuì is society at large, the whole abstract web of people and institutions; you cannot point to its edges. 社区 shèqū is a concrete, bounded place where people actually live near one another, with a name, a gate, and a management office. One is the great collective, the other your local neighbourhood.
社交 shèjiāo social interaction; socializing
N 名词 míngcí
社 shè (social) + 交 jiāo (to associate, to exchange). The act of mixing with others, the give-and-take of social life: 社交活动 "social activities," 社交能力 "social skills," 社交媒体 "social media." It names the personal practice of meeting and getting along with people.
他不太喜欢社交。
Tā bú tài xǐhuan shèjiāo.
He does not much enjoy socializing.
社交媒体改变了人们的生活。
Shèjiāo méitǐ gǎibiàn le rénmen de shēnghuó.
Social media has changed the way people live.
社团 shètuán Associations & Agencies
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Formalise a circle of people around a shared purpose and 社 names the resulting body. On its own, 社团 shètuán is the general word for a club or society, the kind students join on campus. More productively, 社 sits at the tail of a long line of compounds naming a particular kind of organization or agency: a 报社 is a newspaper office, a 通讯社 a news agency, a 旅行社 a travel agency, an 出版社 a publishing house. In each, a domain word sets the field and 社 supplies the institution.

社团 shètuán club; society; an organized group
N 名词 míngcí
社 shè (association) + 团 tuán (a group, a body). A voluntary organized group bound by a shared interest: 学生社团 "student clubs," 民间社团 "civic associations." The everyday word for the clubs and societies people choose to join, especially at school and university.
大学里有很多有趣的社团。
Dàxué lǐ yǒu hěn duō yǒuqù de shètuán.
There are many interesting clubs at the university.
她参加了学校的摄影社团。
Tā cānjiā le xuéxiào de shèyǐng shètuán.
She joined the school photography club.
报社 bàoshè newspaper office; press
N 名词 míngcí
报 bào (newspaper) + 社 shè (an agency, office). The organization that produces a newspaper, both the institution and its premises: 在报社工作 "to work at a newspaper." The 社 here is the institutional body, not a club; the same slot gives 出版社 "publishing house" and 杂志社 "magazine office."
他在一家报社当记者。
Tā zài yì jiā bàoshè dāng jìzhě.
He works as a reporter at a newspaper.
构词 gòucí · The X + 社 agency pattern A domain word set before 社 names an institution of that field: 报社 newspaper office, 出版社 chūbǎnshè (publishing house), 通讯社 tōngxùnshè (news agency), 杂志社 zázhìshè (magazine office). Once you read 社 as "the organization that does this," each new compound explains itself.
旅行社 lǚxíngshè travel agency
N 名词 míngcí
旅行 lǚxíng (to travel) + 社 shè (agency). The business that arranges trips, tours, and tickets: 跟旅行社报名 "to sign up through a travel agency," 旅行社的导游 "the agency's tour guide." A textbook example of the agency sense of 社 attached to a whole two-syllable field word.
我们通过旅行社订了机票。
Wǒmen tōngguò lǚxíngshè dìng le jīpiào.
We booked our plane tickets through a travel agency.
社稷 shèjì The Soil-God & the State
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Beneath the modern senses lies the oldest one, the soil-god and its altar. The key survival is 社稷 shèjì, a pairing of 社, the spirit of the soil, with 稷 jì, the spirit of grain. Together the soil and the grain were the two altars a ruler kept, and because no state could stand without land to hold it and harvest to feed it, 社稷 came to mean the state or the realm itself. The village-level cult survives too in 土地, the local land-and-soil deity honoured at small shrines. These uses are formal or literary now, but they are the root from which everything else in this character grew.

社稷 shèjì the altars of soil and grain; the state, the realm
N 名词 míngcí
社 shè (the soil-god) + 稷 jì (the grain-god). Originally the two altars a ruler maintained, to the powers of the earth and of the harvest. Because land and food are the foundation of any polity, the word came to stand for the state itself: 江山社稷 "the rivers, mountains, and altars," the whole realm. A formal, classical term.
古代帝王把社稷看得比生命还重。
Gǔdài dìwáng bǎ shèjì kàn de bǐ shēngmìng hái zhòng.
Rulers of old valued the state more than their own lives.
土地 tǔdì land, soil; the local earth-god
N 名词 míngcí
土 tǔ (earth) + 地 dì (ground). In everyday speech, land or soil: 土地肥沃 "fertile land." In folk religion it also names the 土地神 or 土地公, the humble local earth-god of a village or plot, the small-scale heir to the old 社 cult, honoured at modest roadside shrines.
农民对脚下的土地有很深的感情。
Nóngmín duì jiǎoxià de tǔdì yǒu hěn shēn de gǎnqíng.
Farmers feel a deep attachment to the land beneath their feet.
文化 wénhuà · From soil-god to earth-god The grand 社 of the state shrank, over time, into the friendly village 土地公, a minor but beloved deity who watches over a small patch of ground. The two are kin: both are the spirit of the soil, one honoured by emperors at the altars of 社稷, the other by farmers at a little shrine by the field.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
江山社稷 jiāng shān shè jì the rivers, mountains, and altars of soil and grain: the whole realm, the state Literally "rivers and mountains, the altars of soil and grain." 江山 the land and 社稷 the altars together name the entire country a ruler holds in trust. A grand, classical phrase for the state as a whole, the territory and the legitimacy that come with it.
人面兽心 rén miàn shòu xīn a human face with the heart of a beast: a cruel person behind a civil mask Not built on 社 itself, but a classic measure of how a person fits or fails the bonds of human society: someone who looks like one of us yet behaves with brute cruelty. A reminder that belonging to a 社会 is judged by conduct, not appearance.
和光同尘 hé guāng tóng chén to soften one's light and share the dust: to blend in and live among ordinary people From the Daoist classics: to dim one's brilliance and mix with the common crowd rather than stand apart. A vision of how the wise move within society, joining its dust instead of towering over it. The ideal of fitting unobtrusively into the social whole.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold one picture at the center of 社: an altar (礻) raised on the earth (土), a shrine to the god of the soil with the village gathered around it. Everything in the character is that gathering, widened or formalised.

Let the circle around the altar grow until it takes in everyone, and you have 社会 society and the smaller 社区 community, with 社交 the act of mixing within them. Carve out a piece of that circle for a single purpose and you get the organization: the 社团 club, and the long line of agencies whose name ends in 社, the 报社 newspaper office, the 旅行社 travel agency, the 出版社 publishing house. Then walk back to the altar itself and you reach the oldest sense, the 社稷 soil-and-grain altars that stood for the whole state, shrunk in the villages to the humble 土地 earth-god by the field. Whenever you meet 社, look for the gathering: who is bound together here, and around what shared ground.
