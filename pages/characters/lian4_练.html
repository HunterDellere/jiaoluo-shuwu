字源zìyuánEtymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

练 = 纟 + 柬. The silk radical 纟 (sī, the abbreviated form of 糸 mì) sits on the left and carries the meaning; the phonetic 柬 (jiǎn) sits on the right and once carried the sound. This is a clean pictophonetic build: thread on one side, a sound-marker on the other, the two welded into one craft-word.

The original sense is physical and wet. Raw silk reeled off the cocoon comes off stiff, gummed, and yellowish with sericin. To make it usable you boil it, again and again, in alkaline water until the gum lifts and the fiber turns soft, white, and pliant. That repeated boiling-and-rinsing of silk is the root meaning of 练: 熟丝 shú sī, cooked silk, as opposed to 生丝 shēng sī, raw silk. The Shuōwén Jiězì glosses it squarely as treating silk.

From the steaming vat the meaning climbed off the thread and onto the person. What you do to raw silk, you also do to a raw skill: subject it to the same treatment over and over until the stiffness boils out and it runs smooth. So 练 became to drill, to practice, to train, and the participle 练 came to mean tempered, seasoned, experienced. A person who is 老练 (lǎoliàn) has been through the vat many times.

Japanese keeps the thread sense vividly in 練 (ren): 練る neru is to knead, to refine, to work dough or metal or a plan until it is smooth, the same image of repeated working that softens and strengthens.

反复fǎnfùPractice & Drill

练 is the everyday verb for putting in the reps. 练 + object (练字, 练琴, 练球) means to practice that thing; 练习 is the standard two-character verb and noun for practice and exercise; 训练 raises the register to organized, often physical or professional, training.

练习liànxíto practice; an exercise
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
练 (drill) + 习 (to study, to be habituated). The two near-synonyms stack into the all-purpose word for practicing, and as a noun for a practice exercise or drill set.
每天练习半个小时，发音就会进步。
Měitiān liànxí bàn gè xiǎoshí, fāyīn jiù huì jìnbù.
Practice half an hour a day and your pronunciation will improve.
这本书后面有很多练习。
Zhè běn shū hòumiàn yǒu hěn duō liànxí.
There are a lot of exercises at the back of this book.
训练xùnliànto train; training
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
训 (to instruct, to discipline) + 练 (to drill). Organized, directed training: athletes, soldiers, dogs, new staff. Heavier and more institutional than 练习.
运动员每天接受严格的训练。
Yùndòngyuán měitiān jiēshòu yángé de xùnliàn.
The athletes undergo strict training every day.
辨析 biànxī · 训练 vs. 练习训练 is run by an instructor or program and aims at a standard; 练习 is the reps themselves and can be solo and casual.
练字liàn zìto practice handwriting
V 动词 dòngcí
练 (drill) + 字 (characters). The classic 练 + object pattern: drilling one's brush or pen strokes. Swap the object for 练琴 (practice an instrument) or 练球 (practice a ball sport).
他每天晚上用毛笔练字。
Tā měitiān wǎnshang yòng máobǐ liàn zì.
He practices calligraphy with a brush every evening.
熟练shúliànSkill & Mastery
语义延伸 yǔyì yánshēn · Sense Extension

When 练 stops being the action and becomes the result, it names the quality the drilling produced: practiced, fluent, seasoned. 熟练 is smooth-from-repetition; 老练 is veteran, weathered by long experience; 教练 turns the trainer into a noun. The thread that came out of the vat soft and white is now the hand that moves without hesitation.

熟练shúliànskilled, proficient, fluent
Adj 形容词 xíngróngcí
熟 (cooked, ripe, familiar) + 练 (drilled). The same 熟 used for cooked food: a skill drilled until it is done, no longer raw. Describes a practiced, effortless command of a task.
她操作这台机器非常熟练。
Tā cāozuò zhè tái jīqì fēicháng shúliàn.
She operates this machine very skillfully.
教练jiàoliàncoach, instructor
N 名词 míngcí
教 (to teach) + 练 (to drill). The one who teaches by drilling: a sports coach, a driving instructor, a fitness trainer.
我们的篮球教练很严格。
Wǒmen de lánqiú jiàoliàn hěn yángé.
Our basketball coach is very strict.
老练lǎoliànseasoned, experienced
Adj 形容词 xíngróngcí
老 (old) + 练 (tempered). Weathered by long practice into steadiness: a veteran's unflappable competence. Praises judgment and poise, not just technical speed.
他处理问题很老练，一点也不慌。
Tā chǔlǐ wèntí hěn lǎoliàn, yìdiǎn yě bù huāng.
He handles problems like a veteran, without the slightest panic.
成语chéngyǔIdioms & Set Phrases
训练有素xùn liàn yǒu sùtrained to a standard: well-drilled, disciplined素 here means a long-cultivated grounding. Praises a team, unit, or staff whose competence is visibly the product of systematic 训练 rather than luck.
练达老成liàn dá lǎo chéngpracticed and worldly, mature and steady练达 means seasoned in the ways of the world; pairs with 老成 (mature beyond one's years). A literary compliment for someone whose poise comes from experience, not age alone.
练兵秣马liàn bīng mò mǎdrill the troops and feed the horses: ready oneself for the contest练 here is its oldest active sense, to drill. The image is an army putting in the reps before battle, so the phrase travels easily to any team or person training hard ahead of a test.
简练揣摩jiǎn liàn chuǎi móto study and refine through relentless, focused effortFrom the Zhanguo Ce account of Su Qin reading until he was exhausted, jabbing his own thigh with an awl to stay awake. 练 carries the sense of working material over and over until it is honed.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture a dye-house courtyard. A worker lifts a hank of raw silk, stiff and yellow, out of a cauldron of boiling alkaline water, dunks it back, lifts it again. Steam, the smell of wood ash, the same motion a hundred times. With each cycle the thread loses a little of its gum and gains a little whiteness, until what came out brittle goes back in soft. That cauldron is the left side of the character, the silk radical 纟; the phonetic 柬 on the right is just the bystander who tells you it sounds like jiǎn.

Now move the cauldron indoors. Swap the silk for a student at a desk, a runner on a track, a recruit on a parade ground. The treatment is identical: the same motion, repeated, until the rawness boils off. 练习 is the dunking. 训练 is a foreman running the vat to a standard. 熟练 is the finished hank, smooth and white. 老练 is the silk so often boiled it will never go stiff again.
