字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

结 jié is a pictophonetic compound: the silk radical 纟 (sī) carries the meaning, and 吉 (jí) supplies the sound. The left element 纟 is the bound, twisted-thread form of 丝 sī (silk), the radical that marks dozens of characters about cord, weaving, and binding. On the right, 吉 jí (auspicious) is the phonetic, close in sound to the whole. The simplified 结 and the traditional 結 differ only in that left radical: simplified 纟 against traditional 糹, the same silk element drawn in full.

The core image is concrete: threads pulled tight into a knot. From the knot in silk, every sense of 结 unwinds. The character is polyphonic, splitting into two readings that share this root image:

jié covers the tying, joining, and concluding senses, the everyday literary readings.
jiē covers the agricultural sense of a plant tying fruit onto its branch, plus a few colloquial words.

Three streams of meaning run through the jié reading:

1. To tie, to knot. The literal act: 打结 dǎ jié (to tie a knot), 领结 lǐngjié (bow tie), 结绳 jié shéng (to knot cords).
2. To join, to unite. Two things bound into one: 结合 jiéhé (to combine), 结婚 jiéhūn (to marry), 团结 tuánjié (to unite).
3. To conclude, to settle. Tying off the loose end of a process: 结束 jiéshù (to end), 结论 jiélùn (conclusion), 结账 jiézhàng (to settle the bill).

The thread through all of them is the knot that holds: a bond made fast, an account closed, a matter tied shut. To 结 something is to bring its loose ends together and fix them in place.

结合 jiéhé Tie & Unite
结合 jiéhé to combine; to integrate; to unite
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
结 jié (to tie) + 合 hé (to join). To bring two things together so they work as one: 理论与实践结合 "to combine theory with practice." Abstract and constructive, used of ideas, methods, and elements, not of physical knots.
把学习和工作结合起来。
Bǎ xuéxí hé gōngzuò jiéhé qǐlái.
Combine study with work.
结婚 jiéhūn to marry; to get married
V 动词 dòngcí
结 jié (to tie) + 婚 hūn (marriage). To tie the marriage bond, the standard word for getting married. A separable verb: 结过婚 "have been married," 结了婚 "got married." Its opposite is 离婚 líhūn (to divorce).
他们去年结婚了。
Tāmen qùnián jiéhūn le.
They got married last year.
团结 tuánjié to unite; unity; solidarity
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
团 tuán (round; group) + 结 jié (to bind). To draw a group tightly together into one body: 团结一致 "united as one." Used of teams, communities, and nations holding together against difficulty.
大家要团结起来。
Dàjiā yào tuánjié qǐlái.
Everyone should unite.
结束 jiéshù Conclude & Settle
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The same knot that joins two threads can also tie off the end of one. In this stream, 结 means to bring a process to a clean stop: to finish, to draw a conclusion, to close an account. 结束 jiéshù ends an event, 结论 jiélùn states the conclusion of an argument, 结账 jiézhàng squares the bill, and 总结 zǒngjié sums up what came before. Each one is the loose end of something tied shut so nothing more dangles.

Watch the high-frequency word 结果, which lives in two of this character's senses at once. Read jiéguǒ it is a noun meaning "result, outcome," the thing a process ties off into: 实验的结果 "the result of the experiment." Read jiē guǒ with the second reading it is a verb meaning "to bear fruit." The settling sense and the fruiting sense meet in this one written word, which is why the reading you choose tells the listener whether you mean an outcome or an actual fruit on a branch.

结束 jiéshù to end; to finish; to conclude
V 动词 dòngcí
结 jié (to tie off) + 束 shù (to bind; a bundle). To bring an event or period to its close: 会议结束了 "the meeting is over." Its opposite is 开始 kāishǐ (to begin).
电影八点结束。
Diànyǐng bā diǎn jiéshù.
The movie ends at eight.
结果 jiéguǒ result; outcome; as a result
N 名词 míngcí
结 jié (to conclude) + 果 guǒ (fruit; result). The outcome a process ties off into. Also a connective: "as it turned out." Read this way it is jiéguǒ; the verb "to bear fruit" reads jiē guǒ instead.
考试的结果还没出来。
Kǎoshì de jiéguǒ hái méi chūlái.
The exam results are not out yet.
辨析 biànxī · 结果 jiéguǒ vs. 结合 jiéhé 结果 jiéguǒ is an outcome, the thing a process ends in. 结合 jiéhé is the joining of two things into one. One looks at the end of a process, the other at the binding of two parts; do not let the shared 结 blur them.
结论 jiélùn conclusion; verdict; final judgment
N 名词 míngcí
结 jié (to conclude) + 论 lùn (argument; discussion). The settled judgment an argument or investigation arrives at: 得出结论 "to reach a conclusion." More formal than 结果, weighted toward reasoning rather than mere outcome.
现在下结论还太早。
Xiànzài xià jiélùn hái tài zǎo.
It is too early to draw a conclusion now.
结果 jiē guǒ Bear Fruit: the Reading jiē
语音洞见 yǔyīn dòngjiàn · Reading Insight

The second reading, jiē, is the down-to-earth one. Its home is the orchard: when a tree ties its blossoms into fruit, it 结 jiē. 树上结了很多苹果 means "the tree has borne many apples," and here 结果 is read jiē guǒ, a verb, the plant tying fruit onto its branch. This is the original literal force of the knot image applied to growing things, the bond between branch and fruit.

From sturdiness comes the second jiē word, 结实 jiēshi, meaning solid, firm, well-built, said of a rope, a chair, or a person's robust frame: 这把椅子很结实 "this chair is very sturdy." Note the contrast in reading once more: with the jié reading, 结实 jiéshí would literally be "to set fruit," but the everyday adjective "sturdy" takes the jiē reading with a neutral-tone second syllable, jiēshi. The reading you hear tells you whether someone means an outcome, an argument, a marriage, or a firm and solid thing.

结果 jiē guǒ to bear fruit; to fruit
V 动词 dòngcí
结 jiē (to tie on) + 果 guǒ (fruit). The verb a plant performs when it sets fruit. Same written word as the noun 结果 jiéguǒ "result," but the jiē reading is always the literal fruiting one.
这棵树今年结了很多果子。
Zhè kē shù jīnnián jiē le hěnduō guǒzi.
This tree bore a lot of fruit this year.
结实 jiēshi sturdy; solid; durable; robust
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
结 jiē + 实 shi (solid). Firm and well-built, whether a knot, a piece of furniture, or a healthy body: 身体很结实 "physically robust." Takes the jiē reading with a toneless second syllable.
这双鞋很结实，能穿好几年。
Zhè shuāng xié hěn jiēshi, néng chuān hǎojǐ nián.
These shoes are sturdy and will last several years.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
张灯结彩 zhāng dēng jié cǎi hang lanterns and tie up silks: to decorate festively for a celebration Lit: hang-lanterns-tie-colored-silks. The classic picture of a place dressed up for a wedding, a festival, or New Year, with lanterns strung and bright silk streamers tied everywhere. Here 结 is the literal tying of decorations.
归根结底 guī gēn jié dǐ to return to the root and settle the bottom: in the final analysis, when all is said and done Lit: return-root-conclude-bottom. Used to introduce the fundamental point after stripping away the surface: 归根结底，还是钱的问题 "in the end, it still comes down to money." Here 结 carries the settling sense.
精诚团结 jīng chéng tuán jié to unite with wholehearted sincerity: to stand together in good faith Lit: pure-sincere-unite. A call for a group to bond in genuine, undivided solidarity rather than in mere appearance. 结 here is the uniting sense, the bond made fast among people.
结草衔环 jié cǎo xián huán to knot grass and carry rings: to repay a kindness, even after death Lit: tie-grass-hold-ring. From two old tales of gratitude: a ghost knotted grass to trip an enemy and save his benefactor, and a magpie brought jade rings to repay a rescuer. Together they stand for never forgetting a debt of kindness.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold the silk radical 纟 in mind and watch a thread pull into a knot. That knot is the whole character. Tie two threads and you have a bond: a union, a marriage, a people held together (结合, 结婚, 团结). Tie off the end of one and you have closure: an ending, a conclusion, a settled bill (结束, 结论, 结账). Tie fruit onto a branch and the tree bears (结果 jiē guǒ); tie a thing together firmly and it is solid (结实 jiēshi).

Let the reading guide you. The rising jié ties bonds and ties off ends, the literary work of joining and concluding. The level jiē ties fruit to a branch and ties a thing together strong, the earthier work of the orchard and the well-built. One written word, two knots: hear which one, and you know whether 结 is binding people, closing a matter, or bearing fruit.
