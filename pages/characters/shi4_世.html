字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

世 shì is a pictograph traditionally read as three tens joined together, the span of thirty years that the ancients counted as one human generation. The oldest forms show three vertical strokes, each marking a unit of ten, gathered onto a single base line, so that the whole glyph stands for thirty and, by extension, for the generation that thirty years measures. The simplified and traditional forms are identical; only one glyph exists. Its assigned radical for dictionary lookup is 一 (one), the horizontal stroke that anchors the figure.

From the image of one generation, thirty years long, the senses widen along two arcs, the human and the cosmic:

1. Generation, lineage. One human cohort succeeding the one before, and the line of descent through which a family or office passes: 世代 shìdài (generations), 后世 hòushì (later generations), 世袭 shìxí (hereditary).
2. Lifetime, an age, the life to come. A single span of life, or one of the lives a soul passes through: 一生一世 yìshēng yíshì (a whole lifetime), 来世 láishì (the next life), 今世 jīnshì (this life).
3. The world, human society. The whole sphere of living people and their dealings: 世界 shìjiè (the world), 世上 shìshàng (in this world), 人世 rénshì (the human world), 世俗 shìsú (secular).
4. An era, a period. A defined stretch of historical time: 世纪 shìjì (century), 盛世 shèngshì (a flourishing age), 当世 dāngshì (the present age).

The thread is the span of a life writ large: one generation handing on to the next, those generations stacking into eras, and the whole crowded stage of living people taken together as the world.

世代 shìdài Generation & Lineage
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The root sense of 世 is the generation, the cohort that holds the world for its thirty years before passing it on. 世代 shìdài names that succession in the plural, the long file of generations stretching back and forward. 后世 hòushì looks ahead to the generations not yet born, the posterity who will judge what we leave them. 世袭 shìxí turns the line of descent into a rule of inheritance, a title or duty handed down by blood rather than chosen. Each word treats the family or the office as a relay, run not by one person but by the chain of those who stand where their parents stood.

世代 shìdài generations; for generations; from age to age
N 名词 míngcí
世 shì (generation) + 代 dài (generation, era). The succession of generations taken together: 世代相传 "handed down through the generations," 世代务农 "farming for generations." Often adverbial, meaning across the whole line of descent.
这门手艺在他家世代相传。
Zhè mén shǒuyì zài tā jiā shìdài xiāngchuán.
This craft has been handed down through the generations in his family.
他们家世代住在这个村子里。
Tāmen jiā shìdài zhù zài zhège cūnzi lǐ.
Their family has lived in this village for generations.
后世 hòushì later generations; posterity
N 名词 míngcí
后 hòu (after; later) + 世 shì (generation). The generations who come after us: 流芳后世 "to leave a good name to posterity," 后世子孙 "descendants of later generations." Looks forward to those who will inherit and remember.
他的著作对后世影响很大。
Tā de zhùzuò duì hòushì yǐngxiǎng hěn dà.
His writings had a great influence on later generations.
辨析 biànxī · 后世 vs. 后代 后世 means later ages and the people living in them, the broad sweep of posterity who inherit a legacy. 后代 leans toward one's own descendants, the children and grandchildren of a specific line. A scholar influences 后世; a family worries about its 后代.
世袭 shìxí hereditary; to inherit by succession
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
世 shì (generation) + 袭 xí (to inherit; to succeed to). Passed down by birth from one generation to the next: 世袭制度 "the hereditary system," 世袭爵位 "a hereditary title." Marks a right or rank inherited through bloodline rather than earned or appointed.
古代的贵族爵位常常是世袭的。
Gǔdài de guìzú juéwèi chángcháng shì shìxí de.
In ancient times, noble titles were often hereditary.
来世 láishì Lifetime & the Next Life
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Narrow 世 from the line of generations down to a single span and it becomes a lifetime, the one life a person is given. 一生一世 yìshēng yíshì binds 生 (a life) and 世 (a lifetime) into a vow of permanence, the whole of one's days. Touched by Buddhist thought, 世 also names one life among many: 来世 láishì is the life to come, 前世 the life before, 今世 the present one, the three woven together by rebirth. Here the same syllable that counts the dynasties of history counts the lives a soul moves through, each a single turn of the same wheel.

一生一世 yìshēng yíshì all one's life; a whole lifetime; forever
N 名词 míngcí
一生 yìshēng (one life) + 一世 yíshì (one lifetime). A doubled phrase for the entirety of a person's days, common in vows of love and loyalty: 我会爱你一生一世 "I will love you for the rest of my life." Warmer and more lyrical than the plain 一辈子.
他们约定一生一世在一起。
Tāmen yuēdìng yìshēng yíshì zài yìqǐ.
They promised to be together for their whole lives.
来世 láishì the next life; the afterlife; the life to come
N 名词 míngcí
来 lái (coming) + 世 shì (life). The life that follows this one in the Buddhist cycle of rebirth: 来世再相见 "to meet again in the next life." Paired with 前世 (a former life) and 今世 (this life) to span the chain of rebirths.
他相信有来世。
Tā xiāngxìn yǒu láishì.
He believes there is a next life.
辨析 biànxī · 来世 vs. 一世 来世 is one of several lives in the cycle of rebirth, the next turn of the wheel after this one ends. 一世 is simply one whole lifetime taken on its own, the full span of a single life with no claim about what comes after.
今世 jīnshì this life; the present life; this age
N 名词 míngcí
今 jīn (present; now) + 世 shì (life; age). The life one is living now, set against 前世 (a former life) and 来世 (the next): 今生今世 "this very life." Can also mean the present age in a more general sense.
能遇见你是我今世的幸运。
Néng yùjiàn nǐ shì wǒ jīnshì de xìngyùn.
Meeting you is the good fortune of this life of mine.
世界 shìjiè The World & the Age
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Open 世 to its widest setting and it names the world itself, the whole crowded stage of living people and their affairs. 世界 shìjiè, the great everyday word for the world, joins 世 (the span of living time) to 界 (a boundary), so that the world is time bounded into a place we all share. 世俗 shìsú marks what belongs to this ordinary human world rather than the sacred, the secular and the commonplace. 世故 shìgù distills the world into hard-won knowledge of how people really behave, the worldly wisdom of someone no longer naive. And stacked into long stretches, 世 gives 世纪 shìjì (the century) and 盛世 shèngshì (a flourishing age), the eras by which history measures itself.

世界 shìjiè world; the world
N 名词 míngcí
世 shì (the living age) + 界 jiè (boundary; realm). The world as a whole, both the physical globe and the human sphere within it: 全世界 "the whole world," 世界和平 "world peace," 世界观 "worldview." One of the most common nouns in the language.
这是世界上最高的山。
Zhè shì shìjiè shàng zuì gāo de shān.
This is the highest mountain in the world.
他想环游世界。
Tā xiǎng huányóu shìjiè.
He wants to travel around the world.
辨析 biànxī · 世界 vs. 天下 世界 is the modern, geographic and human world: the planet, its nations, all its people. 天下 is the older, more poetic "all under heaven," the world conceived as the realm subject to one moral and political order. 世界 measures the globe; 天下 once measured an empire and now survives mostly in idiom and rhetoric.
世纪 shìjì century
N 名词 míngcí
世 shì (age) + 纪 jì (a record; a span of years). A hundred-year stretch of time: 二十一世纪 "the twenty-first century," 半个世纪 "half a century," 跨世纪 "spanning the turn of the century." The standard unit for dividing modern history.
我们生活在二十一世纪。
Wǒmen shēnghuó zài èrshíyī shìjì.
We live in the twenty-first century.
世俗 shìsú secular; worldly; common; mundane
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
世 shì (the human world) + 俗 sú (custom; common). What belongs to ordinary worldly life rather than the religious or ideal: 世俗社会 "secular society," 世俗的眼光 "a worldly point of view." Can describe the secular as a neutral domain or carry a mild note of the common and unrefined.
他不在乎世俗的看法。
Tā bú zàihu shìsú de kànfǎ.
He does not care about conventional, worldly opinions.
盛世 shèngshì a flourishing age; a golden age; a prosperous era
N 名词 míngcí
盛 shèng (flourishing; abundant) + 世 shì (age). An era of peace and prosperity, the high point of a dynasty or a civilization: 太平盛世 "an age of peace and plenty," 康乾盛世 "the High Qing flourishing." The bright opposite of 乱世, an age of chaos.
人们都向往太平盛世。
Rénmen dōu xiàngwǎng tàipíng shèngshì.
Everyone longs for an age of peace and prosperity.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
举世闻名 jǔ shì wén míng world-famous: renowned throughout the whole world Lit: the whole world has heard the name. 举世 means the entire world taken together, 闻名 means to be known by name. Said of a person, place, or work whose fame reaches everywhere, as in the Great Wall or a celebrated master.
世外桃源 shì wài táo yuán a haven of peace: an idyllic refuge cut off from the troubled world Lit: a peach-blossom spring beyond the world. From Tao Yuanming's fable of a fisherman who stumbles on a hidden valley untouched by war and time. Now any tranquil place sheltered from the strife and bustle of ordinary life.
与世无争 yǔ shì wú zhēng to stand aloof from strife: to live without contending against the world Lit: with the world, no contention. Describes a person who seeks neither fame nor advantage and stays clear of rivalry, content with a quiet life. Carries a Daoist flavor of withdrawal and unforced ease.
不可一世 bù kě yī shì insufferably arrogant: to think no one in the whole age is one's equal Lit: cannot be matched by anyone of the age. Said of someone so overweeningly proud that they look down on everyone alive, behaving as though the entire era holds no rival worthy of them. Strongly critical in tone.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold one picture at the center of 世: three tens gathered onto a single line, thirty years, the length of one human generation. That single span is the seed of every sense.

Keep the span at human scale and you get the generation: 世代 generations, 后世 later generations, 世袭 a title handed down by blood. Shrink it to one person's days and you get the lifetime: 一生一世 a whole life, and, touched by the wheel of rebirth, 来世 the life to come and 今世 this one. Now stack many lifetimes end to end and you get the era: 世纪 a century, 盛世 a golden age. Open the same word to its widest setting and it becomes the world entire, the crowded stage of all the living: 世界 the world, 世俗 the worldly, 世故 the wisdom of one who knows how the world works. Whenever you meet 世, ask which circle is meant: a generation, a lifetime, an age, or the whole turning world.
