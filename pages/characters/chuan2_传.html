字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

传 is a pictophonetic compound: the person radical 亻 carries the meaning, while 专 (zhuān) supplies the sound. The 亻 on the left marks the character as something done by or among people, and the core idea is one person handing a thing on to the next. The phonetic on the right lends mainly its pronunciation. The simplified 传 reduces the busier right side of the traditional 傳, but the left-hand person radical and the structure are the same in both forms.

From the single image of passing a thing from hand to hand, the character splits into two readings:

Read chuán the senses are verbal and outward-moving:
1. To pass on, to hand down. To carry something forward through time or along a line: 传统 chuántǒng (tradition), 传递 chuándì (to pass, to relay), 遗传 yíchuán (heredity), 流传 liúchuán (to circulate).
2. To transmit, to spread. To send a thing outward through space or a population: 传播 chuánbō (to disseminate), 传染 chuánrǎn (to infect), 宣传 xuānchuán (to publicize), 传开 chuánkāi (to spread around).
3. To convey, to impart. To deliver a message or teaching: 传达 chuándá (to convey), 传授 chuánshòu (to impart), 传唤 chuánhuàn (to summon).

Read zhuàn the sense turns nominal and literary: a written record handed down, a 传记 zhuànjì (biography) or a 经传 jīngzhuàn (classic and its commentary). The thread is the same in both readings: something carried onward from one party to another, whether a spoken word, a contagion, or a written life.

传统 chuántǒng To Pass On & Hand Down
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The first face of 传 chuán is carrying a thing forward through time, from one hand or one generation to the next. 传统 chuántǒng is the great noun of this sense: tradition, the body of custom handed down across generations. 传递 chuándì is the plain verb, to pass or relay an object, a message, a baton, from one point to another. Push the idea into the body and you get 遗传 yíchuán, heredity, the traits a parent passes to a child. Spread it across a whole society over time and you get 流传 liúchuán, the way a story or a song circulates and survives. All share the image of something kept alive by being handed on.

传统 chuántǒng tradition; traditional; conventional
N/ADJ 名词 míngcí
传 chuán (to hand down) + 统 tǒng (a continuous line, a system). The customs, values, and practices passed from one generation to the next: 传统文化 "traditional culture," 优良传统 "fine tradition." As an adjective it marks something as established and inherited rather than new.
春节是中国最重要的传统节日。
Chūnjié shì Zhōngguó zuì zhòngyào de chuántǒng jiérì.
The Spring Festival is China's most important traditional holiday.
这是我们家的老传统。
Zhè shì wǒmen jiā de lǎo chuántǒng.
This is an old tradition in our family.
传递 chuándì to pass; to relay; to convey; to transmit
V 动词 dòngcí
传 chuán (to pass on) + 递 dì (to hand over). To move something from one party to the next in a chain: 传递信息 "to relay information," 传递火炬 "to pass the torch." Stresses the act of handing along, often through several hands or steps.
请把这本书传递给后面的同学。
Qǐng bǎ zhè běn shū chuándì gěi hòumiàn de tóngxué.
Please pass this book back to the classmate behind you.
这条消息很快就传递开了。
Zhè tiáo xiāoxi hěn kuài jiù chuándì kāi le.
This piece of news was relayed onward very quickly.
遗传 yíchuán heredity; to pass on (genetically); hereditary
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
遗 yí (to leave behind) + 传 chuán (to hand down). The biological passing of traits from parent to offspring: 遗传病 "hereditary disease," 遗传基因 "the genes one inherits." The body's own version of handing something down across generations.
他的高个子是遗传的。
Tā de gāo gèzi shì yíchuán de.
His tall height is inherited.
流传 liúchuán to circulate; to spread and survive; to be handed down
V 动词 dòngcí
流 liú (to flow) + 传 chuán (to pass on). For a story, saying, or work to circulate widely and endure over time: 这个传说一直流传至今 "this legend has been handed down to the present day." Combines the flowing of 流 with the passing-on of 传.
这首古诗流传了上千年。
Zhè shǒu gǔshī liúchuán le shàng qiān nián.
This ancient poem has been handed down for over a thousand years.
传播 chuánbō To Transmit & Spread
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Where the first sense moves a thing forward through time, the second sends it outward through space and through a population. 传播 chuánbō is to disseminate, to broadcast a message, an idea, or a disease so that it reaches many. 传染 chuánrǎn narrows that to contagion, the spread of an illness from one body to another. 宣传 xuānchuán turns the act deliberate: to publicize, to propagate a message on purpose. Each takes the radical's image of handing a thing on and multiplies it outward to a crowd.

传播 chuánbō to disseminate; to spread; to broadcast; communication
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
传 chuán (to transmit) + 播 bō (to sow, to broadcast). To spread information, ideas, or culture widely: 传播知识 "to disseminate knowledge," 大众传播 "mass communication." Also the academic field name, 传播学 "communication studies."
网络让信息传播得更快。
Wǎngluò ràng xìnxī chuánbō de gèng kuài.
The internet lets information spread faster.
传染 chuánrǎn to infect; to be contagious; to spread (a disease)
V 动词 dòngcí
传 chuán (to transmit) + 染 rǎn (to dye, to be tainted). The spread of an illness from one person to another: 传染病 "infectious disease." Figuratively, an emotion or a mood that catches: 他的笑很有传染力 "his laugh is contagious."
感冒很容易传染给别人。
Gǎnmào hěn róngyì chuánrǎn gěi biérén.
A cold spreads to others very easily.
辨析 biànxī · 传播 vs. 传染 传播 is broad dissemination through space and society, of news, ideas, culture, or, neutrally, of a disease across a population. 传染 is the specific person-to-person passing of an illness (or, by extension, a mood). A virus can 传播 through a city as a phenomenon, while it 传染 from one individual to the next as a direct contact. Use 传播 for the wide spread, 传染 for the catching.
宣传 xuānchuán to publicize; to promote; publicity; propaganda
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
宣 xuān (to proclaim) + 传 chuán (to spread). To put out a message deliberately so it reaches the public: 宣传海报 "a promotional poster," 宣传活动 "a publicity campaign." Carries a sense of organized, purposeful spreading, and in political contexts the meaning of propaganda.
学校在宣传新的环保政策。
Xuéxiào zài xuānchuán xīn de huánbǎo zhèngcè.
The school is publicizing the new environmental policy.
传达 chuándá to convey; to pass on (a message); to communicate
V 动词 dòngcí
传 chuán (to convey) + 达 dá (to reach, to arrive). To deliver a message, an order, or a feeling so that it reaches its target: 传达指示 "to convey instructions," 传达上级的精神 "to pass on the leadership's intent." Stresses that the content arrives intact.
请把这个消息传达给大家。
Qǐng bǎ zhège xiāoxi chuándá gěi dàjiā.
Please convey this message to everyone.
传记 zhuànjì Biography & Commentary (read zhuàn)
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Shift the tone from chuán to zhuàn and the character turns from a verb into a noun: a written record handed down. A 传记 zhuànjì is a biography, the account of a life set down for posterity; tell your own and it becomes 自传 zìzhuàn, an autobiography. In the classical histories, the 列传 lièzhuàn are the chapters of collected biographies, the lives of statesmen and generals arranged one after another. The same reading names a 传 in the sense of a commentary attached to a classic, as in 经传 jīngzhuàn, "the classics and their commentaries." It even survives in famous titles: 水浒传 Shuǐhǔ Zhuàn, the great novel known in English as Water Margin, where 传 marks the work as the handed-down chronicle of its heroes.

传记 zhuànjì biography; a life story
N 名词 míngcí
传 zhuàn (a handed-down record) + 记 jì (to record). A written account of a person's life: 名人传记 "celebrity biographies," 人物传记 "biographies of notable figures." Note the reading shifts to zhuàn here, never chuán.
我最近在读一本科学家的传记。
Wǒ zuìjìn zài dú yì běn kēxuéjiā de zhuànjì.
I have lately been reading the biography of a scientist.
自传 zìzhuàn autobiography
N 名词 míngcí
自 zì (self) + 传 zhuàn (a life record). A biography written by its own subject, the story of a life told in the first person: 写自传 "to write an autobiography." Like 传记, it takes the zhuàn reading.
这位作家晚年写了一本自传。
Zhè wèi zuòjiā wǎnnián xiě le yì běn zìzhuàn.
This writer wrote an autobiography in his later years.
读音 dúyīn · chuán vs. zhuàn The same glyph 传 carries two readings. As a verb of passing, transmitting, or spreading, it is chuán (传播, 传统, 传染). When it names a written life or a commentary handed down, it is zhuàn (传记, 自传, 列传, 经传). A useful rule of thumb: if the word means an act of carrying something on, read chuán; if it names a book or chapter about a person, read zhuàn.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
言传身教 yán chuán shēn jiào to teach by word and by example: to instruct both through speech and through one's own conduct Lit: words pass it on, the body teaches it. Describes the ideal of a teacher or parent who not only explains in words (言传) but also models the lesson through their own actions (身教). The deeper teaching is the one carried by example.
传宗接代 chuán zōng jiē dài to carry on the family line: to continue the ancestral lineage through descendants Lit: pass on the clan, link the generations. The traditional duty of producing heirs so that the family line does not end. 宗 is the ancestral lineage and 代 the generations; 传 and 接 are the passing-on and the picking-up that keep the chain unbroken.
名不虚传 míng bù xū chuán the reputation is not falsely spread: to live up to one's name; to be as good as people say Lit: the name is not emptily passed around. Said of a person or thing whose fame turns out to be well deserved, the praise that circulated was no exaggeration. Here 传 keeps the chuán reading, the spreading of a reputation.
言归正传 yán guī zhèng zhuàn let the words return to the main story: to get back to the main topic Lit: words return to the proper narrative. A storyteller's phrase, after a digression, for steering back to the central thread. Notably 传 takes the zhuàn reading here, since 正传 means the main body of the tale, the narrative proper, not a casual transmission.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold one picture at the center of 传: a person (亻) holding something out, handing it on to whoever comes next. That single gesture of passing a thing forward is the seed of every sense.

Hand it forward through time and you get the first sense, chuán as handing down: 传统 tradition, 传递 to relay, 遗传 heredity, 流传 to circulate. Hand it outward to a crowd and you get the second, chuán as spreading: 传播 to disseminate, 传染 to infect, 宣传 to publicize, 传达 to convey. Now imagine the thing being handed on is not an object or a word but a written life, a scroll bearing the story of a person, and the tone changes to zhuàn: 传记 a biography, 自传 an autobiography, 列传 the collected lives of a history. Whenever you meet 传, ask what is being passed on, and to whom, and whether the answer is an act (chuán) or a record (zhuàn).
