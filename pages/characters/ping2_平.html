字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

平 píng is a pictograph of something flat and in balance. The early forms are read as the image of breath or sound spreading out evenly, or of a level surface with two marks rising equally on either side of a central stem. Whatever the precise picture, the felt sense is the same: a thing settled level, with nothing tilted higher on one side than the other. The modern glyph keeps that symmetry, a horizontal base crossed by an upright stem with a balanced stroke to each side. The simplified and traditional forms are identical.

From the image of the level surface grow four senses that fan across the language:

1. Flat, level, even. The literal meaning, a surface with no bumps: 平面 píngmiàn (a flat surface, a plane), 平地 píngdì (level ground), 平稳 píngwěn (steady; smooth).
2. Equal, fair. A balance where no side is weighted over another: 公平 gōngpíng (fair), 平等 píngděng (equality), 平均 píngjūn (average; even).
3. Peaceful, calm. A surface undisturbed, with no waves or strife: 和平 hépíng (peace), 平安 píngān (safe and sound), 平静 píngjìng (calm).
4. Ordinary, plain. Nothing rising above the common level: 平常 píngcháng (ordinary), 平凡 píngfán (commonplace), 水平 shuǐpíng (level; standard).

The thread through every sense is the level line. A floor with no bumps, a scale with no tilt, a sea with no waves, a life with no peaks: each is 平, something brought to an even plane where nothing stands out of place.

平面 píngmiàn Flat & Level
平面 píngmiàn flat surface; plane; two-dimensional
N 名词 míngcí
平 píng (flat) + 面 miàn (surface; face). A flat surface, and in geometry a plane. Also the modifier in 平面设计 píngmiàn shèjì "graphic design," the flat, two-dimensional kind.
这张桌子的平面很光滑。
Zhè zhāng zhuōzi de píngmiàn hěn guānghuá.
The flat surface of this table is very smooth.
平地 píngdì level ground; flat land
N 名词 míngcí
平 píng (level) + 地 dì (ground). Flat, even land, as opposed to slopes or mountains: 一片平地 "a stretch of level ground." The opposite is hilly or broken terrain.
山下有一大片平地。
Shān xià yǒu yī dà piàn píngdì.
There is a large stretch of level ground at the foot of the mountain.
平稳 píngwěn steady; smooth; stable
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
平 píng (level) + 稳 wěn (steady; firm). Moving or sitting on an even keel, without jolts or swings: 平稳着陆 "a smooth landing," 物价平稳 "prices are stable."
飞机平稳地降落了。
Fēijī píngwěn de jiàngluò le.
The plane landed smoothly.
公平 gōngpíng Equal & Fair
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The fairness sense of 平 is the level line applied to people. A scale that hangs even, with neither pan dipping, is the oldest image of justice, and 平 carries it into a whole family of words about treating sides alike. 公平 gōngpíng pairs 平 with 公 gōng (public, impartial) for fairness in dealings and rules. 平等 píngděng pairs it with 等 děng (rank, grade) for equality of standing, the principle that no rank sits above another. 平均 píngjūn pairs it with 均 jūn (even) for the arithmetic average, the level a set of values would reach if shared out evenly.

The shared picture is the balanced scale. To be 公平 is to weigh without a thumb on either pan; to be 平等 is to stand on the same line as everyone else; to be 平均 is the number where all the weight evens out. Wherever 平 means fair, imagine the beam of a balance coming to rest dead level.

公平 gōngpíng fair; just; impartial
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
公 gōng (public; impartial) + 平 píng (level). Even-handed dealing that favors no side: 公平竞争 "fair competition," 这不公平 "that's not fair." The everyday word for fairness.
比赛的规则很公平。
Bǐsài de guīzé hěn gōngpíng.
The rules of the contest are very fair.
平等 píngděng equality; equal in status
ADJ / N 形容词 xíngróngcí
平 píng (level) + 等 děng (rank). Equal standing, no rank set above another: 人人平等 "everyone is equal," 男女平等 "equality of men and women." More about status than about fairness in dealings.
法律面前人人平等。
Fǎlǜ miànqián rénrén píngděng.
Everyone is equal before the law.
辨析 biànxī · 公平 vs. 平等 公平 is about even-handed treatment in a particular dealing, game, or rule, can it be called fair? 平等 is about equal standing as a matter of status or principle, do the parties rank the same? A contest can be 公平 between players who are not socially 平等, and people can be 平等 in law yet face outcomes that feel 不公平.
平均 píngjūn average; on average; to even out
ADJ / V 形容词 / 动词
平 píng (even) + 均 jūn (equal). The arithmetic mean, the level reached when a total is shared out evenly: 平均分 "average score," 平均年龄 "average age," 平均分配 "to distribute evenly."
学生的平均成绩很好。
Xuéshēng de píngjūn chéngjì hěn hǎo.
The students' average grades are very good.
和平 hépíng Peaceful & Ordinary
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

When the level surface is a sea, 平 means calm; when it is a life, 平 means ordinary. 和平 hépíng joins 和 hé (harmony) with 平 (calm) for peace, the absence of war and strife, the social water without waves. 平安 píngān joins 平 with 安 ān (safe; at ease) for being safe and sound, the wish travelers and families exchange. 平静 píngjìng joins 平 with 静 jìng (quiet) for an inner or outer calm.

The same flatness turns into plainness. 平常 píngcháng (ordinary, usual) and 平凡 píngfán (commonplace) describe what does not rise above the common level, the everyday rather than the remarkable. 水平 shuǐpíng, literally the level of water, names a standard or skill level, since still water finds one even line. Calm, ordinary, standard: each is the surface lying flat, with nothing breaking through.

和平 hépíng peace; peaceful
N / ADJ 名词 / 形容词
和 hé (harmony) + 平 píng (calm). Peace as the opposite of war and conflict: 世界和平 "world peace," 和平谈判 "peace talks," 和平相处 "to live together in peace."
大家都希望世界和平。
Dàjiā dōu xīwàng shìjiè hépíng.
Everyone hopes for world peace.
平安 píngān safe and sound; at peace; well
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
平 píng (calm) + 安 ān (safe). Safe and well, free of harm or trouble: 一路平安 "have a safe trip," 平安回家 "to get home safely." A staple of blessings and farewells.
祝你一路平安。
Zhù nǐ yīlù píngān.
Wishing you a safe journey.
平常 píngcháng ordinary; usual; usually
ADJ / ADV 形容词 / 副词
平 píng (plain) + 常 cháng (usual; constant). The ordinary and everyday, as a quality or as the usual state of things: 平常的一天 "an ordinary day," 我平常六点起床 "I usually get up at six."
他平常很少说话。
Tā píngcháng hěn shǎo shuōhuà.
He usually says very little.
辨析 biànxī · 平常 vs. 平凡 平常 means ordinary or usual and is neutral, it can describe a day, a habit, or a routine, often meaning "as a rule." 平凡 means commonplace or unremarkable and applies to people, lives, and work, carrying a quiet judgment that something is plain rather than great: 平凡的一生 "an ordinary, unremarkable life." You can do something 平常 (usually), but you would not do it 平凡.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
平易近人 píng yì jìn rén approachable and easy to get along with: unassuming and warm Lit: level-easy-near-people. Said of someone, often of high rank, who is modest and easy to approach rather than aloof. A leader who is 平易近人 puts ordinary people at ease.
风平浪静 fēng píng làng jìng the wind is calm and the waves are still: calm seas, a tranquil situation Lit: wind-level-waves-quiet. The sea at perfect rest, used both literally and for any situation that has settled into calm after trouble has passed.
平心静气 píng xīn jìng qì a level heart and quiet breath: calm and composed Lit: level-heart-quiet-breath. To approach something with a settled mind and even temper, neither agitated nor hasty. 平心静气地谈 "to talk it over calmly."
平分秋色 píng fēn qiū sè to split the autumn colors evenly: to share equally, to be evenly matched Lit: evenly-divide-autumn-scenery. Two sides each take half, as if dividing the beauty of autumn. Used when rivals are so closely matched that neither comes out ahead.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold one image: a level line with nothing rising above it. That flat line is the whole character. Everything 平 means is some surface brought even, with no bump, no tilt, no wave, no peak.

Lay the line on the floor and it is flat ground (平地, 平面). Lay it across a balance and it is fairness, neither pan dipping (公平, 平等, 平均). Lay it on the sea and it is calm and peace (和平, 平安, 平静). Lay it across a life and it is the ordinary, the everyday that rises no higher than the common level (平常, 平凡). Whenever you meet 平, picture the line settling level, and ask what surface it has just made even.
