字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

外 wài = 夕 (xī, evening) + 卜 (bǔ, divination). The left element 夕 is the pictograph of a crescent moon, marking dusk and the falling of night; the right element 卜 shows the crack that opens in a heated oracle bone or turtle shell, the sign the diviner reads. Divination was meant to be done in the morning, so to consult the shells in the evening was to act off the proper schedule, at an irregular, unsanctioned time. From that idea of "outside the norm" the character settles into its core meaning: outside, beyond, external.

From the boundary line, the senses fan out:

1. Outside, outer, exterior. Anything past the edge of a space: 外面 wàimiàn (outside), 户外 hùwài (outdoors), 国外 guówài (abroad), 外表 wàibiǎo (outward appearance).
2. Foreign, external. Outside one''s own country or group: 外国 wàiguó (foreign country), 外语 wàiyǔ (foreign language), 外卖 wàimài (takeout, food sent out from the kitchen).
3. Other, besides, in addition. Outside the matter already named: 另外 lìngwài (besides; another), 此外 cǐwài (apart from this), 除外 chúwài (to exclude).
4. Maternal relatives. Kin reckoned outside the patrilineal surname: 外婆 wàipó and 外公 wàigōng (maternal grandmother and grandfather), 外甥 wàishēng (sister''s son).

The thread through every sense is the line between in and out. Its constant partner and opposite is 里 lǐ (inside): wherever 外 marks what lies beyond, 里 marks what lies within, and the pair 里外 (inside and out) carries the whole of a space.

外面 wàimiàn Outside & Outer
外面 wàimiàn outside; the outside; outdoors
N 名词 míngcí
外 wài (outside) + 面 miàn (side; surface). The everyday word for the area beyond an enclosed space, or the outer surface of a thing. Its pair is 里面 lǐmiàn (inside).
外面在下雨。
Wàimiàn zài xià yǔ.
It''s raining outside.
他在外面等你。
Tā zài wàimiàn děng nǐ.
He''s waiting for you outside.
辨析 biànxī · 外 vs. 里 外 names everything beyond a boundary; 里 lǐ names everything within it. They are the basic inside/outside pair: 里面 (inside) and 外面 (outside), 里边 and 外边, 国内 (within the country) and 国外 (abroad). Pair them and you cover the whole of a space, as in 里里外外 "inside and out, every corner."
户外 hùwài outdoors; in the open air
N 名词 míngcí
户 hù (door; household) + 外 wài (outside). Literally "outside the door," the open air beyond a building. Common in 户外运动 "outdoor sports" and 户外活动 "outdoor activities."
他喜欢户外运动。
Tā xǐhuan hùwài yùndòng.
He likes outdoor sports.
外表 wàibiǎo outward appearance; exterior; looks
N 名词 míngcí
外 wài (outer) + 表 biǎo (surface). The outside as it appears to the eye, set against the inner reality: 外表 (the surface) versus 内心 nèixīn (the inner heart).
不要只看外表。
Bùyào zhǐ kàn wàibiǎo.
Don''t judge by appearances alone.
外国 wàiguó Foreign & Abroad
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Stretch "outside" past the wall of a house to the wall of a country, and 外 becomes "foreign." 外国 wàiguó is a "foreign country," literally an "outside country," and a person from one is an 外国人 wàiguórén. The same 外 marks anything that comes from beyond one''s own borders or circle: 外语 wàiyǔ (a foreign language), 外资 wàizī (foreign capital), 外交 wàijiāo (diplomacy, the management of relations with the outside).

The sense also covers what is simply sent out rather than kept in. 外卖 wàimài, now the everyday word for food delivery, is literally "sell-out," a meal dispatched beyond the kitchen rather than eaten in. In every case 外 draws a circle and points past its edge: outside the home, outside the nation, outside the kitchen door.

外国 wàiguó foreign country; abroad
N 名词 míngcí
外 wài (outside) + 国 guó (country). A country other than one''s own. A person from one is 外国人 wàiguórén (foreigner); the things of one are 外国 X, as in 外国文化 "foreign culture."
他想去外国留学。
Tā xiǎng qù wàiguó liúxué.
He wants to go abroad to study.
外语 wàiyǔ foreign language
N 名词 míngcí
外 wài (foreign) + 语 yǔ (language). A language other than one''s own. 学外语 "to study a foreign language," 外语学院 "a foreign-languages institute."
学外语需要时间。
Xué wàiyǔ xūyào shíjiān.
Learning a foreign language takes time.
外卖 wàimài takeout; food delivery
N 名词 míngcí
外 wài (out) + 卖 mài (to sell). Food sold to be taken away rather than eaten in. In modern usage it means app-based delivery: 点外卖 "to order takeout," 外卖员 "a delivery rider."
今天我们点外卖吧。
Jīntiān wǒmen diǎn wàimài ba.
Let''s order takeout today.
另外 lìngwài Other & Besides
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The third path 外 takes is from spatial "outside" to logical "besides." If something stands outside the matter just named, it is an addition or an exception. 另外 lìngwài (另 "another" + 外 "outside") means "besides, in addition, another": 另外一个 "another one," or as a connector, 另外，我还想说一件事 "besides, there''s one more thing I want to say." Its formal cousin 此外 cǐwài means "apart from this."

The same logic gives 例外 lìwài (an exception, what falls outside the rule) and 除外 chúwài (to leave out, to exclude). And 外 reaches into kinship the same way: relatives on the mother''s side stood outside the father''s surname line, so they are marked 外, giving 外婆 wàipó (maternal grandmother), 外公 wàigōng (maternal grandfather), and 外孙 wàisūn (a daughter''s son).

另外 lìngwài besides; in addition; another
ADV / ADJ 副词 fùcí
另 lìng (other) + 外 wài (outside). Either a connector ("besides, moreover") or a determiner ("another, a separate one"): 另外一个人 "another person," 另外一种方法 "a different method."
我还有另外一个问题。
Wǒ hái yǒu lìngwài yí ge wèntí.
I have another question as well.
此外 cǐwài apart from this; besides; furthermore
ADV 连词 liáncí
此 cǐ (this) + 外 wài (outside). A formal written connector, "outside of this," used to add a further point: 此外，还有一点需要注意 "besides, there is one more point to note."
此外，价格也很合理。
Cǐwài, jiàgé yě hěn hélǐ.
Besides, the price is also quite reasonable.
外婆 wàipó maternal grandmother (mother''s mother)
N 名词 míngcí
外 wài (outside the surname) + 婆 pó (old woman). One''s mother''s mother, marked 外 because she belongs to the maternal line. Its pair is 外公 wàigōng (maternal grandfather); the father''s parents are 奶奶 and 爷爷.
我小时候住在外婆家。
Wǒ xiǎo shíhou zhù zài wàipó jiā.
When I was little I lived at my grandmother''s house.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
喜出望外 xǐ chū wàng wài joy beyond what one hoped for: pleasantly surprised, overjoyed Lit: joy-comes-beyond-expectation. The good news lies 望外, outside the bounds of what one dared hope, so the delight is all the greater. Said when something turns out far better than expected.
置身事外 zhì shēn shì wài to place oneself outside the matter: to stay aloof, keep clear of it Lit: place-body-affair-outside. To keep oneself out of an affair, neither helping nor interfering. Often mildly critical, of someone who stands apart when they ought to be involved.
内外有别 nèi wài yǒu bié inside and outside are distinct: insiders and outsiders are treated differently Lit: inside-outside-have-distinction. The principle that there is a proper line between those within a family, group, or nation and those without, and that the two are not handled the same way.
里应外合 lǐ yìng wài hé those inside respond, those outside coordinate: a pincer with help from within Lit: inside-respond-outside-join. A coordinated attack in which an attacker outside and an accomplice inside act together. The inside/outside pair 里 and 外 set in tactical partnership.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture the diviner at dusk, 夕, cracking the shell, 卜, at the wrong hour, off the proper morning schedule. That single image, "doing it outside the normal time," is the whole character: outside, beyond, off the inside track.

Then draw a circle and step past its edge. Past the wall of the house, 外 is the outdoors (外面, 户外). Past the wall of the country, it is the foreign (外国, 外语). Past the kitchen door, it is the takeout (外卖). Past the matter at hand, it is the addition (另外, 此外). Past the father''s surname line, it is the mother''s kin (外婆, 外公). Whatever the circle, 外 is always the step beyond it, and its partner 里 is the step back in.
