字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

样 yàng = 木 (mù, wood) + 羊 (yáng, the phonetic). It is a pictophonetic compound: 木 on the left once supplied a meaning tied to wood (the traditional form 樣 originally named a kind of oak and its acorn), while 羊 on the right carries the sound yáng to yàng. The old botanical sense has fallen away, and only the pictophonetic frame survives in the modern character.

What the word came to mean is appearance and kind. A 样 is the look or form of a thing, and from that one idea three senses branch:

1. Shape, appearance, manner. How something looks or presents itself: 样子 yàngzi (appearance, manner), 模样 múyàng (looks, the cast of a face).
2. Kind, type, pattern. One sort among many: 一样 yíyàng (the same kind, alike), 样式 yàngshì (style, pattern), 各种各样 gè zhǒng gè yàng (all kinds).
3. Sample, model. One specimen held up to stand for the rest: 样品 yàngpǐn (a sample), 榜样 bǎngyàng (a role model, a thing to copy).

From these come the busiest 样 words of all, the manner words that point at how something is done: 这样 zhèyàng (this way), 那样 nàyàng (that way), 怎样 zěnyàng (in what way, how). The thread throughout is form and likeness: the look a thing has, the type it belongs to, and the model it is measured against.

一样 yíyàng Same & Kind
一样 yíyàng the same; alike; just like
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
一 yī (one) + 样 yàng (kind). Of one and the same kind. The everyday word for "the same": 我们的想法一样 "we think alike." In the frame 像…一样 it means "just like": 像水一样清 "as clear as water."
这两件衣服一样吗？
Zhè liǎng jiàn yīfu yíyàng ma?
Are these two pieces of clothing the same?
他跑得像风一样快。
Tā pǎo de xiàng fēng yíyàng kuài.
He runs as fast as the wind.
辨析 biànxī · 一样 vs. 同样 一样 is a predicate adjective: it says two things are alike (A 和 B 一样). 同样 tóngyàng is attributive or adverbial: it modifies a following noun or clause to mean "the same kind of" or "likewise" (同样的问题 "the same problem," 同样, 他也反对 "likewise, he too objects").
同样 tóngyàng same; alike; likewise
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
同 tóng (same) + 样 yàng (kind). Of the same kind, used before a noun (同样的方法 "the same method") or as a sentence adverb meaning "in the same way, likewise."
我们用同样的方法解决问题。
Wǒmen yòng tóngyàng de fāngfǎ jiějué wèntí.
We solve the problem using the same method.
样子 yàngzi appearance; look; manner; the way it seems
N 名词 míngcí
样 yàng (appearance) + 子 zi (noun-suffix). The look or manner of a person or thing. The idiom 看样子 kàn yàngzi means "from the looks of it." For the toneless suffix 子, see the 子 entry.
看样子今天会下雨。
Kàn yàngzi jīntiān huì xià yǔ.
From the looks of it, it will rain today.
这样 zhèyàng Manner Words: This Way and How
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Some of the highest-frequency words in spoken Chinese are 样 manner words, built by fixing a pointing word to 样. 这样 zhèyàng is "this way, like this"; 那样 nàyàng is "that way, like that"; 怎样 zěnyàng is "in what way, how." Each names not a thing but a manner, the how of an action or the form of a state: 别这样 "don't be like this," 事情怎样了 "how did things turn out."

怎样 overlaps heavily with 怎么 zěnme. Both ask "how," but 怎样 leans a little more formal and tends to ask after manner or outcome (你怎样 "how are you doing"), while 怎么 is the workaday "how" of method and reason (怎么做 "how to do it," 怎么了 "what is the matter"). In casual speech they are often interchangeable, and 这样 / 那样 likewise pair with the shorter 这么 / 那么 for degree.

这样 zhèyàng like this; this way; so; in this manner
PRON 代词 dàicí
这 zhè (this) + 样 yàng (manner). Points at a way of being or doing close at hand: 这样做 "do it like this," 就这样吧 "let it be this way, then."
你为什么这样想？
Nǐ wèishénme zhèyàng xiǎng?
Why do you think this way?
怎样 zěnyàng how; in what way; what about
PRON 代词 dàicí
怎 zěn (how) + 样 yàng (manner). Asks after the manner or outcome of something: 这件事你打算怎样处理 "how do you plan to handle this?" Close to 怎么 zěnme, slightly more formal.
最近你过得怎样？
Zuìjìn nǐ guò de zěnyàng?
How have you been lately?
样品 yàngpǐn Sample & Model
样品 yàngpǐn sample; specimen (of a product)
N 名词 míngcí
样 yàng (sample) + 品 pǐn (article, product). One specimen held up to represent a whole batch: a free sample, a trade-show display, a test piece sent to a buyer.
请先给我们看看样品。
Qǐng xiān gěi wǒmen kànkan yàngpǐn.
Please show us a sample first.
榜样 bǎngyàng role model; good example to follow
N 名词 míngcí
榜 bǎng (a posted list, a board) + 样 yàng (model). A person held up as a pattern for others: 树立榜样 "to set an example," 学习的榜样 "a model to learn from."
老师是学生的榜样。
Lǎoshī shì xuésheng de bǎngyàng.
A teacher is a role model for students.
花样 huāyàng pattern; variety; (figurative) trick, gimmick
N 名词 míngcí
花 huā (flower; fancy) + 样 yàng (pattern). A decorative pattern, and by extension a clever variation or a ploy: 玩花样 "to play tricks," 花样滑冰 "figure skating."
他总是想出各种花样。
Tā zǒngshì xiǎng chū gèzhǒng huāyàng.
He's always coming up with all sorts of tricks.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
各种各样 gè zhǒng gè yàng every kind and every sort: all manner of things Lit: each-kind-each-sort. The standard everyday phrase for "all kinds of," stressing variety: 各种各样的人 "all sorts of people," 各种各样的问题 "every kind of problem."
一模一样 yì mú yí yàng cast from one mould: exactly alike, identical Lit: one-mould-one-shape. Said of two things so alike they might have come from a single cast: 这对双胞胎长得一模一样 "the twins look exactly alike."
装模作样 zhuāng mú zuò yàng to fake a shape and put on a show: to playact, to put on airs Lit: feign-mould-make-shape. To strike a false pose, going through the motions of sincerity or effort while meaning none of it. A pointed way to call out pretense.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

The wood radical 木 on the left is a faint memory of the character's origin in a kind of tree, but let it go: what 样 means now is the look and kind of a thing. Picture a single sample on a shelf, held up to show what the whole batch is like. That is 样品, and from it everything else follows.

Ask of any 样 word what aspect of "form" it points at. Two things sharing a form are 一样 (the same). A form worth copying is a 榜样 (role model). The form an action takes is its manner: 这样 (this way), 那样 (that way), 怎样 (which way, how). Whenever you meet 样, you are being shown the shape or kind of something.
