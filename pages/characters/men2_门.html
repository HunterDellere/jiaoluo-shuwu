字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

门 mén is a pictograph of a two-leaf gate. The traditional form 門 shows it plainly: two door-panels hung side by side, each with its post and pivot, the kind of double door that closed the entrance to a courtyard or a city ward. The simplified 门 keeps one stylized leaf with its hinge. Either way the glyph is the gate, and 门 serves as a radical in a whole family of door-and-enclosure characters: 间 jiān (the space within a gate), 问 wèn (to ask, mouth at the gate), 闭 bì (to shut).

From the swinging doors, four senses open out:

1. Door, gate, entrance. The literal opening: 门口 ménkǒu (doorway), 大门 dàmén (the main gate), 出门 chūmén (to go out).
2. Family, house, school. Who lives or studies behind the door: 家门 jiāmén (one's family), and by extension a lineage or a master's following.
3. Division, category, field. A doorway into a body of knowledge or work: 部门 bùmén (department), 专门 zhuānmén (specialized), 入门 rùmén (to get started, "enter the door").
4. A measure word. For things grasped as whole units entered through a doorway: 一门课 yì mén kè (a course of study), 一门技术 (a skill), 一门大炮 (a cannon).

The thread is the threshold. A gate is the point where inside meets outside, and so 门 marks every kind of entry: into a building, into a family, into a trade, into a discipline. To learn the basics of anything is 入门, to step through its door.

门口 ménkǒu Door & Gate
门口 ménkǒu doorway; entrance
N 名词 míngcí
门 mén (door) + 口 kǒu (mouth; opening). The mouth of the door, the spot just at the entrance: 在门口等 "to wait at the door," 学校门口 "the school gate."
我在门口等你。
Wǒ zài ménkǒu děng nǐ.
I'll wait for you at the door.
出门 chūmén to go out; to leave home; to set off on a trip
V 动词 dòngcí
出 chū (to exit) + 门 mén (door). To step out the door, whether for the day or for a journey: 我出门了 "I'm heading out." Its opposite is 回家 huí jiā or 进门 jìnmén.
他每天七点出门。
Tā měitiān qī diǎn chūmén.
He leaves the house at seven every day.
开门 kāimén to open the door; (of a shop) to open for business
V 动词 dòngcí
开 kāi (to open) + 门 mén (door). To open a door, and idiomatically for a shop to open: 商店几点开门？ "What time does the shop open?" Its pair is 关门 guānmén, to close.
银行九点开门。
Yínháng jiǔ diǎn kāimén.
The bank opens at nine.
部门 bùmén Household & Division
部门 bùmén department; division; branch
N 名词 míngcí
部 bù (section) + 门 mén (gateway; division). A division of an organization, each entered through its own door: 销售部门 "the sales department," 政府部门 "government departments."
这件事要问别的部门。
Zhè jiàn shì yào wèn bié de bùmén.
You'll have to ask another department about this.
门票 ménpiào entrance ticket; admission
N 名词 míngcí
门 mén (gate) + 票 piào (ticket). The ticket that gets you through the gate, for a park, museum, concert, or attraction: 门票多少钱？ "How much is admission?"
公园的门票不贵。
Gōngyuán de ménpiào bú guì.
The park admission isn't expensive.
专门 zhuānmén Field & Specialty
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

A body of knowledge or skill is imagined in Chinese as a building you enter through a door, and 门 carries that metaphor. To begin learning a subject is 入门 rùmén, literally "to enter the door": a 入门教程 is a beginner's tutorial. To devote yourself to one field is 专门 zhuānmén, "specialized," concentrating on a single doorway rather than wandering many. And each whole discipline can be counted with 门 as its measure word: 一门科学 "a science," 一门外语 "a foreign language," 一门手艺 "a craft."

The image is worth holding onto, because it shapes how Chinese talks about expertise. Mastery is not a flat accumulation of facts but a series of thresholds crossed. You stand outside a field, you 入门 across its doorstep, and only then do you begin to move through its rooms.

专门 zhuānmén specialized; specially; on purpose
ADJ/ADV 兼类 jiānlèi
专 zhuān (focused; sole) + 门 mén (field). Concentrated on one doorway. As an adjective, specialized: 专门知识 "specialized knowledge." As an adverb, specially or on purpose: 我专门来看你 "I came specially to see you."
他是专门研究历史的。
Tā shì zhuānmén yánjiū lìshǐ de.
He specializes in studying history.
入门 rùmén to get started; to cross the threshold of a subject; a primer
V 动词 dòngcí
入 rù (to enter) + 门 mén (door). To step across the doorway of a field, to grasp its basics. A book titled 《汉语入门》 is "Chinese: A Primer," a doorway into the language.
学了三个月，我才算入门。
Xué le sān ge yuè, wǒ cái suàn rùmén.
After three months of study, I'd only just gotten started.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
门当户对 mén dāng hù duì matched gates and doors: of equal social standing (for a marriage) Lit: gate-matching-door-matching. The old principle that the two families in a marriage should be of comparable rank and wealth. Still used, sometimes critically, of pairing by status.
门庭若市 mén tíng ruò shì the gateway like a marketplace: thronged with visitors Lit: gate-courtyard-like-market. Said of a household or office so busy with callers that the entrance resembles a crowded market. Its opposite is 门可罗雀, a gate so deserted you could net sparrows in it.
班门弄斧 bān mén nòng fǔ to wield an axe at Lu Ban's gate: to show off before a true master Lit: at-Ban's-gate-brandish-axe. 鲁班 Lǔ Bān was the legendary master carpenter. To display one's small skill in front of an expert is to swing an axe at the carpenter-god's door, a self-deprecating apology or a mockery of presumption.
独门绝技 dú mén jué jì a unique school's matchless skill: a signature technique known to no one else Lit: sole-school-unmatched-skill. 门 here is a school or lineage of practice. A 独门绝技 is the secret art that belongs to one tradition or master alone.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

See the two leaves of a gate swinging on their posts. That is the whole character, and every meaning is something passing through it. Step through and you are at the doorway (门口), heading out (出门) or opening up for the day (开门). The people behind the gate are the household and, by extension, a division of an organization (部门).

Then make the gate the entrance to a body of knowledge. To learn the basics is to 入门, to cross its threshold; to specialize is 专门, to commit to one doorway; and each whole discipline is counted as 一门, one door entered. Wherever inside meets outside, 门 is the point of passage.
