字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

合 hé pictures a lid coming down over the mouth of a vessel. The top element is 亼 (jí), an inverted, roof-like shape read as a cover, and below it sits 口 (kǒu), the round opening of a container drawn as a mouth. Set one over the other and you see a lid settling onto a jar so that the two edges meet and seal. The early sense is exactly that: two parts brought together so they fit and close. The simplified and traditional forms are identical; there is no separate traditional glyph.

From that one image of edges meeting, three related senses spread across thousands of words:

1. To join, to combine. Two things brought into one: 合作 hézuò (to cooperate), 结合 jiéhé (to combine; to integrate), 联合 liánhé (to unite), 合并 hébìng (to merge).
2. To suit, to match, to fit. When the lid sits true on the jar, the parts agree: 合适 héshì (suitable), 适合 shìhé (to suit), 符合 fúhé (to conform to), 合理 hélǐ (reasonable).
3. To close; the whole. The closing motion itself, and the sum it produces: 合上 héshàng (to close up), 合眼 héyǎn (to close the eyes), 合计 héjì (to total up).

The thread running through every sense is two edges meeting cleanly. Whether the parts are people (cooperation), measurements (a good fit), an argument (reasonable), or eyelids (shutting), 合 is always the moment when separate things come together and agree.

合作 hézuò Join & Cooperate
合作 hézuò to cooperate; to collaborate; cooperation
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
合 hé (to join) + 作 zuò (to do; to work). To join efforts and work as one. The everyday word for partnership of every scale, from two classmates to two nations: 合作伙伴 "a cooperation partner."
我们公司想和你们合作。
Wǒmen gōngsī xiǎng hé nǐmen hézuò.
Our company would like to cooperate with you.
谢谢你的合作。
Xièxie nǐ de hézuò.
Thank you for your cooperation.
结合 jiéhé to combine; to integrate; to unite
V 动词 dòngcí
结 jié (to tie; to knot) + 合 hé (to join). To bind two things into one so they work together: 理论和实践相结合 "to combine theory with practice." Stronger and more structural than a loose pairing.
把学习和工作结合起来。
Bǎ xuéxí hé gōngzuò jiéhé qǐlái.
Combine study with work.
联合 liánhé to unite; to join together; joint; united
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
联 lián (to link; to connect) + 合 hé (to join). To link separate parties into a single front: 联合国 "the United Nations," 联合声明 "a joint statement." The uniting is formal and often institutional.
两家公司联合开发新产品。
Liǎng jiā gōngsī liánhé kāifā xīn chǎnpǐn.
The two companies jointly develop a new product.
合适 héshì Suit & Fit
合适 héshì suitable; appropriate; fitting
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
合 hé (to fit) + 适 shì (to be apt). An adjective describing something that fits the situation well: 合适的工作 "a suitable job," 大小合适 "the right size." Its opposite is 不合适.
这件衣服很合适。
Zhè jiàn yīfu hěn héshì.
This piece of clothing fits well.
辨析 biànxī · 合适 vs. 适合 合适 is an adjective: it stands on its own to say a thing is suitable (这个时间很合适 "this time is suitable"). 适合 is a verb: it takes an object to say one thing suits another (这个时间适合开会 "this time suits a meeting"). Same two characters, reversed order, different grammar.
适合 shìhé to suit; to be suited to; to fit
V 动词 dòngcí
适 shì (to be apt) + 合 hé (to fit). A verb taking an object: X 适合 Y means X suits Y. 这本书适合孩子 "this book is suited to children."
这个工作很适合你。
Zhège gōngzuò hěn shìhé nǐ.
This job suits you well.
符合 fúhé to conform to; to accord with; to match
V 动词 dòngcí
符 fú (a tally; to tally) + 合 hé (to fit). To match a standard or expectation exactly, the way two halves of an ancient tally fit: 符合规定 "to conform to the rules," 符合条件 "to meet the conditions."
这个结果符合我们的预期。
Zhège jiéguǒ fúhé wǒmen de yùqī.
This result matches our expectations.
合理 hélǐ reasonable; rational; sound
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
合 hé (to accord with) + 理 lǐ (reason; principle). Something that accords with reason or fairness: 合理的价格 "a reasonable price," 合理安排 "a sensible arrangement." Its opposite is 不合理.
他的要求很合理。
Tā de yāoqiú hěn hélǐ.
His request is quite reasonable.
合并 hébìng Close & Merge into a Whole
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The most literal sense of 合 is the physical act of closing: bringing two edges together until they meet. 合上 héshàng means to close something that opens along a seam, a book or a lid or a door: 合上书 "to close the book." 合眼 héyǎn is to close the eyes, often a gentle way of saying to fall asleep or, in mourning, to pass away peacefully.

From closing comes merging, and from merging comes the idea of a whole made of parts. 合并 hébìng joins two separate units into one: two companies, two departments, two cells. 综合 zōnghé gathers many strands into a single comprehensive view. Even 合唱 héchàng, a chorus, is voices closed together into one sound. Across all of these, 合 carries the same motion the etymology shows: parts drawn in until they form a single, sealed whole.

合上 héshàng to close (a book, lid, door); to shut
V 动词 dòngcí
合 hé (to close) + 上 shàng (a result complement: shut to). To bring two edges together so a thing is closed: 合上书 "close the book," 合上盖子 "put the lid on."
请合上你的书。
Qǐng héshàng nǐ de shū.
Please close your book.
合并 hébìng to merge; to combine; to amalgamate
V 动词 dòngcí
合 hé (to join) + 并 bìng (to combine side by side). To merge separate units into one: 两个部门合并了 "the two departments merged." Common in business, administration, and biology.
这两家银行合并成了一家。
Zhè liǎng jiā yínháng hébìng chéng le yī jiā.
These two banks merged into one.
综合 zōnghé to synthesize; comprehensive; integrated
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
综 zōng (to gather; to sum) + 合 hé (to join). To draw many parts into one comprehensive whole: 综合考虑 "to consider comprehensively," 综合大学 "a comprehensive university."
我们需要综合考虑各种因素。
Wǒmen xūyào zōnghé kǎolǜ gè zhǒng yīnsù.
We need to consider all the factors together.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
合情合理 hé qíng hé lǐ in keeping with feeling and reason: fair and reasonable Lit: accords-with-feeling, accords-with-reason. Said of a decision or request that satisfies both human sentiment and good sense, so no one can fault it. The doubled 合 stresses that it fits on every count.
志同道合 zhì tóng dào hé shared aims and a common path: kindred spirits Lit: ambitions-the-same, the-way-fits. Said of people whose goals and convictions align so well that they make natural partners or friends. A warm phrase for genuine like-mindedness.
天作之合 tiān zuò zhī hé a union made by Heaven: a match made in heaven Lit: Heaven-made union. A classical blessing for a marriage, suggesting the couple was destined to come together. Still used today on wedding scrolls and congratulations.
不谋而合 bù móu ér hé to agree without prior consultation: to coincide spontaneously Lit: without-conferring yet-agree. Said when two people arrive at the same view or plan independently, their thoughts fitting together though they never discussed it.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

See a lid, 亼, settling down onto the mouth of a jar, 口, until the two rims meet and seal. That single closing motion is the whole character. Everything 合 means is parts coming together until they fit.

When the parts are people, the meeting is cooperation (合作). When the parts are a thing and its setting, it is a good fit (合适). When the parts are a claim and a standard, it is conformity (符合). When the parts are two units, it is a merger (合并). And when the parts are simply two edges of a book, it is the plain act of closing (合上). Whenever you meet 合, ask what two things are being brought together, and you will have its sense.
