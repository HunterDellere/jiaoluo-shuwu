字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

业 yè began as a pictograph of a notched wooden board. The traditional form 業 shows a serrated rack or crossbeam, the toothed frame from which ancient bells and stone chimes were hung in a ceremonial hall. The notches gripped the cords; the board held the instruments. From that concrete object the word moved outward, first to the work mounted on the frame, then to any task, undertaking, or line of work a person takes up.

The simplified 业 keeps the toothed silhouette in five strokes, the upright teeth above a base line. It is classed under the 一 (one) radical, the horizontal stroke at the foot of the frame, a purely structural placement rather than a meaning component.

From the rack of hung work, several senses fan out:

1. Occupation, trade. The line of work one practices: 职业 zhíyè (profession), 行业 hángyè (trade, line of business), 失业 shīyè (to lose one's job).
2. Industry, business. A whole sector of production: 工业 gōngyè (industry), 农业 nóngyè (agriculture), 商业 shāngyè (commerce), 企业 qǐyè (enterprise).
3. An undertaking, a cause. The life's work one builds: 事业 shìyè (career, cause), 创业 chuàngyè (to found a venture).
4. Study, coursework. The work of learning: 作业 zuòyè (homework), 毕业 bìyè (to graduate), 专业 zhuānyè (major, specialty).
5. Karma (Buddhist). The accumulated weight of one's deeds: 业 as the cause that ripens into consequence, seen in 业障 yèzhàng (the hindrance of past karma).

The thread is work that is taken up and carried. Whether the work is a trade, a sector, a lifelong cause, a course of study, or the moral residue of deeds, 业 names the labor that hangs on the frame of a life.

职业 zhíyè Occupation & Industry
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The most everyday face of 业 is the work one does for a living and the larger field that work belongs to. At the personal scale it is the trade or profession: 职业 zhíyè (one's occupation), 行业 hángyè (the line of business). At the scale of the whole economy it names the great sectors: 工业 gōngyè (industry), 农业 nóngyè (agriculture), 商业 shāngyè (commerce). The same syllable that marks a single person's job also labels an entire branch of national production, the work seen up close and the work seen from above.

职业 zhíyè occupation; profession; vocational
N 名词 míngcí
职 zhí (post; duty) + 业 yè (work). The line of work a person practices for a living: 职业选择 "choice of profession." As a modifier it means professional or vocational: 职业运动员 "professional athlete," 职业学校 "vocational school."
请问您的职业是什么？
Qǐngwèn nín de zhíyè shì shénme?
May I ask what your occupation is?
他是一名职业医生。
Tā shì yī míng zhíyè yīshēng.
He is a professional doctor.
行业 hángyè trade; industry; line of business
N 名词 míngcí
行 háng (trade; row) + 业 yè (work). A branch of business or a field of trade: 服务行业 "the service trade," 各行各业 "all walks of life." Note that 行 here reads háng, not the more common xíng.
他在金融行业工作。
Tā zài jīnróng hángyè gōngzuò.
He works in the finance industry.
工业 gōngyè industry; the industrial sector
N 名词 míngcí
工 gōng (labor; work) + 业 yè (sector). Manufacturing industry as a branch of the economy: 重工业 "heavy industry," 工业化 "industrialization." Paired with 农业 (agriculture) in the standard rhetoric of national development.
这座城市以工业为主。
Zhè zuò chéngshì yǐ gōngyè wéi zhǔ.
This city is mainly industrial.
辨析 biànxī · 职业 vs. 专业 职业 is the job one does for a living, the occupation itself. 专业 zhuānyè is the field of study or specialty one trained in. They often differ: someone whose 专业 was history may end up with a 职业 in sales. One names what you studied, the other what you do.
农业 nóngyè agriculture; farming
N 名词 míngcí
农 nóng (farming) + 业 yè (sector). The agricultural sector: 农业生产 "agricultural production," 农业大国 "a major agricultural country." The oldest of the great 业 in Chinese economic thought, long held as the root on which all else rests.
农业是国家的基础。
Nóngyè shì guójiā de jīchǔ.
Agriculture is the foundation of the nation.
企业 qǐyè Enterprise & Undertaking
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

When the work is not a job but a thing one builds and sustains, 业 shades into the sense of an undertaking. 企业 qǐyè is the firm or enterprise as an organized body; 事业 shìyè is the cause or career one devotes a life to, weightier and more idealistic than a mere job; 创业 chuàngyè is the act of founding a venture from nothing. Here 业 carries ambition: not the labor one is assigned, but the labor one chooses to raise up and stand behind.

企业 qǐyè enterprise; firm; company (as an organization)
N 名词 míngcí
企 qǐ (to stand on tiptoe; to aspire) + 业 yè (undertaking). A business organization: 国有企业 "state-owned enterprise," 私营企业 "private enterprise," 企业家 "entrepreneur." The standard term for a firm as a legal and economic body.
这家企业有上千名员工。
Zhè jiā qǐyè yǒu shàng qiān míng yuángōng.
This enterprise has over a thousand employees.
事业 shìyè cause; career; lifework; undertaking
N 名词 míngcí
事 shì (matter; affair) + 业 yè (undertaking). The cause or career one commits to, larger and more aspirational than a job: 事业心 "career ambition," 教育事业 "the cause of education." To 干一番事业 is to accomplish something lasting with one's life.
他把一生献给了科学事业。
Tā bǎ yīshēng xiàn gěi le kēxué shìyè.
He devoted his whole life to the cause of science.
辨析 biànxī · 事业 vs. 职业 职业 is the occupation one holds, the job that pays. 事业 is the larger cause one pursues, often carrying a sense of purpose and lasting worth. A 职业 can be left behind at the end of the day; a 事业 is something one builds across a lifetime.
创业 chuàngyè to start a business; to found a venture; to pioneer
V 动词 dòngcí
创 chuàng (to create; to initiate) + 业 yè (undertaking). To found something from the ground up, whether a company or a larger enterprise: 自主创业 "to start one's own business," 创业公司 "a startup." Carries the flavor of building where nothing stood before.
他大学毕业后就开始创业。
Tā dàxué bìyè hòu jiù kāishǐ chuàngyè.
He started his own business right after graduating from university.
学业 xuéyè Study & Coursework
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

Long before it meant a career, 业 named the work of a student. In the classical academy the 业 was the lesson or course one received and recited, and that sense survives in modern schooling. 作业 zuòyè is the homework one is set; 专业 zhuānyè is the major one specializes in; 毕业 bìyè is the moment one has finished the whole course of study, literally "to complete the work." School is, in this old framing, simply another rack of work to be taken up, carried, and at last laid down.

作业 zuòyè homework; assignment; (work) operation
N 名词 míngcí
作 zuò (to do; to make) + 业 yè (coursework). The schoolwork a student is assigned: 做作业 "to do homework," 家庭作业 "home assignment." In technical contexts it also means a work operation, as in 水下作业 "underwater operations."
老师布置了很多作业。
Lǎoshī bùzhì le hěn duō zuòyè.
The teacher assigned a lot of homework.
毕业 bìyè to graduate; graduation
V 动词 dòngcí
毕 bì (to finish; to complete) + 业 yè (studies). To complete one's course of study: 大学毕业 "to graduate from university," 毕业典礼 "graduation ceremony." A separable verb, so the object slots in the middle: 毕了业 "have graduated."
她去年从北京大学毕业。
Tā qùnián cóng Běijīng Dàxué bìyè.
She graduated from Peking University last year.
专业 zhuānyè major; specialty; professional, specialized
N/ADJ 名词 míngcí
专 zhuān (to specialize; sole) + 业 yè (field of study). As a noun, the major one studies: 我的专业是历史 "my major is history." As an adjective, professional or specialized: 专业知识 "specialized knowledge," 很专业 "very professional."
你大学学的是什么专业？
Nǐ dàxué xué de shì shénme zhuānyè?
What was your major in university?
学业 xuéyè studies; schoolwork; academic work
N 名词 míngcí
学 xué (to learn) + 业 yè (course of work). One's studies taken as a whole: 完成学业 "to complete one's studies," 学业成绩 "academic results." The general term for the body of work a student carries.
他的学业成绩很优秀。
Tā de xuéyè chéngjì hěn yōuxiù.
His academic results are excellent.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
安居乐业 ān jū lè yè to live in peace and be content in one's work: a settled, secure life Lit: peaceful-dwelling, joyful-work. 安居 is to live securely, 乐业 to take pleasure in one's occupation. The classic image of social well-being: a people housed, employed, and at ease. Often invoked as the aim of good governance.
兢兢业业 jīng jīng yè yè cautious and conscientious: diligent and painstaking in one's work 兢兢 is the look of careful, anxious attentiveness; 业业 the steady devotion to one's task. Said of someone who works with unflagging diligence and care, never cutting a corner. A standard term of praise for a dedicated worker.
业精于勤 yè jīng yú qín mastery of one's work comes from diligence: skill is born of effort Lit: work, refined-through, diligence. From Han Yu's essay on study: 业精于勤，荒于嬉, "mastery comes from diligence and is ruined by play." A maxim that one's craft or learning is perfected only through steady, hard application.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

See a toothed wooden rack standing in a hall, bells and chimes hung along its notches, waiting to be played. That frame of mounted work is the whole character. Everything 业 means is some kind of work hung upon a frame and carried.

Hang a single trade on it and it is occupation (职业, 行业). Hang a whole sector of the economy and it is industry (工业, 农业, 商业). Hang the cause of a lifetime and it is an undertaking, the venture one stands behind (企业, 事业, 创业). Hang the lessons of a student and it is study, the coursework taken up and at last completed (作业, 毕业, 专业). And in Buddhist thought, hang the residue of one's deeds and it is karma, the work that ripens into consequence. Whenever you meet 业, ask what work is hanging on the frame.
