字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

法 fǎ = 氵 (shuǐ, water) + 去 (qù, to go). The simplified and traditional forms are identical here; the modern shape is the streamlined version of a longer archaic graph. The water radical on the left is the key to the old meaning. A water surface always settles dead level, and that level is the natural emblem of fairness: the law as the line everyone is measured against, with no side standing higher than another. The right element 去 carries the sense of going forth, the law flowing out evenly to all. So the character pairs water as a standard of levelness with 去 as the going-out of that standard.

From this image of level, flowing measure, four senses spread across thousands of words:

1. Law, statute. The rule that binds everyone equally: 法律 fǎlǜ (law), 法院 fǎyuàn (court of law), 合法 héfǎ (lawful), 违法 wéifǎ (to break the law).
2. Method, way of doing. The settled procedure for a task: 方法 fāngfǎ (method), 办法 bànfǎ (a way; a solution), 用法 yòngfǎ (usage; how a thing is used).
3. The Buddhist Dharma. The teaching and the truth it points to: 佛法 Fófǎ (the Buddhist teaching), 法师 fǎshī (a Dharma master).
4. France, in proper names. Chosen for its sound: 法国 Fǎguó (France), 法语 Fǎyǔ (the French language).

The thread running through every sense is a fixed standard you measure against. Whether the standard is the statute, the proven method, or the Dharma, 法 is the level line that water finds on its own, the rule that holds true wherever it is laid.

法律 fǎlǜ Law & Justice
法律 fǎlǜ law; statute; legislation
N 名词 míngcí
法 fǎ (law) + 律 lǜ (rule; statute). The body of law as a system, the formal rules a state enacts and enforces. 遵守法律 "to obey the law," 法律问题 "a legal question."
每个人都要遵守法律。
Měi ge rén dōu yào zūnshǒu fǎlǜ.
Everyone must obey the law.
这是一个法律问题。
Zhè shì yí ge fǎlǜ wèntí.
This is a legal question.
法院 fǎyuàn court of law; courthouse
N 名词 míngcí
法 fǎ (law) + 院 yuàn (institution; courtyard). The court where law is applied and cases are judged. 人民法院 "the People's Court," 上法院 "to go to court."
他们决定去法院解决这件事。
Tāmen juédìng qù fǎyuàn jiějué zhè jiàn shì.
They decided to settle the matter in court.
合法 héfǎ lawful; legal; legitimate
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
合 hé (to fit; to accord with) + 法 fǎ (law). In accordance with the law, on the right side of the line: 合法权利 "lawful rights." Its opposite is 非法 fēifǎ (illegal) or 违法 wéifǎ (to break the law).
这种做法完全合法。
Zhè zhǒng zuòfǎ wánquán héfǎ.
This practice is entirely legal.
违法 wéifǎ to break the law; to act illegally
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
违 wéi (to violate; to go against) + 法 fǎ (law). To step over the line the law draws: 违法行为 "an unlawful act." The mirror image of 合法, the foot landing outside the level mark.
酒后开车是违法的。
Jiǔ hòu kāichē shì wéifǎ de.
Driving after drinking is against the law.
方法 fāngfǎ Method & Means
方法 fāngfǎ method; means; way of doing something
N 名词 míngcí
方 fāng (square; direction) + 法 fǎ (way). A method or procedure considered in the abstract, the systematic way to approach a task: 学习方法 "study methods," 科学方法 "the scientific method."
这个方法很有效。
Zhège fāngfǎ hěn yǒuxiào.
This method is very effective.
辨析 biànxī · 方法 vs. 办法 方法 is the method in the abstract, the systematic procedure or technique for doing something well, often taught or studied. 办法 is the practical way out of a concrete problem, the solution you reach for here and now. You study a 学习方法 (study method), but when stuck you ask 有什么办法 (is there any way / what can be done). 方法 leans toward technique; 办法 leans toward a fix.
办法 bànfǎ a way; a means; a solution
N 名词 míngcí
办 bàn (to handle; to manage) + 法 fǎ (way). The practical way to get a concrete thing done or to solve a problem at hand: 想办法 "to think of a way," 没办法 "there's nothing to be done."
我们得想个办法。
Wǒmen děi xiǎng ge bànfǎ.
We have to think of a way.
没办法，只能等了。
Méi bànfǎ, zhǐ néng děng le.
Nothing to be done, we can only wait.
用法 yòngfǎ usage; how something is used
N 名词 míngcí
用 yòng (to use) + 法 fǎ (way). The way a thing is properly used, especially a word or a tool: 这个词的用法 "the usage of this word." Built on the same "way of doing" sense as 方法.
老师讲解了这个词的用法。
Lǎoshī jiǎngjiě le zhège cí de yòngfǎ.
The teacher explained the usage of this word.
语法 yǔfǎ grammar
N 名词 míngcí
语 yǔ (language; speech) + 法 fǎ (rule; law). The "law of language," the system of rules by which a language is built. The same 法 of fixed standard, here governing how words combine.
中文语法跟英文很不一样。
Zhōngwén yǔfǎ gēn Yīngwén hěn bù yíyàng.
Chinese grammar is very different from English.
佛法 Fófǎ The Dharma: the Buddhist 法
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

When Buddhism crossed into China, translators reached for 法 to render the Sanskrit dharma, and the fit was natural. Dharma means at once the teaching of the Buddha and the underlying truth or law of how things are, so the Chinese 法 with its sense of a fixed, impartial standard carried the idea cleanly. 佛法 Fófǎ is the body of Buddhist teaching; 说法 shuōfǎ is to expound the Dharma (and in everyday speech, a way of putting something); 法师 fǎshī is a master of the Dharma, a respectful title for a learned monk.

So the single glyph 法 spans a remarkable range. In a courtroom it is the statute. In a workshop it is the proven method. In a temple it is the cosmic law the Buddha awakened to. The common thread is the same standard that the water radical first set down: a truth that holds level and even, whether written in a legal code or realized in meditation.

佛法 Fófǎ the Buddhist teaching; the Dharma
N 名词 míngcí
佛 Fó (the Buddha) + 法 fǎ (Dharma; teaching). The whole of the Buddha's teaching and the truth it reveals: 弘扬佛法 "to spread the Dharma," 佛法无边 "the Dharma is boundless."
他研究佛法很多年了。
Tā yánjiū Fófǎ hěn duō nián le.
He has studied the Dharma for many years.
法师 fǎshī Dharma master; a learned monk or nun
N 名词 míngcí
法 fǎ (Dharma) + 师 shī (master; teacher). A respectful title for a monk or nun versed in the teaching, qualified to expound it to others. Used before or after a religious name.
法师为大家讲经。
Fǎshī wèi dàjiā jiǎng jīng.
The Dharma master expounded the sutra for everyone.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
知法犯法 zhī fǎ fàn fǎ to know the law and break it anyway: to offend with full awareness Lit: know-law-violate-law. Said of someone who understands exactly what the law forbids and breaks it regardless, which makes the offense worse, not more excusable.
执法如山 zhí fǎ rú shān to enforce the law like a mountain: to uphold it firmly and without bending Lit: enforce-law-like-mountain. Praise for an official or judge who applies the law unshakably, immovable as a mountain, favoring no one and yielding to no pressure.
想方设法 xiǎng fāng shè fǎ to try every method: to do everything possible to achieve something Lit: think-of-directions, set-up-methods. To exhaust every avenue and means in pursuit of a goal. Here 法 carries its "method" sense rather than "law."
法不容情 fǎ bù róng qíng the law allows no room for sentiment: justice must not bend to feeling Lit: law-not-tolerate-feeling. The principle that the law must be applied evenly, without softening for personal ties or sympathy. The level standard of the water radical made into a maxim.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture a still pool of water finding its own level, flat and even from edge to edge. That level surface is the heart of 法: a standard that holds true the same for everyone, with no side raised above another. The water radical 氵 on the left is the pool; the 去 on the right is that levelness going forth into the world.

From the one image, follow the senses out. Lay the level over a society and it becomes the law (法律), enforced in the court (法院). Lay it over a task and it becomes the proven method (方法), the way that reliably works. Lay it over the cosmos and it becomes the Dharma (佛法), the truth a Buddha awakens to. Whenever you meet 法, look for the standard being measured against, and you will find the level water beneath it.
