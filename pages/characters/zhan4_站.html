字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

站 zhàn = 立 (lì) + 占 (zhàn). It is a pictophonetic character (形声字). The left side, 立, is the semantic component: it pictures a person standing with feet apart on the ground, the line beneath the figure marking the earth they press into. The right side, 占 zhàn, is the phonetic component, supplying the sound. So the structure reads at a glance: a standing thing that sounds like 占.

The radical 立 is the whole logic of the character. On its own 立 already means to stand, to set up, to establish. Adding the phonetic 占 carved out a separate, more concrete word: to plant the body upright and hold that spot. Where 立 leans abstract (to found a state, to set up a rule), 站 stays physical: a body on its two feet, motionless.

From the standing body the meaning extends outward by a simple logic of stopping in place:

1. To stand. The literal posture: 站立 zhànlì (to stand erect), 站起来 zhàn qǐlái (to stand up), 站岗 zhàngǎng (to stand guard).
2. To halt, to stop moving. A vehicle or traveler that has come to a standstill: 站住 zhànzhù (halt! stop!), and the verb behind every transit stop.
3. A station, a stop. The fixed point where you halt: 车站 chēzhàn (bus or train station), 加油站 jiāyóuzhàn (petrol station), 终点站 zhōngdiǎnzhàn (terminus).
4. A post, a site. By the twentieth century, any service point: 广播站 guǎngbōzhàn (broadcast station), and in the internet age 网站 wǎngzhàn (website), the place on the network where you stop and stay.

Japanese borrowed the character for its postal-relay sense, though modern Japanese writes the train station as 駅 (eki); Korean keeps 站 (cham) for relay stations of the old courier roads. The thread across all of them is one body, planted, not going anywhere.

站立 zhànlì Standing & Halting
站zhànto stand; to stop
V 动词 dòngcí
The bare verb. On its own 站 means to be on one's feet, upright and still. As a command it means halt. It is one of the first action words a learner meets, paired endlessly with 起来 (up) and 住 (fast, in place).
公交车上没有座位，我站了一路。
Gōngjiāochē shàng méiyǒu zuòwèi, wǒ zhàn le yílù.
There were no seats on the bus, so I stood the whole way.
站住！别动！
Zhànzhù! Bié dòng!
Halt! Don't move!
辨析 biànxī · 站 vs. 立站 is the everyday spoken verb for standing on your feet; 立 is more literary and abstract, and survives mostly in compounds like 立正 (stand to attention) and 成立 (to establish).
站立zhànlìto stand erect
V 动词 dòngcí
站 (stand) + 立 (stand): two near-synonyms stacked for emphasis, a written-register word for standing upright. More formal than plain 站, common in instructions, ceremonies, and description.
士兵们笔直地站立在广场上。
Shìbīngmen bǐzhí de zhànlì zài guǎngchǎng shàng.
The soldiers stood bolt upright in the square.
站起来zhàn qǐláito stand up
V 动词 dòngcí
站 + 起来 (the directional complement "up"): to rise from sitting to standing. The 起来 makes the verb a completed upward motion rather than a static posture.
老师进来的时候，学生们都站起来了。
Lǎoshī jìnlái de shíhou, xuéshengmen dōu zhàn qǐlái le.
When the teacher came in, the students all stood up.
站岗zhàngǎngto stand guard
VO 动宾 dòngbīn
站 (to stand) + 岗 (gǎng, a post or sentry position): to take up a guard post. A verb-object phrase, literally "to stand a post," used of soldiers, guards, and watchmen holding their station.
两个警卫在大门口站岗。
Liǎng gè jǐngwèi zài dàménkǒu zhàngǎng.
Two guards stand watch at the main gate.
车站 chēzhàn Station & Stop

As a noun, 站 names the place where movement stops. The pattern is almost always [what stops there] + 站: 车站 (vehicles stop), 加油站 (cars stop to refuel), 终点站 (the line stops for good). To count stops along a route, 站 even works as a measure word: 三站 sān zhàn (three stops away).

车站chēzhànstation; bus or train stop
N 名词 míngcí
车 (chē, vehicle) + 站 (stop): the place where buses or trains halt and passengers board. The base word for any ground-transit station; specify with 火车站 huǒchēzhàn (train station) or 汽车站 qìchēzhàn (bus station).
火车站离我家很近，走路十分钟就到。
Huǒchēzhàn lí wǒ jiā hěn jìn, zǒulù shí fēnzhōng jiù dào.
The train station is close to my home; it's a ten-minute walk.
加油站jiāyóuzhànpetrol station
N 名词 míngcí
加油 (jiāyóu, to add fuel) + 站 (station): the place a car stops to refuel. Note that 加油 doubles as the cheer "let's go! keep it up!", literally "add oil," but here it is strictly the fuel sense.
前面有个加油站，我们停下来加点油吧。
Qiánmiàn yǒu ge jiāyóuzhàn, wǒmen tíng xiàlái jiā diǎn yóu ba.
There's a petrol station ahead; let's pull over and fill up.
站台zhàntáiplatform
N 名词 míngcí
站 (station) + 台 (tái, raised platform): the boarding platform at a station, the raised edge alongside the track. By extension, 站台 also means to publicly back someone, to "stand on the platform" for them.
请在三号站台等候开往北京的列车。
Qǐng zài sān hào zhàntái děnghòu kāiwǎng Běijīng de lièchē.
Please wait on platform three for the train to Beijing.
终点站zhōngdiǎnzhànterminus; last stop
N 名词 míngcí
终点 (zhōngdiǎn, end point) + 站 (stop): the final stop on a route, where the line ends and everyone gets off. Its opposite is 起点站 qǐdiǎnzhàn, the first stop.
这是终点站，请大家带好行李下车。
Zhè shì zhōngdiǎnzhàn, qǐng dàjiā dài hǎo xíngli xià chē.
This is the last stop; please take your luggage and get off.
网站 wǎngzhàn Site & Post
古义新用 gǔyì xīnyòng · Old Sense, New Use

The newest life of 站 came with the internet. A website is a 网站 wǎngzhàn, literally a "net station," the place on the 网 (wǎng, net) where you stop and stay a while. The metaphor is exact: just as a 车站 is a fixed point on a transit line where travelers halt, a 网站 is a fixed point on the network where users land. The same logic gives 网站 a relative, 站长 zhànzhǎng, the "station chief," meaning a website's administrator or webmaster. An old word for a courier relay post slid cleanly into the language of servers and domains.

网站wǎngzhànwebsite
N 名词 míngcí
网 (wǎng, net) + 站 (station): a website, a fixed point on the internet. The everyday word for any site you visit, from a shop to a search engine.
你可以在我们的网站上查到所有信息。
Nǐ kěyǐ zài wǒmen de wǎngzhàn shàng chádào suǒyǒu xìnxī.
You can find all the information on our website.
站长zhànzhǎngstation chief; webmaster
N 名词 míngcí
站 (station) + 长 (zhǎng, head, chief): the person in charge of a station. Originally a stationmaster; now also the administrator of a 网站, a webmaster.
有问题可以直接联系站长。
Yǒu wèntí kěyǐ zhíjiē liánxì zhànzhǎng.
If you have a problem you can contact the site admin directly.
站点zhàndiǎnsite; node; stop
N 名词 míngcí
站 (station) + 点 (diǎn, point): a designated point or node, whether a physical service point or a website. Broad and slightly technical, common in logistics, networks, and field operations.
公司在全国设立了上百个服务站点。
Gōngsī zài quánguó shèlì le shàng bǎi gè fúwù zhàndiǎn.
The company has set up over a hundred service points nationwide.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
站稳脚跟zhàn wěn jiǎo gēnto stand firm; to gain a footholdLiterally "to stand steady on one's heels." A common set phrase, not a classical four-character idiom: to establish oneself securely in a place, job, or market before making a move. Everyday modern usage.
站不住脚zhàn bù zhù jiǎocannot stand on its feet: untenableLiterally "the feet cannot hold their stand." Used of an argument, excuse, or theory that collapses under scrutiny. Colloquial and very frequent.
站在风口浪尖zhàn zài fēng kǒu làng jiānto stand where the wind and waves meet: at the eye of the stormTo be exposed at the most dangerous, contested forefront of events, where pressure and scrutiny are fiercest. Modern journalistic register, common in commentary.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Picture a lone sentry planted on a stone platform at the edge of a railway. His feet are set apart, pressed into the ground, and he does not move: that is the left half of the character, 立, the standing figure with the earth-line beneath his boots. Trains slide in and slide out; he stays. He is the stop.

Now read the whole glyph as the scene around him. The right half, 占 zhàn, only lends its sound, but you can hear it in the word the sentry guards: 车站, the station, the one fixed point on a moving line. Every sense of 站 is this same picture at a different scale. A body that stands (站立). A guard who holds his post (站岗). A platform where travelers halt (站台). And on the network, a 网站, the still place where you stop scrolling and stay. Wherever motion comes to rest on its feet, that is 站.
