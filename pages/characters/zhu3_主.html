字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

主 zhǔ is a pictograph of a flame burning on a lamp-stand. The oracle-bone and bronze forms show a tall stand with a single point of fire resting on top. That top dot, classed under the radical 丶 (zhǔ, "dot"), is the flame itself; the strokes below are the body of the lamp. The lamp was the steady central light of a room, and from that fixed, presiding point came the idea of the one in charge: the master of the house, the host who keeps the hearth.

From the central light four senses radiate outward:

1. Master, owner, host. The one who presides: 主人 zhǔrén (master; host), 房主 fángzhǔ (homeowner), 主席 zhǔxí (chairman; the one at the head of the table).
2. Main, primary, principal. The central thing among many: 主要 zhǔyào (main), 主题 zhǔtí (theme), 主意 zhǔyi (idea).
3. To be in charge, to decide, to advocate. The master's act of deciding: 做主 zuòzhǔ (to decide), 主持 zhǔchí (to host; to preside), 主张 zhǔzhāng (to advocate).
4. The Lord, a deity. The supreme master in religion: 主 zhǔ is how Chinese Christians address God, and 天主 Tiānzhǔ ("Lord of Heaven") names the Catholic God.

The thread through every sense is the presiding center. Whether the field is a household, a meeting, an argument, or the heavens, 主 is the one fixed point that holds authority, the flame at the middle of the room that everything else arranges itself around.

主人 zhǔrén Master & Host
主人 zhǔrén master; owner; host
N 名词 míngcí
主 zhǔ (master) + 人 rén (person). The one who owns a thing or receives a guest: an animal's owner, a house's master, the host of a gathering. Its pair is 客人 kèrén, the guest.
这只狗的主人很友好。
Zhè zhī gǒu de zhǔrén hěn yǒuhǎo.
This dog's owner is very friendly.
主人热情地招待了客人。
Zhǔrén rèqíng de zhāodài le kèrén.
The host warmly received the guests.
主席 zhǔxí chairman; chairperson; president (of a body)
N 名词 míngcí
主 zhǔ (presiding) + 席 xí (seat; mat). Literally "the presiding seat," the one who sits at the head of a meeting or a state. Used for the chair of a committee and for a head of state.
他被选为协会主席。
Tā bèi xuǎn wéi xiéhuì zhǔxí.
He was elected chairman of the association.
房主 fángzhǔ homeowner; landlord
N 名词 míngcí
房 fáng (house) + 主 zhǔ (owner). The person who owns a dwelling. The pattern noun + 主 names the owner of a thing: 车主 chēzhǔ (car owner), 业主 yèzhǔ (property owner).
房主同意降低租金。
Fángzhǔ tóngyì jiàngdī zūjīn.
The landlord agreed to lower the rent.
辨析 biànxī · 主人 vs. 房主 主人 is broad: the owner of any thing or the host of any gathering, and it carries the warm sense of a host welcoming guests. 房主 is narrow and neutral: strictly the legal owner of a house, with no hosting sense at all.
主要 zhǔyào Main & Primary
主要 zhǔyào main; chief; principal; primary
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
主 zhǔ (principal) + 要 yào (important). The central, leading one among several: 主要原因 "the main reason," 主要任务 "the chief task." The opposite is 次要 cìyào, secondary.
这是我们主要的目标。
Zhè shì wǒmen zhǔyào de mùbiāo.
This is our main goal.
辨析 biànxī · 主要 vs. 重要 主要 ranks one thing as the chief among several, the principal one in a set: the main reason, the leading cause. 重要 zhòngyào simply says a thing matters a lot, with no ranking implied. Many things can be 重要 at once, but only the foremost is 主要.
主题 zhǔtí theme; subject; main topic
N 名词 míngcí
主 zhǔ (central) + 题 tí (topic). The governing idea that a work, talk, or event turns around: 文章的主题 "the theme of the essay," 主题公园 "a theme park."
这部电影的主题是友谊。
Zhè bù diànyǐng de zhǔtí shì yǒuyì.
The theme of this film is friendship.
主意 zhǔyi idea; plan; decision
N 名词 míngcí
主 zhǔ (governing) + 意 yì (intention). The plan one settles on, the idea one acts by: 好主意 "a good idea," 拿主意 "to make up one's mind." Note the neutral tone on yi.
这真是个好主意。
Zhè zhēn shì ge hǎo zhǔyi.
That really is a good idea.
主张 zhǔzhāng Advocate & Be in Charge
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The master's defining act is to decide. 做主 zuòzhǔ is to "act as master," to be the one who settles a matter: 这件事你做主 "you make the call on this." From the same root comes 主持 zhǔchí, to preside over or host an event, the verb behind every emcee and chair. And 主张 zhǔzhāng is to advocate, to put forward and stand by a position: as a verb it means "to maintain," as a noun it means "a stance," the view one has decided to hold.

The same character anchors the political word 民主 mínzhǔ, democracy, literally "the people as master." Where a 君主 jūnzhǔ (monarch) is the single presiding master of a state, 民主 hands that mastering role to the people themselves. The flame that once meant the one in charge of a household scales all the way up to who holds authority over a country.

主张 zhǔzhāng to advocate; to maintain; a stance; a position
V/N 动词/名词 dòngcí/míngcí
主 zhǔ (to decide) + 张 zhāng (to stretch; to spread). As a verb, to put forward and stand by a view; as a noun, the view itself: 我主张和平 "I advocate peace," 这是我的主张 "this is my position."
他主张用对话解决问题。
Tā zhǔzhāng yòng duìhuà jiějué wèntí.
He advocates solving the problem through dialogue.
民主 mínzhǔ democracy; democratic
N 名词 míngcí
民 mín (the people) + 主 zhǔ (master). The people as master, the modern term for democracy. Also used adjectivally: 民主国家 "a democratic country," 民主选举 "a democratic election."
他们争取更多的民主。
Tāmen zhēngqǔ gèng duō de mínzhǔ.
They are fighting for more democracy.
主持 zhǔchí to host; to preside over; to chair
V 动词 dòngcí
主 zhǔ (to preside) + 持 chí (to hold). To run an event from the chair: to host a show, chair a meeting, or take charge of a project. The host of a broadcast is a 主持人 zhǔchírén.
她主持了今天的会议。
Tā zhǔchí le jīntiān de huìyì.
She chaired today's meeting.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
喧宾夺主 xuān bīn duó zhǔ the noisy guest usurps the host: a secondary thing overshadows the main one Lit: clamoring-guest-seizes-host. Said when something minor draws all the attention away from what should be central, like a sideshow that swallows the main event or a flashy detail that buries the point.
先入为主 xiān rù wéi zhǔ what enters first becomes master: first impressions dominate later judgment Lit: first-enter-become-master. The idea that lodges in the mind earliest takes the seat of authority and colors everything after it, the classical name for the grip of a first impression or a prior bias.
反客为主 fǎn kè wéi zhǔ turn from guest to host: to seize the initiative and take control Lit: reverse-guest-become-host. Once a stratagem of war: the side that arrives in a weaker, guest-like position turns the tables and takes command. Now used of anyone who flips a passive role into a controlling one.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

See the single flame resting on its stand: the top dot is the fire, the strokes below are the lamp. That steady central light is the whole character. Everything 主 means is the one presiding point that the room arranges itself around.

In a household, that point is the master and host (主人). Among many things, it is the main one (主要). In a meeting, it is the one who presides (主持). In an argument, it is the position one holds and stands by (主张). In a state, it is who holds authority, whether a single monarch (君主) or the people themselves (民主). Whenever you meet 主, look for the presiding center, and you will find the flame on the stand.
