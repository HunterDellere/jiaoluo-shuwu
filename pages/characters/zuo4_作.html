字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

作 zuò = 亻 (rén, person) + 乍 (zhà, the phonetic). It is a pictophonetic compound: the standing-person radical 亻 supplies the meaning, a human being engaged in activity, and 乍 supplies the sound. The right-hand 乍 originally meant "to begin, all of a sudden," so the early sense of 作 is a person setting something in motion, starting an action. From "to begin doing" the meaning broadened to the whole field of doing, making, and producing.

Three threads run out from that core:

1. To do, to engage in. Activity in general: 工作 gōngzuò (to work; a job), 作业 zuòyè (homework; an assignment).
2. To make, to produce, to compose. Bringing a thing into being, especially a crafted or written one: 作品 zuòpǐn (a work), 作文 zuòwén (a composition), 写作 xiězuò (writing), 制作 zhìzuò (to produce).
3. To act as, to regard as. Taking on a role or treating one thing as another: 作为 zuòwéi (as; conduct), 当作 dàngzuò (to treat as), 看作 kànzuò (to regard as).

The thread is purposeful making. Where its everyday cousin 做 zuò handles the concrete, hands-on doing of ordinary life, 作 leans toward the formal, the literary, and the abstract: composing, producing, and acting in a capacity. The two words share a sound and overlap heavily, which is exactly why their division of labor is worth learning.

工作 gōngzuò To Do & Work
工作 gōngzuò to work; work; a job
V/N 动名词
工 gōng (labor; craft) + 作 zuò (to do). The everyday word for work, both the verb (我在银行工作 "I work at a bank") and the noun (找工作 "to look for a job"). One of the first 作-words a learner meets.
他在一家公司工作。
Tā zài yì jiā gōngsī gōngzuò.
He works at a company.
这份工作很有意思。
Zhè fèn gōngzuò hěn yǒu yìsi.
This job is very interesting.
作业 zuòyè homework; assignment; (field) operation
N 名词 míngcí
作 zuò (to do) + 业 yè (task; trade). The work assigned to a student: 写作业 "to do homework," 交作业 "to hand in homework." In technical contexts it also means a field operation, as in 水下作业 "underwater work."
我还没做完作业。
Wǒ hái méi zuò wán zuòyè.
I haven't finished my homework yet.
合作 hézuò to cooperate; to collaborate; cooperation
V/N 动名词
合 hé (to join; together) + 作 zuò (to do). To work together toward a shared aim: 合作伙伴 "a business partner," 国际合作 "international cooperation."
两家公司决定合作。
Liǎng jiā gōngsī juédìng hézuò.
The two companies decided to cooperate.
作品 zuòpǐn To Compose & Create
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

This is where 作 sits closest to its literary heart. To 作 is to compose, to bring a crafted thing into being: a 作品 zuòpǐn (a work of art or literature), a 作文 zuòwén (a written composition), a piece of 音乐 written by a 作曲家 zuòqǔjiā (a composer). The maker is the 作者 zuòzhě (author) or 作家 zuòjiā (writer). Here 作 keeps the dignity of its older sense, the deliberate act of creation, distinct from mere doing.

This creative weight is why 作 rather than 做 fills the vocabulary of the arts and letters. You 做 a meal or 做 the dishes, the hands-on chores of the day; but you 作 a poem, 作 a painting, 作 a symphony. The same falling-tone sound splits cleanly along this line: 做 for the concrete and ordinary, 作 for the composed and the made-to-last.

作品 zuòpǐn a work (of art, literature, music)
N 名词 míngcí
作 zuò (to compose) + 品 pǐn (article; item). A finished creative work: 文学作品 "a literary work," 这是他最好的作品 "this is his finest work."
这幅画是他的代表作品。
Zhè fú huà shì tā de dàibiǎo zuòpǐn.
This painting is his representative work.
作者 zuòzhě author; writer; the one who made it
N 名词 míngcí
作 zuò (to compose) + 者 zhě (the one who). The maker of a work, most often a writer but usable for any creator. Compare 作家 zuòjiā, which specifically means a professional writer or author by trade.
这本书的作者是谁？
Zhè běn shū de zuòzhě shì shéi?
Who is the author of this book?
作用 zuòyòng effect; function; role (that something plays)
N 名词 míngcí
作 zuò (to act) + 用 yòng (use). The effect a thing produces, the function it serves: 起作用 "to take effect; to work," 副作用 "side effect." The work that something does in the world.
这种药对感冒很有作用。
Zhè zhǒng yào duì gǎnmào hěn yǒu zuòyòng.
This medicine works well for colds.
作为 zuòwéi To Act As & Regard As
作为 zuòwéi as (in the capacity of); conduct, deeds; to act as
PREP/N 兼类
作 zuò (to act) + 为 wéi (to be; as). As a preposition it frames a role: 作为老师 "as a teacher." As a noun it means one's deeds or accomplishments: 有所作为 "to achieve something," 大有作为 "to have great prospects."
作为父母，我们很担心。
Zuòwéi fùmǔ, wǒmen hěn dānxīn.
As parents, we are very worried.
当作 dàngzuò to treat as; to regard as; to take for
V 动词 dòngcí
当 dàng (to treat as) + 作 zuò (to act as). To take one thing as if it were another: 把这里当作自己的家 "treat this place as your own home." A close synonym is 看作 kànzuò, to regard as.
他把我当作好朋友。
Tā bǎ wǒ dàngzuò hǎo péngyou.
He treats me as a good friend.
辨析 biànxī · 作 vs. 做 Both are zuò and both mean "to do / make," but they divide the labor. 做 handles the concrete and everyday: 做饭 cook, 做事 handle matters, 做生意 do business. 作 handles the abstract, formal, and composed: 作文 write an essay, 作曲 compose music, 作出决定 make a decision. When in doubt about a physical, hands-on action, reach for 做; for composing, producing, or acting in a capacity, reach for 作.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
自作自受 zì zuò zì shòu what you make, you suffer: to reap the consequences of your own actions Lit: self-do-self-bear. The Chinese counterpart of "you made your bed, now lie in it." Said of trouble that a person has brought entirely upon themselves.
作茧自缚 zuò jiǎn zì fù to spin a cocoon and bind oneself: to trap yourself by your own doing Lit: make-cocoon-self-bind. As a silkworm spins the cocoon that encloses it, a person can create rules, habits, or schemes that end up trapping them.
装模作样 zhuāng mú zuò yàng to fake a manner and put on an act: to playact, to be affected Lit: feign-form-make-appearance. To strike a pose for show, behaving in a studied or insincere way to impress or deceive others.
为非作歹 wéi fēi zuò dǎi to do wrong and commit evil: to behave wickedly Lit: do-wrong-make-evil. A set phrase for serious wrongdoing, often said of criminals or tyrants who harm others without restraint.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

See the person 亻 on the left and read 乍 as the spark of beginning: a human being who sets something into motion. That is 作 at its root, to start a deed and carry it through to a made thing.

Follow the spark in three directions. Bend it toward labor and you get the daily job (工作) and the assignment (作业). Bend it toward craft and you get the composed work (作品) and its author (作者). Bend it toward role and you get acting in a capacity (作为) and treating one thing as another (当作). And when the doing is plain and physical rather than composed, the language hands the task to its twin 做. Keep that division in mind, and the two zuò will never blur.
