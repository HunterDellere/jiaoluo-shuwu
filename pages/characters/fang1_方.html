字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

方 fāng is an ancient pictograph whose exact original referent is debated. The oracle-bone form shows a shape often read as a plough with a crossbar, or alternatively as two boats lashed side by side; the Shuōwén Jiězì glosses it as boats joined together. What matters for the modern character is the idea that settled onto the glyph: a flat, four-sided, oriented thing. 方 became the word for "square," and from squareness came the whole family of meaning.

Four senses branch from that core:

1. Square, the shape. 正方形 zhèngfāngxíng (square), 长方形 chángfāngxíng (rectangle), 方块 fāngkuài (a block, a cube).
2. Direction and place. A square has four sides, so 方 names orientation: 方向 fāngxiàng (direction), 东方 dōngfāng (the east), 地方 dìfang (place).
3. Method and way. The path by which something is approached: 方法 fāngfǎ (method), 方式 fāngshì (way, manner), and the prescription a doctor writes, 方子 fāngzi.
4. Side or party. One of two parties to a matter: 对方 duìfāng (the other party), 双方 shuāngfāng (both sides).

The thread is orientation. A square has fixed sides and corners, and from that fixity 方 supplies the language of where a thing is, which way it faces, and how it is to be done. As an adverb it also means "just now," the moment squarely at hand.

地方 dìfang Place & Direction
地方 dìfang place; spot; (also) local, regional
N 名词 míngcí
地 dì (earth; ground) + 方 fang. The all-purpose word for "place," from a physical spot to an abstract point in an argument (这个地方我不同意 "on this point I disagree"). With full tone, 地方 dìfāng means "local" as opposed to "central": 地方政府 local government.
这个地方很安静。
Zhège dìfang hěn ānjìng.
This place is very quiet.
你住在什么地方？
Nǐ zhù zài shénme dìfang?
Where do you live?
方向 fāngxiàng direction; orientation; bearing
N 名词 míngcí
方 fāng (square; direction) + 向 xiàng (toward). The way one faces or heads, literally on a road and figuratively in life or work: 人生的方向 "the direction of one's life."
我们走错了方向。
Wǒmen zǒu cuò le fāngxiàng.
We went the wrong way.
东方 dōngfāng the east; the Orient
N 名词 míngcí
东 dōng (east) + 方 fāng (direction). The eastern direction, and by extension "the East" as a cultural sphere. Its pair is 西方 xīfāng, the West. The four directions all take 方: 南方 the south, 北方 the north.
太阳从东方升起。
Tàiyáng cóng dōngfāng shēngqǐ.
The sun rises in the east.
方法 fāngfǎ Method & Way
方法 fāngfǎ method; means; way of doing something
N 名词 míngcí
方 fāng (way) + 法 fǎ (method; law). The standard word for a method or technique, the concrete procedure for getting something done: 学习方法 study methods, 解决方法 a solution.
这个方法很有效。
Zhège fāngfǎ hěn yǒuxiào.
This method is very effective.
辨析 biànxī · 方法 vs. 方式 方法 is the concrete procedure or technique, the steps you follow. 方式 fāngshì is the broader manner or style of doing something: 生活方式 "way of life," 思维方式 "way of thinking." A 方法 solves a task; a 方式 characterizes a whole approach.
方式 fāngshì way; manner; mode; style
N 名词 míngcí
方 fāng (way) + 式 shì (form; pattern). The manner or mode in which something is done, broader and more abstract than 方法: 生活方式 lifestyle, 付款方式 payment method.
每个人的生活方式都不同。
Měi ge rén de shēnghuó fāngshì dōu bùtóng.
Everyone's way of life is different.
方便 fāngbiàn convenient; to make things easy for; (euphemism) to use the toilet
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
方 fāng + 便 biàn (convenient; suitable). Easy and convenient, of a time, a place, or an arrangement. 方便面 fāngbiànmiàn is instant noodles, literally "convenient noodles." As a polite euphemism it also means to relieve oneself.
这里交通很方便。
Zhèlǐ jiāotōng hěn fāngbiàn.
The transport here is very convenient.
方面 fāngmiàn Aspect & Party
方面 fāngmiàn aspect; respect; side (of a matter)
N 名词 míngcí
方 fāng (side) + 面 miàn (face; surface). One facet of a many-sided thing. The paired construction 一方面…另一方面 yī fāngmiàn… lìng yī fāngmiàn means "on one hand… on the other hand."
这个问题有很多方面。
Zhège wèntí yǒu hěn duō fāngmiàn.
This problem has many aspects.
对方 duìfāng the other party; the opposite side
N 名词 míngcí
对 duì (facing; opposite) + 方 fāng (party). The other side in a conversation, negotiation, contest, or relationship. Its pairs are 双方 shuāngfāng (both parties) and 我方 wǒfāng (our side).
请站在对方的角度想一想。
Qǐng zhàn zài duìfāng de jiǎodù xiǎng yī xiǎng.
Please think from the other person's point of view.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
四面八方 sì miàn bā fāng four faces and eight directions: from all sides, everywhere at once Lit: four-sides-eight-directions. People or things converging 从四面八方 come "from all over." One of the most common everyday set phrases for "every direction."
想方设法 xiǎng fāng shè fǎ to think of squares and devise methods: to try every possible means Lit: think-method-set-up-method. To rack one's brains for a way through, doing whatever it takes. 方 and 法 here both carry the "method" sense, doubled for emphasis.
大大方方 dà dà fāng fāng generous and unaffected; poised and at ease in manner The reduplicated form of 大方 dàfang (generous; gracious; not stingy or awkward). Said in praise of someone whose bearing is relaxed and dignified rather than petty or self-conscious.
千方百计 qiān fāng bǎi jì a thousand methods and a hundred schemes: by every conceivable means Lit: thousand-methods-hundred-plans. Stronger than 想方设法, often with a hint that the effort is dogged or even scheming. 千方百计地想办法 "to try every trick in the book."
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold a square in mind. It has four equal sides and four fixed corners, and everything 方 means radiates from that fixity. The four sides become the four directions (东方, 西方, 南方, 北方). A fixed spot on the ground becomes a place (地方). The settled procedure for a task becomes a method (方法). And one of the two facing sides of a matter becomes a party to it (对方).

When 方 appears as an adverb meaning "just now," picture the present moment as the corner squarely under your foot: the point you are standing on right now. From the geometry of the square, the whole vocabulary of orientation and approach falls out.
