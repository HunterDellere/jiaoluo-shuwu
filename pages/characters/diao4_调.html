字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

调 is the simplified form of traditional 調. It is a pictophonetic compound: the speech radical 讠 (yán, the simplified form of 言) carries the meaning, while 周 (zhōu) supplies the sound. The speech radical marks 调 as a word that began in the realm of the voice, of tones, words, and the harmonizing of speech and sound.

The phonetic 周 zhōu means "all around, complete, well-rounded." That sense of bringing things into a rounded whole sits quietly under both readings of 调: to tune is to bring sounds into accord, to blend is to bring flavors into balance, to mediate is to bring two sides into agreement. The radical and phonetic together point to harmony achieved through the voice and, by extension, through careful arrangement.

调 is a polyphone, and the reading splits the senses cleanly:

1. diào (falling tone). To transfer or move from one place to another: 调动 diàodòng (to transfer, to mobilize), 调查 diàochá (to investigate). Also a melody, key, or pitch: 曲调 qǔdiào (a tune), 音调 yīndiào (pitch), 声调 shēngdiào (a tone). By extension, an accent or manner of speech: 腔调 qiāngdiào (an accent), 论调 lùndiào (a line of argument).
2. tiáo (rising tone). To adjust, to bring into balance: 调整 tiáozhěng (to adjust), 调节 tiáojié (to regulate). To blend or mix: 调味 tiáowèi (to season), 调料 tiáoliào (seasoning), 调和 tiáohé (to harmonize). To mediate: 调解 tiáojiě (to mediate), 调停 tiáotíng (to mediate a dispute).

The thread through both readings is arrangement. In the falling tone diào the arrangement is of position and of pitch: where a person is posted, how high a note sits, what an accent sounds like. In the rising tone tiáo the arrangement is of balance: tuning a dial, blending a sauce, reconciling a quarrel. Hear the tone and you know whether 调 is moving and pitching things or harmonizing them.

调动 diàodòng Transfer & Investigate: the diào Reading
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

In the falling tone diào, one branch of 调 is about moving things into place. 调动 diàodòng is to transfer a person to a new post, or to mobilize resources and energy. 调度 diàodù is to dispatch and schedule, the work of a controller directing trains, flights, or crews. From "moving people to find things out" comes 调查 diàochá, to investigate or conduct a survey: an inquiry sent out to gather facts. The common image is of resources and questions being directed to where they are needed.

调动 diàodòng to transfer; to mobilize; to arouse
V 动词 dòngcí
调 diào (to transfer) + 动 dòng (to move). To move a person to a new post, or to mobilize forces and feelings. 调动工作 (transfer to a new job), 调动积极性 (arouse enthusiasm). The sense is of marshalling people or energy toward a purpose.
他被调动到南方工作。
Tā bèi diàodòng dào nánfāng gōngzuò.
He was transferred to work in the south.
好老师能调动学生的积极性。
Hǎo lǎoshī néng diàodòng xuéshēng de jījíxìng.
A good teacher can arouse students' enthusiasm.
调查 diàochá to investigate; to survey; investigation
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
调 diào (to direct, to send out) + 查 chá (to check, to examine). To look into a matter systematically, whether a police inquiry or a market survey. 市场调查 (market survey), 调查结果 (the findings of an investigation). Both verb and noun.
警察正在调查这件案子。
Jǐngchá zhèngzài diàochá zhè jiàn ànzi.
The police are investigating this case.
我们做了一次市场调查。
Wǒmen zuò le yí cì shìchǎng diàochá.
We carried out a market survey.
辨析 biànxī · 调查 (diào) vs. 调整 (tiáo) 调查 diàochá uses the falling tone diào and means "to investigate," to send out an inquiry. 调整 tiáozhěng uses the rising tone tiáo and means "to adjust," to bring into balance. Same glyph, two tones: one moves and checks, the other harmonizes. This pair is the cleanest test of which reading you have.
调度 diàodù to dispatch; to schedule; a dispatcher
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
调 diào (to move, to transfer) + 度 dù (to measure, to manage). To dispatch and coordinate trains, vehicles, crews, or tasks; also the person who does it. 列车调度 (train dispatching), 调度中心 (control center). The work of directing many moving parts.
调度员负责安排所有列车。
Diàodùyuán fùzé ānpái suǒyǒu lièchē.
The dispatcher is responsible for arranging all the trains.
音调 yīndiào Tune, Tone & Accent: still the diào Reading
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · The Sound of Speech

The other branch of the falling tone diào lives in sound. 调子 diàozi is a tune or melody, and by extension the pitch of a voice or even the mood of a piece of writing. 音调 yīndiào is pitch, how high or low a sound sits. 声调 shēngdiào is a tone in the linguistic sense: the four tones of Mandarin are its 声调. Widen further and 调 names the manner of speech itself: 腔调 qiāngdiào is an accent or a way of talking, 论调 lùndiào is the slant or line of an argument. From a single note to a whole accent, this branch of 调 is the pitch and color of the voice.

声调 shēngdiào tone (in a tonal language)
N 名词 míngcí
声 shēng (sound, voice) + 调 diào (pitch pattern). The linguistic tone: the rise and fall of pitch that distinguishes meaning in Mandarin. 第一声 (first tone), 四个声调 (the four tones). Every syllable on this very page carries a 声调.
汉语有四个声调。
Hànyǔ yǒu sì gè shēngdiào.
Mandarin has four tones.
他的声调不太准。
Tā de shēngdiào bú tài zhǔn.
His tones are not quite accurate.
调子 diàozi tune; melody; tone (of voice or mood)
N 名词 míngcí
调 diào (melody, pitch) + 子 zi (noun suffix). A tune or melody, and figuratively the pitch or mood of speech or writing. 唱调子 (carry a tune), 定调子 (set the tone, decide the direction). The everyday word for the shape of a sound.
这首歌的调子很好听。
Zhè shǒu gē de diàozi hěn hǎotīng.
The tune of this song is lovely.
腔调 qiāngdiào accent; intonation; tone of speech
N 名词 míngcí
腔 qiāng (cavity, the way speech resonates) + 调 diào (pitch, manner). The accent and intonation of a person's speech, often the regional or affected way they talk. 南方腔调 (a southern accent), 装腔作势 (put on airs). The sound that marks where a voice comes from.
他说话带着浓重的北方腔调。
Tā shuōhuà dài zhe nóngzhòng de běifāng qiāngdiào.
He speaks with a heavy northern accent.
调整 tiáozhěng Adjust, Blend & Mediate: the tiáo Reading
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · The Second Reading

Let the tone rise from falling diào to rising tiáo and 调 turns from moving and pitching things to harmonizing them. 调整 tiáozhěng is to adjust, to set something right by fine degrees. 调节 tiáojié is to regulate, to keep a level steady, as a thermostat regulates heat. In the kitchen, 调味 tiáowèi is to season and 调料 tiáoliào is the seasoning itself, while 调和 tiáohé is to blend flavors or, more broadly, to harmonize. Among people, 调解 tiáojiě is to mediate a dispute and 调停 tiáotíng is to step in and settle a quarrel. Across all of these the rising tone tiáo names the act of bringing things into balance.

调整 tiáozhěng to adjust; to regulate; adjustment
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
调 tiáo (to adjust) + 整 zhěng (to put in order). To set something right by careful change: 调整计划 (adjust the plan), 调整心态 (adjust one's attitude), 价格调整 (a price adjustment). The everyday word for fine-tuning anything.
我们需要调整一下计划。
Wǒmen xūyào tiáozhěng yíxià jìhuà.
We need to adjust the plan a little.
请把座位调整到合适的高度。
Qǐng bǎ zuòwèi tiáozhěng dào héshì de gāodù.
Please adjust the seat to a suitable height.
调节 tiáojié to regulate; to adjust; to balance
V 动词 dòngcí
调 tiáo (to adjust) + 节 jié (to control, to moderate). To keep a level or rhythm steady: 调节温度 (regulate the temperature), 空调 (air conditioning, literally "air regulator"), 自我调节 (self-regulation). The sense is ongoing balance rather than a single change.
这个开关可以调节灯的亮度。
Zhège kāiguān kěyǐ tiáojié dēng de liàngdù.
This switch can regulate the brightness of the light.
调味 tiáowèi to season; to flavor
V 动词 dòngcí
调 tiáo (to blend) + 味 wèi (flavor, taste). To season food, to bring its flavors into balance. The seasoning itself is 调料 tiáoliào or 调味品 tiáowèipǐn. The kitchen sense of tiáo: mixing tastes until they are right.
他用酱油和糖给菜调味。
Tā yòng jiàngyóu hé táng gěi cài tiáowèi.
He seasons the dish with soy sauce and sugar.
调解 tiáojiě to mediate; to reconcile; mediation
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
调 tiáo (to harmonize) + 解 jiě (to resolve, to untie). To step between two sides and settle their dispute. 调解纠纷 (mediate a dispute), 调解员 (a mediator). The social form of tiáo: bringing people, not flavors, into accord.
他帮两家邻居调解了矛盾。
Tā bāng liǎng jiā línjū tiáojiě le máodùn.
He helped the two neighbor families resolve their conflict.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
风调雨顺 fēng tiáo yǔ shùn favorable weather: good conditions for a harvest Lit: the wind is well-tuned and the rain comes in due course. A set wish for a year of mild, timely weather and a good harvest, often paired with 国泰民安 (the country at peace and the people content). Here 调 takes the tiáo reading, "to harmonize," the weather brought into balance.
南腔北调 nán qiāng běi diào a jumble of accents: speech mixing many regional tongues Lit: southern resonance and northern tones. Said of speech that mixes accents from all over, or of a gathering where many dialects are heard at once. Here 调 takes the diào reading, "accent, manner of speech."
老调重弹 lǎo diào chóng tán to rehash old ideas: to bring out a tired theme again Lit: to pluck the old tune once more. A dismissive phrase for repeating a stale argument or worn-out idea as if it were new. Here 调 takes the diào reading, "a tune, a melody."
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

Hold one idea at the center of 调: arrangement, with the speech radical 讠 reminding you it began in the realm of sound. The phonetic 周 means "well-rounded, complete," and that is what 调 does in every sense, it brings things into a rounded whole. The tone tells you which kind of arrangement you mean.

Hear the falling tone diào and think of moving and pitching. A person is 调动 to a new post; an inquiry is sent out as a 调查; a dispatcher does the 调度. Or the arrangement is of sound: the four 声调 of Mandarin, the 调子 of a song, the 腔调 of an accent. Then let the tone rise to tiáo and the arrangement turns to balance. You 调整 a plan, 调节 a temperature, 调味 a dish, 调解 a quarrel. Falling diào moves and pitches; rising tiáo blends and harmonizes. The cleanest pair to lock it in is 调查 diàochá, to investigate, against 调整 tiáozhěng, to adjust: one tone sends out, the other brings into balance.
