字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

正 zhèng = 一 (a line, the target) + 止 (zhǐ, foot). The lower element 止 is the ancient pictograph of a foot, and the stroke above it marks a place to reach. The oracle-bone form shows a foot advancing on a walled town or a fixed mark; the early sense is to march straight toward a target and halt squarely on it. From "to head straight at the mark and stop on it" came the whole field of meaning: straight, upright, correct, exact.

Four senses branch from the foot on the line:

1. Straight, upright, correct. Hitting the mark without deviation: 正确 zhèngquè (correct), 公正 gōngzhèng (just; fair), 端正 duānzhèng (upright in form and conduct).
2. Exactly, precisely. Squarely on the spot: 正好 zhènghǎo (just right), 正是 zhèngshì (precisely is).
3. Principal, main, regular. The central one as opposed to the deputy or the deviant: 正门 zhèngmén (main gate), 正常 zhèngcháng (normal).
4. In the midst of (the ongoing). The action squarely underway right now: 正在 zhèngzài (in the process of), as in 我正在吃饭 "I'm right in the middle of eating."

The thread is the mark hit dead center. Whether the target is moral (justice), factual (correctness), spatial (the main entrance), or temporal (the present instant), 正 is the foot landing exactly where it should, with nothing crooked or off to the side.

正确 zhèngquè Correct & Upright
正确 zhèngquè correct; right; accurate
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
正 zhèng (straight; right) + 确 què (sure; firm). Correct in the sense of matching the truth or the standard: 正确答案 "the correct answer," 正确的方法 "the right method." Its opposite is 错误 cuòwù.
你的答案是正确的。
Nǐ de dá'àn shì zhèngquè de.
Your answer is correct.
公正 gōngzhèng just; fair; impartial
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
公 gōng (public; impartial) + 正 zhèng (upright). Straight dealing that favors no side: 公正的判决 "a fair verdict." The moral 正, justice as the line everyone is held to equally.
法官应该公正。
Fǎguān yīnggāi gōngzhèng.
A judge should be impartial.
改正 gǎizhèng to correct; to put right; to amend
V 动词 dòngcí
改 gǎi (to change) + 正 zhèng (straight). To change something crooked back to straight: 改正错误 "to correct a mistake." The act of bringing a thing back onto the line.
他很快就改正了错误。
Tā hěn kuài jiù gǎizhèng le cuòwù.
He corrected the mistake quickly.
正好 zhènghǎo Exact & Principal
正好 zhènghǎo just right; exactly; as it happens
ADV 副词 fùcí
正 zhèng (exactly) + 好 hǎo (good). Landing exactly on the mark of fit or timing: 大小正好 "exactly the right size," 你来得正好 "you've come at just the right moment."
这双鞋的大小正好。
Zhè shuāng xié de dàxiǎo zhènghǎo.
This pair of shoes fits perfectly.
正常 zhèngcháng normal; regular
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
正 zhèng (regular) + 常 cháng (constant; usual). On the standard, ordinary track: 正常情况 "normal circumstances." Its opposite is 不正常 or 反常 fǎncháng (abnormal).
他的体温很正常。
Tā de tǐwēn hěn zhèngcháng.
His temperature is perfectly normal.
真正 zhēnzhèng true; genuine; real
ADJ 形容词 xíngróngcí
真 zhēn (true) + 正 zhèng (proper). Genuinely, properly so, not in name only: 真正的朋友 "a true friend." For the full sense of 真, see the 真 entry.
这才是真正的好茶。
Zhè cái shì zhēnzhèng de hǎo chá.
This is what real good tea is.
正在 zhèngzài Right Now: the Ongoing 正
语法洞见 yǔfǎ dòngjiàn · Grammatical Insight

One of the first jobs a learner meets 正 doing is marking the present continuous. 正在 zhèngzài placed before a verb says the action is squarely underway at this moment: 我正在吃饭 "I'm (right in the middle of) eating," 他们正在开会 "they're in a meeting right now." The 正 supplies "right at this point," the 在 supplies "in the act of," and a final 呢 ne often rounds it off: 正在下雨呢 "it's raining right now."

The aspectual 正 is the temporal cousin of the spatial one. Just as 正 marks the foot landing dead center on a target, 正在 marks the action caught dead center in its course, neither before it has begun nor after it has finished, but exactly in the midst. To go deeper on continuous aspect and the 在 / 着 / 呢 system, see the 在 entry.

正在 zhèngzài in the process of; right in the middle of (doing)
ADV 副词 fùcí
正 zhèng (right now) + 在 zài (in the act of). The marker of the present continuous, placed before the verb: 正在工作 "is working (right now)."
妈妈正在做饭。
Māma zhèngzài zuò fàn.
Mom is cooking right now.
反正 fǎnzhèng anyway; in any case; regardless
ADV 副词 fùcí
反 fǎn (reverse) + 正 zhèng (right). Literally "reverse-or-right," whichever way it turns out: the all-purpose "anyway, the outcome is the same." 反正我不去 "anyway, I'm not going."
反正时间还早，不用急。
Fǎnzhèng shíjiān hái zǎo, bùyòng jí.
It's still early anyway, no need to rush.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
堂堂正正 táng táng zhèng zhèng dignified and upright: open, honorable, with nothing to hide Reduplicated from 堂正. Said of someone who acts squarely and aboveboard, facing things head-on rather than scheming in the shadows. 堂堂正正做人 "to live as an upright person."
名正言顺 míng zhèng yán shùn the name is right and the words follow: properly justified and beyond reproach From the Analects, where Confucius holds that if names are not 正 (correct), speech will not accord with reality. To do a thing 名正言顺 is to do it with full and rightful warrant.
公正无私 gōng zhèng wú sī fair and selfless: impartial, with no private interest Lit: fair-upright-without-self. The ideal of the just official or judge who holds to the line without bending it toward kin, friends, or personal gain.
改邪归正 gǎi xié guī zhèng to abandon the crooked and return to the straight: to mend one's ways Lit: change-evil-return-upright. Said of someone who gives up wrongdoing and comes back to honest living. 邪 (crooked, evil) is the standing opposite of 正.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

See a foot, 止, stepping up to a line, 一, and stopping exactly on it: dead center, no swerve, no overshoot. That foot on the mark is the whole character. Everything 正 means is the foot landing where it should.

On a question, that is correctness (正确). On a scale of conduct, it is justice (公正). On a measurement, it is the exact fit (正好). On a building, it is the main gate rather than the side door (正门). And on the clock of an action, it is the present instant, the deed caught squarely in progress (正在). Whenever you meet 正, ask what the target is, and you will find the foot resting on it.
