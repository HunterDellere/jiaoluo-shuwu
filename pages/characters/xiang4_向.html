字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

向 xiàng is a pictograph of a window opening in a wall. The oracle-bone and bronze forms show the gabled outline of a house with an opening cut into the upper wall, the 口 below standing for that opening. A north-facing window in an early dwelling was a fixed point of orientation, so the glyph came to mean the way a thing faces, and from there the whole field of direction. The 口 at the base, classed as the radical, is the mouth of the opening rather than a spoken mouth.

From the facing window four senses fan out:

1. Direction, the way a thing points. The orientation itself: 方向 fāngxiàng (direction), 风向 fēngxiàng (wind direction), 方向感 fāngxiànggǎn (sense of direction).
2. Toward, to (the preposition). Placed before a target to aim an action at it: 向前 xiàngqián (forward), 向东 xiàng dōng (toward the east), 向他借钱 xiàng tā jièqián (to borrow money from him).
3. To face, to incline toward. The heart or body turning to something: 朝向 cháoxiàng (to face toward), 倾向 qīngxiàng (tendency), 向往 xiàngwǎng (to yearn for).
4. All along, hitherto. Time facing back to the present: 一向 yīxiàng (all along), 向来 xiànglái (always up to now).

The thread is a fixed facing. Whether the orientation is spatial (which way the window opens), directed (the arrow of a preposition), emotional (where the heart leans), or temporal (the settled habit reaching up to now), 向 names the steady way something is turned.

构词 gòucí Word-Formation Patterns

方向 fāngxiàng Direction
方向 fāngxiàng direction; orientation; the way ahead
N 名词 míngcí
方 fāng (square; side; region) + 向 xiàng (facing). The standard word for direction, both literal and figurative: 哪个方向 "which direction," 人生的方向 "the direction of one's life." The 方 supplies the side or region, the 向 the facing within it.
我们走错了方向。
Wǒmen zǒu cuò le fāngxiàng.
We went the wrong way.
他失去了方向感。
Tā shīqù le fāngxiànggǎn.
He lost his sense of direction.
风向 fēngxiàng wind direction; (figurative) which way the wind blows
N 名词 míngcí
风 fēng (wind) + 向 xiàng (direction). The direction the wind comes from. Used figuratively for the drift of opinion or events: 看风向 "to see which way the wind is blowing," to read the mood before acting.
今天的风向变了。
Jīntiān de fēngxiàng biàn le.
The wind direction has changed today.
走向 zǒuxiàng to move toward; trend; the run or layout of something
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
走 zǒu (to go) + 向 xiàng (toward). As a verb, to head toward an outcome: 走向成功 "to head toward success." As a noun, the trend or physical run of a thing, as in the 走向 of a mountain range or a market.
两国关系走向正常。
Liǎng guó guānxì zǒuxiàng zhèngcháng.
Relations between the two countries are moving toward normal.
辨析 biànxī · 方向 vs. 方位 方向 is the way one faces or heads, a line of travel. 方位 fāngwèi is a fixed bearing or position, the compass point a thing sits at. You walk in a 方向; you locate something by its 方位.
向前 xiàngqián Toward: the Preposition 向
语法洞见 yǔfǎ dòngjiàn · Grammatical Insight

The everyday job of 向 is as a preposition meaning "toward" or "to." It sits before the target and aims the verb at it: 向前走 xiàng qián zǒu (walk forward), 向东开 xiàng dōng kāi (drive toward the east), 向他学习 xiàng tā xuéxí (to learn from him). With a person it can mark the source as well as the goal, the one you direct a request at: 向他借钱 xiàng tā jièqián (to borrow money from him), 向老师提问 xiàng lǎoshī tíwèn (to put a question to the teacher).

Its near-twin is 朝 cháo, which also means "toward" or "facing." The two overlap for physical orientation (向南 and 朝南 both mean "facing south"), but they part ways with abstract or interpersonal targets: 向 is the one used for directing speech, requests, and learning at a person, while 朝 leans toward physical facing and gesture. See the disambiguation note below for the working rule.

向前 xiàngqián forward; toward the front; onward
ADV/ADJ 副词 fùcí
向 xiàng (toward) + 前 qián (front). Directed at the space ahead: 向前走 "walk forward," 向前看 "look ahead," both literally and in the sense of looking to the future rather than dwelling on the past.
请大家向前走。
Qǐng dàjiā xiàng qián zǒu.
Everyone please move forward.
向 xiàng toward; to; in the direction of (preposition)
PREP 介词 jiècí
The bare preposition, placed before a target to aim the verb: 向他借钱 "borrow money from him," 向南走 "walk south." Marks both the goal an action heads toward and the person a request is directed at.
他向我招手。
Tā xiàng wǒ zhāoshǒu.
He waved toward me.
我向他借了一本书。
Wǒ xiàng tā jiè le yī běn shū.
I borrowed a book from him.
朝向 cháoxiàng to face toward; the aspect or facing of (a building)
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
朝 cháo (to face) + 向 xiàng (toward). To face a given way, or the facing itself. Common in describing buildings: 这套房子朝向南 "this flat faces south," a prized aspect for sunlight in Chinese homes.
这间屋子朝向花园。
Zhè jiān wūzi cháoxiàng huāyuán.
This room faces the garden.
辨析 biànxī · 向 vs. 朝 For physical facing the two are interchangeable: 向南 and 朝南 both mean "facing south." The split: 向 is the one used to direct speech, requests, and learning at a person (向他借钱, 向老师请教), where 朝 sounds wrong. 朝 leans toward bodily facing and gesture toward something physical.
向往 xiàngwǎng Inclination & Yearning
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

When the thing that faces is the heart rather than a wall, 向 turns inward into inclination. 倾向 qīngxiàng is the way a mind leans, a tendency or bias; 向往 xiàngwǎng is the heart turned toward a place or life it longs for; 志向 zhìxiàng is the direction of one's ambition, the aspiration one is set on. In each, 向 keeps its core meaning of a steady facing and applies it to feeling: not where the body points, but where the desire does.

倾向 qīngxiàng tendency; inclination; to lean toward
V/N 动名词 dòngmíngcí
倾 qīng (to lean; to tilt) + 向 xiàng (toward). The way a person or thing leans, a tendency or bias: 政治倾向 "political leanings." As a verb, to be inclined toward: 我倾向于同意 "I'm inclined to agree."
他有暴力倾向。
Tā yǒu bàolì qīngxiàng.
He has violent tendencies.
向往 xiàngwǎng to yearn for; to long for; to look forward to
V 动词 dòngcí
向 xiàng (toward) + 往 wǎng (to go toward). The heart facing a place or life it longs for: 向往自由 "to yearn for freedom," 向往美好的生活 "to long for a good life." Warmer and more aspirational than plain "to want."
他一直向往大海。
Tā yīzhí xiàngwǎng dàhǎi.
He has always yearned for the sea.
志向 zhìxiàng aspiration; ambition; the goal one is set on
N 名词 míngcí
志 zhì (will; resolve) + 向 xiàng (direction). The direction of one's will, what one aspires to: 远大的志向 "lofty aspirations." The facing of ambition, where a person has set their life.
年轻人应该有志向。
Niánqīng rén yīnggāi yǒu zhìxiàng.
Young people should have aspirations.
一向 yīxiàng All Along: the Temporal 向
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

The last sense bends direction onto time. To say a habit has faced one way up to the present is to say it has held all along. 一向 yīxiàng and 向来 xiànglái both mean "all along, hitherto," describing a settled state that has run unbroken from the past up to now: 他一向很准时 "he has always been punctual," 这里向来安静 "it has always been quiet here." The 向 here points back along time to the speaking moment, the way the window points along a line of sight.

一向 yīxiàng all along; consistently; up to now
ADV 副词 fùcí
一 yī (one; throughout) + 向 xiàng (facing back along). A habit or state held unbroken up to the present: 他一向如此 "he has always been like this." Marks long-standing consistency, not a one-off.
她一向很努力。
Tā yīxiàng hěn nǔlì.
She has always worked hard.
向来 xiànglái always (up to now); from the start; hitherto
ADV 副词 fùcí
向 xiàng (facing back along) + 来 lái (coming up to now). A close synonym of 一向, slightly more literary: 他向来诚实 "he has always been honest." Frames a quality as having held from the beginning to the present.
这家店向来很受欢迎。
Zhè jiā diàn xiànglái hěn shòu huānyíng.
This shop has always been very popular.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
所向披靡 suǒ xiàng pī mǐ all before one is swept aside: to carry all before one, irresistible Lit: wherever-faced, grass-flattens. 所向 is "wherever one turns," 披靡 the way grass bends flat before the wind. Said of an army or a force so strong that everything in its path gives way.
欣欣向荣 xīn xīn xiàng róng flourishing and thriving: full of vigor and growth Lit: joyful-joyful, facing-flourishing. 欣欣 is the look of glad, vigorous growth; 向荣 is turning toward flourishing. Said of plants in full bloom, and by extension of a thriving enterprise or society.
人心向背 rén xīn xiàng bèi whether hearts face toward or turn away: popular support or opposition Lit: people's-hearts, facing-or-backing. 向 is to face toward (support), 背 to turn one's back (oppose). The phrase names the balance of public sentiment that decides whether a cause or a ruler will stand.
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

See a window cut high in a wall, opening one fixed way. That single facing is the whole character. Everything 向 means is something turned in a settled direction.

Point the window at the compass and it is direction (方向, 风向). Point it as an arrow before a target and it is the preposition toward (向前, 向他借钱). Point the heart through it and it is inclination, the longing for what lies beyond (倾向, 向往). And point it back along the corridor of time and it is the habit held all along, up to this very moment (一向, 向来). Whenever you meet 向, ask which way the window faces.
