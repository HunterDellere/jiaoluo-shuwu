字源 zìyuán Etymology & Structure
字源洞见 zìyuán dòngjiàn · Etymological Insight

因 yīn shows a person lying inside an enclosure. The outer 囗 (wéi, an enclosed space) frames a 大 (dà, a person with arms and legs outstretched) resting within it. The early reading is concrete: a person stretched out on a mat or bed, the thing one lies upon. From "the mat one rests on" came the sense of a support, a basis, the ground one leans on. That is the hinge of the whole character: what you rely on becomes what a thing rests upon, and what a thing rests upon is its cause.

Three senses branch from the figure on the mat:

1. Cause, reason. The basis behind an outcome, what a result rests on: 原因 yuányīn (cause; reason), 因素 yīnsù (factor), 起因 qǐyīn (the cause that starts a thing).
2. Because of, due to. Used as a connective to name the reason for something: 因为 yīnwèi (because), 因此 yīncǐ (therefore; for this reason).
3. To follow, to go along with. The older sense of leaning on a given ground: to take an existing thing as one's basis and act accordingly, as in 因地制宜 yīn dì zhì yí (adapt measures to local conditions).

The thread is the act of resting upon. A mat is what the body rests on; a basis is what an action rests on; a cause is what an effect rests on. Whether 因 names the reason, the connective that points to it, or the act of following an existing ground, it keeps the same image: one thing leaning on another that came before it.

原因 yuányīn Cause & Reason
原因 yuányīn cause; reason
N 名词 míngcí
原 yuán (origin; source) + 因 yīn (cause). The standard noun for the cause or reason behind something: 失败的原因 "the reason for the failure." Its pair is 结果 jiéguǒ, the result.
这次事故的原因还不清楚。
Zhè cì shìgù de yuányīn hái bù qīngchu.
The cause of this accident is still unclear.
他迟到的原因是堵车。
Tā chídào de yuányīn shì dǔchē.
The reason he was late is the traffic jam.
辨析 biànxī · 原因 vs. 理由 原因 is the objective cause, what actually brought a result about, neutral and factual. 理由 lǐyóu is the justification a person gives, the grounds offered to explain or defend a choice. A delay has an 原因 (the traffic); a person offers a 理由 for being late (an excuse or a reason they put forward).
因素 yīnsù factor; element (contributing to a result)
N 名词 míngcí
因 yīn (cause) + 素 sù (element). One of the contributing causes that feed into an outcome, used of the many partial causes behind a complex result: 经济因素 "economic factors," 不安全因素 "safety hazards."
天气是影响收成的重要因素。
Tiānqì shì yǐngxiǎng shōuchéng de zhòngyào yīnsù.
Weather is an important factor affecting the harvest.
起因 qǐyīn the cause; what set something off
N 名词 míngcí
起 qǐ (to rise; to start) + 因 yīn (cause). The cause that started a chain of events, the spark that set a matter in motion: 事情的起因 "the origin of the matter."
这场争论的起因是一个误会。
Zhè chǎng zhēnglùn de qǐyīn shì yí ge wùhuì.
The cause of this argument was a misunderstanding.
因为 yīnwèi Because & Therefore
语法洞见 yǔfǎ dòngjiàn · Grammatical Insight

The busiest modern job of 因 is as a connective that names a reason. 因为 yīnwèi introduces the cause clause and pairs naturally with 所以 suǒyǐ in the result clause: 因为下雨，所以我们没去 "because it rained, we didn't go." The cause comes first, marked by 因为; the result follows, often marked by 所以. Either marker can be dropped once the relationship is clear.

The mirror image is 因此 yīncǐ, literally "because of this," which sits at the head of the result clause and points back at a cause already stated: 他病了，因此没来上班 "he was ill, and therefore did not come to work." Its close relative 因而 yīn'ér works the same way in more written registers. Where 因为 looks forward to a cause it is about to give, 因此 and 因而 look back at a cause already given and draw the conclusion. The literary 由于 yóuyú overlaps with 因为 but leans formal and prefers the front of a sentence.

因为 yīnwèi because; for the reason that
CONJ 连词 liáncí
因 yīn (cause) + 为 wèi (for the sake of). The everyday "because," introducing the reason clause and pairing with 所以 suǒyǐ in the result. It can also follow the result: 我没去，因为下雨了 "I didn't go, because it rained."
因为太累了，他早早睡了。
Yīnwèi tài lèi le, tā zǎozao shuì le.
Because he was too tired, he went to sleep early.
因此 yīncǐ therefore; for this reason; consequently
CONJ 连词 liáncí
因 yīn (because of) + 此 cǐ (this). "Because of this," opening the result clause and pointing back at a cause just stated. More written and conclusive than the spoken 所以.
价格上涨，因此销量下降了。
Jiàgé shàngzhǎng, yīncǐ xiāoliàng xiàjiàng le.
Prices rose, and therefore sales fell.
因而 yīn'ér thus; as a result; consequently
CONJ 连词 liáncí
因 yīn (because of) + 而 ér (and so). A written connective that draws a result from a stated cause, close to 因此 but smoother in flowing prose: 准备充分，因而很顺利 "well prepared, and so it went smoothly."
他工作努力，因而得到了提升。
Tā gōngzuò nǔlì, yīn'ér dédào le tíshēng.
He worked hard, and as a result got a promotion.
因果 yīnguǒ Cause and Effect: the 因 / 果 Frame
语义洞见 yǔyì dòngjiàn · Semantic Insight

因 has a natural partner in 果 guǒ, "fruit, result." Together they name the whole arc from cause to consequence: 因果 yīnguǒ, cause and effect. The image is agricultural and apt: 因 is the seed-ground you rest a thing on, 果 is the fruit that grows out of it. What you plant in the 因 is what you reap in the 果.

The pair carries weight in Buddhist thought, where 因果 names the law of karma: every deed is a cause that ripens into a result, and 种因得果 "plant the cause, gain the fruit" captures the moral physics of action and consequence. In ordinary speech 因果关系 yīnguǒ guānxì simply means "a causal relationship," the link a scientist or a detective tries to establish. The four-character 前因后果 qián yīn hòu guǒ stretches the frame across time: the causes before and the effects after, the full story from start to finish.

因果 yīnguǒ cause and effect; (Buddhism) karma
N 名词 míngcí
因 yīn (cause) + 果 guǒ (fruit; result). The cause-and-effect pair as a single concept, from the everyday 因果关系 "causal relationship" to the Buddhist law of karma, where every act ripens into its fruit.
科学研究讲究因果关系。
Kēxué yánjiū jiǎngjiu yīnguǒ guānxì.
Scientific research is concerned with cause-and-effect relationships.
由于 yóuyú due to; owing to; because of
CONJ 连词 liáncí
由 yóu (from) + 于 yú (at; in). A formal, written near-synonym of 因为, marking the cause at the front of a sentence: 由于天气原因，航班取消了 "owing to the weather, the flight was cancelled." It pairs with 因此 rather than 所以.
由于时间有限，我们简单介绍一下。
Yóuyú shíjiān yǒuxiàn, wǒmen jiǎndān jièshào yíxià.
Owing to limited time, we will give just a brief introduction.
成语 chéngyǔ Idioms & Set Phrases
因地制宜 yīn dì zhì yí adapt measures to local conditions: act according to what the place calls for Lit: follow-the-land, make-it-fitting. 因 here is the older sense, to take an existing ground as one's basis. The phrase counsels suiting your plan to local circumstances rather than forcing one rigid method everywhere.
因材施教 yīn cái shī jiào teach according to aptitude: tailor the lesson to each student Lit: follow-the-talent, give-teaching. A principle associated with Confucius, who answered the same question differently for different disciples. To teach 因材施教 is to fit the instruction to the learner rather than treat all alike.
因小失大 yīn xiǎo shī dà lose much over a trifle: sacrifice the great for the sake of the small Lit: because-of-the-small, lose-the-big. A warning against penny-wise foolishness: chasing a minor gain or saving while letting something far more valuable slip away.
前因后果 qián yīn hòu guǒ the causes before and the effects after: the whole story from start to finish Lit: front-cause, back-result. The full chain of a matter laid out in order: what led up to it and what came of it. 把前因后果说清楚 "to explain the whole sequence clearly."
记忆法 jìyìfǎ · Master Retention Image

See the enclosure 囗 as a mat, and the 大 inside it as a person stretched out, resting upon it. That is the literal picture: a body lying on what supports it. Hold the idea of resting-upon and every sense of 因 follows from it.

What the body rests on is a support; what an action rests on is its basis; what an effect rests on is its cause (原因). When you point at that cause to explain a result, 因 becomes the connective: because (因为), and therefore (因此). And when you take an existing ground and build on it instead of fighting it, 因 is to follow, as in adapting to local conditions (因地制宜). Plant the cause and you reap the fruit, 果: that is the whole 因果 arc, from the mat at the bottom to the harvest at the end.
