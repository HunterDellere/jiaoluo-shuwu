合同 hétong The Contract Itself
字面意思 zìmiàn yìsi · Agreeing Together

合同 (hétong) is "join and match," from the old practice of writing an agreement twice and splitting the paper, so each side's half matched the other. A Chinese factory contract is usually short, and what is left out is decided later by whoever has the leverage. Three questions decide how much a contract protects you: which language version governs, whether it is sealed by the right legal entity, and where a dispute will be heard. This page covers the vocabulary for each. It is a vocabulary guide, not legal advice; have a lawyer who practises in China read anything that matters.

以中文版为准 yǐ Zhōngwén bǎn wéi zhǔn the Chinese version governs
A bilingual contract needs a clause saying which version prevails if they conflict (如有冲突 rú yǒu chōngtū). Chinese courts and arbitrators work in Chinese, so the Chinese text is the one that will be read. Have it translated back to you independently rather than trusting that the two versions match.
本合同中英文各一份，如有冲突，以中文版为准。
Běn hétong Zhōng-Yīngwén gè yí fèn, rú yǒu chōngtū, yǐ Zhōngwén bǎn wéi zhǔn.
This contract is in Chinese and English; in case of conflict, the Chinese version governs.
条款 tiáokuǎn clause, term
第五条 (dì-wǔ tiáo) is Article 5. 补充协议 (bǔchōng xiéyì) is a supplementary agreement, the usual way to add terms later. 附件 (fùjiàn) is an annex, where specifications and drawings belong.
技术要求写在附件一里，附件和合同具有同等效力。
Jìshù yāoqiú xiě zài fùjiàn yī lǐ, fùjiàn hé hétong jùyǒu tóngděng xiàolì.
The technical requirements are in Annex 1, which has the same force as the contract.
公章 gōngzhāng company seal
A Chinese company binds itself with its red seal more than with a signature. 合同专用章 (hétong zhuānyòngzhāng) is a seal used only for contracts and is equally valid. Check that the name on the seal matches the name on the 营业执照 (yíngyè zhízhào, business licence) and the 统一社会信用代码 (the 18-character company registration code). A seal of a different company, or a 部门章 (department seal), is a warning sign.
合同要盖公司公章或者合同专用章，法人签字。
Hétong yào gài gōngsī gōngzhāng huòzhě hétong zhuānyòngzhāng, fǎrén qiānzì.
The contract must carry the company seal or the contract seal, signed by the legal representative.
能发一下营业执照吗？我们核对一下公司名称。
Néng fā yíxià yíngyè zhízhào ma? Wǒmen héduì yíxià gōngsī míngchēng.
Could you send your business licence? We'd like to check the company name.
知识产权 zhīshi chǎnquán IP and Confidentiality
先申请先得 xiān shēnqǐng xiān dé · First to File Wins

China awards patents and trademarks to whoever files first, not to whoever used the design first. A registration in your home country gives you nothing in China. The defensive steps are to register your trademark (商标 shāngbiāo) in China, in Chinese characters as well as Latin letters, before you show a design around, and to sign a confidentiality agreement written for Chinese law. A Western-style NDA alone is weak; the common form for China is the NNN agreement: 不使用、不泄露、不规避 (no use, no disclosure, no circumvention), in Chinese, governed by Chinese law, enforceable in a Chinese court.

保密协议 bǎomì xiéyì confidentiality agreement
The general term for an NDA. NNN协议 is the China-specific form. Ask for it to be signed and sealed before you send drawings, not after.
发图纸之前，我们需要先签一份保密协议。
Fā túzhǐ zhīqián, wǒmen xūyào xiān qiān yí fèn bǎomì xiéyì.
Before we send drawings, we need to sign a confidentiality agreement.
专利 zhuānlì patent
China has three kinds: 发明专利 (fāmíng zhuānlì) invention patents, 实用新型 (shíyòng xīnxíng) utility models for structural improvements, and 外观设计 (wàiguān shèjì) design patents for appearance. Design patents and utility models are faster and cheaper to obtain, and are the ones most often used against copies of consumer products.
这个外观我们已经在中国申请了外观设计专利。
Zhège wàiguān wǒmen yǐjīng zài Zhōngguó shēnqǐng le wàiguān shèjì zhuānlì.
We've already filed a design patent in China for this appearance.
模具归属 mújù guīshǔ tooling ownership
The clause that states who owns the mold, that it may only be used for your orders, and that the factory must release it on request. 归 is "belongs to," 所有权 (suǒyǒuquán) ownership.
模具所有权归买方，未经书面同意，不得为第三方生产。
Mújù suǒyǒuquán guī mǎifāng, wèi jīng shūmiàn tóngyì, bùdé wèi dì-sān fāng shēngchǎn.
The buyer owns the tooling; without written consent it may not be used to produce for any third party.
合作结束的话，模具要无条件交还给我们。
Hézuò jiéshù de huà, mújù yào wú tiáojiàn jiāohuán gěi wǒmen.
If the partnership ends, the mold is to be returned to us unconditionally.
违约 wéiyuē Breach and Disputes
违约金 wéiyuējīn liquidated damages
A fixed sum payable on breach, such as late delivery. Under Chinese law a court or arbitrator can reduce it if it is far above the actual loss, so set it at a level you can justify. 赔偿 (péicháng) is compensation for actual loss.
每延迟一天，按订单金额的千分之五支付违约金。
Měi yánchí yì tiān, àn dìngdān jīn'é de qiān fēn zhī wǔ zhīfù wéiyuējīn.
For each day of delay, liquidated damages of 0.5% of the order value are payable.
质保期 zhìbǎoqī warranty period
How long the factory stands behind the goods. Pair it with 验收标准 (yànshōu biāozhǔn), the acceptance standard, normally the sealed sample and an AQL level.
质保期为出货后十二个月，验收标准以封样为准。
Zhìbǎoqī wéi chūhuò hòu shí'èr ge yuè, yànshōu biāozhǔn yǐ fēngyàng wéi zhǔn.
The warranty is twelve months from shipment; acceptance is judged against the sealed sample.
仲裁 zhòngcái arbitration
A foreign court judgment is hard to enforce in China, while an arbitration award can be enforced under the New York Convention. Many contracts name 中国国际经济贸易仲裁委员会 (CIETAC) or the Hong Kong (HKIAC) or Singapore (SIAC) centres. 管辖 (guǎnxiá) is jurisdiction; 适用法律 (shìyòng fǎlǜ) the governing law.
因本合同发生的争议，提交中国国际经济贸易仲裁委员会仲裁。
Yīn běn hétong fāshēng de zhēngyì, tíjiāo Zhōngguó Guójì Jīngjì Màoyì Zhòngcái Wěiyuánhuì zhòngcái.
Disputes arising from this contract are submitted to CIETAC for arbitration.
