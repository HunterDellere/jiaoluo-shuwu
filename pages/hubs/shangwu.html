shāngwù Visiting a Chinese Factory: A Reading Path

The business Chinese for a buyer's factory visit, in the order you will need it: the meeting, the floor, the product, the price, the contract, problems, the tour, the dinner, and the follow-up.

How to Use This Path

商务 shāngwù · Business Affairs

商务 is "commercial affairs," the word on business visas and business-class tickets. This path collects the pages written for one situation: a foreign buyer visiting a Chinese manufacturer, sitting through the meeting, walking the floor, negotiating terms, and keeping the relationship going afterwards. Read the first stage before the trip, the second before any discussion of price or contracts, and the third the night before the visit. The cheat sheet below is the short list to have on your phone on the day.

Day-of Cheat Sheet

会议 huìyì · In the Meeting 今天想谈三件事 jīntiān xiǎng tán sān jiàn shì → today I'd like to cover three things
我确认一下 wǒ quèrèn yíxià → let me confirm
能写下来吗？ néng xiě xiàlái ma? → could you write it down?
取决于什么？ qǔjué yú shénme? → what does it depend on?
具体是哪天？ jùtǐ shì nǎ tiān? → which day exactly?
价格 jiàgé · On Price 含税还是不含税？ hánshuì háishi bù hánshuì? → with tax or without?
FOB哪个港口？ FOB nǎge gǎngkǒu? → FOB which port?
报价有效期多久？ bàojià yǒuxiàoqī duō jiǔ? → how long is the quote valid?
起订量是每个颜色吗？ qǐdìngliàng shì měi ge yánsè ma? → is the MOQ per colour?
如果我们下一万个，单价是多少？ rúguǒ wǒmen xià yíwàn ge, dānjià shì duōshao? → if we order 10,000, what is the unit price?
工厂 gōngchǎng · On the Floor 这道工序是自己做还是外发？ zhè dào gōngxù shì zìjǐ zuò háishi wàifā? → do you do this step in-house or send it out?
模具归谁？ mújù guī shéi? → who owns the mold?
交期从什么时候算？ jiāoqī cóng shénme shíhou suàn? → when does the lead time start?
大货以封样为准 dàhuò yǐ fēngyàng wéi zhǔn → bulk must match the sealed sample
可以拍照吗？ kěyǐ pāizhào ma? → may I take photos?
饭桌和之后 fànzhuō hé zhīhòu · At Dinner and After 我以茶代酒 wǒ yǐ chá dài jiǔ → I'll toast with tea
我随意，您随意 wǒ suíyì, nín suíyì → I'll sip, you drink as you like
谢谢您的热情招待 xièxie nín de rèqíng zhāodài → thank you for your warm hospitality
我们加个微信吧 wǒmen jiā ge Wēixìn ba → let's add each other on WeChat
会议纪要如下，如有出入请指出 huìyì jìyào rúxià, rú yǒu chūrù qǐng zhǐchū → minutes below; please point out anything that differs
