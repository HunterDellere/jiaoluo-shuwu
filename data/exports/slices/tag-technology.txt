//角落書屋/Slices/Technology
本草	běn cǎo	Materia medica — Materia medica: the traditional term for the pharmacological literature and its subject matter.
本草纲目	běn cǎo gāng mù	the materia medica
蚕丝	cán sī	Silk thread , literally "silkworm thread — Silk thread , literally "silkworm thread." The raw fiber before weaving. 蚕 is the silkworm; 丝 is the thread.
车次	chē cì	train number — G-trains are the fastest, D-trains next. 检票口 (jiǎnpiàokǒu) is the boarding gate, shown on the screens next to the train number; 车厢 (chēxiāng) is the carriage, 座位号 the seat number.
地铁	dì tiě	subway
滴滴	Dī dī	Didi ride-hailing — The main ride app, also usable inside Alipay and WeChat. Industrial zones have confusing addresses, so ask the factory to send a 定位 (dìngwèi, location pin) and set that as the destination. 上车点 (shàngchēdiǎn) is the pickup point.
点赞	diǎn zàn	To like (a post) — To like (a post): tap-approve. 赞 alone means to praise; 点赞之交 ("a likes-only friendship") names the acquaintance you never see but always like.
电脑	diàn nǎo	Computer — Computer: electric brain. 台式电脑 is a desktop, 笔记本 a laptop. The formal 计算机 persists in degree names and government documents.
毒性	dú xìng	Toxicity, one of the key properties documented for each drug in the Bencao Gangmu. Li distinguished between non-toxic (无毒), slightly toxic (小毒), moderately toxic (有毒), and highly toxic (大毒) substances, with corresponding cautions about dosage, preparation, and contraindications.
二维码	èr wéi mǎ	QR code
分子	fēn zǐ	Molecule — Molecule: divided unit. The same word, differently stressed, means "member of a group" in political vocabulary (知识分子, intellectuals), a coincidence of compounding worth knowing before it confuses.
感谢	gǎn xiè	to thank sincerely; gratitude (formal) — 感 gǎn + 谢 xiè (to thank; originally: water overflowing, pouring out one's feelings). Warmer and more sincere than 谢谢. Standard in writing, speeches, and formal situations. Also functions as a noun in 表示感谢.
干支	gān zhī	Stems and Branches — Stems and Branches: the shorthand for the combined system of Ten Heavenly Stems and Twelve Earthly Branches used to name years, months, days, and two-hour periods.
高铁	gāo tiě	high-speed rail — Trains are booked on the official 12306 app or site (it has an English version) or through travel apps, and your passport is the ticket: it is checked at the station entrance and at the gate. 动车 (dòngchē) are slightly slower bullet trains. Arrive early; large stations have security checks.
工程	gōng chéng	engineering
工程师	gōng chéng shī	engineer — 工程 gōngchéng (engineering; project , lit. "work + process") + 师 shī (master; teacher; professional). Engineer , one of the most respected professional titles in China, associated with the nation's modernization drive. 工程 alone means a large project or undertaking: 三峡工程 = Three Gorges Project.
红包	hóng bāo	red envelope; a cash gift; a digital money packet — 红 hóng + 包 bāo (packet; bundle). The red paper envelope filled with money, given to children at New Year, to a couple at their wedding, and to employees as a bonus. The red is the point: the envelope blesses the money inside.
护照	hù zhào	passport
化学	huà xu é	Chemistry — Chemistry: transformation-study. 化学反应 is a chemical reaction; 化学式 (huàxuéshì) is a chemical formula, read left to right like grammar.
黄历	huáng lì	The almanac — The almanac: literally "yellow calendar," named for the imperial yellow of the official calendar. An annual publication (now also a mobile app feature) that gives day-by-day auspiciousness ratings based on the traditional calendrical system.
回复	huí fù	to reply
会议纪要	huì yì jì yào	meeting minutes — A short written summary of decisions, owners, and dates, sent the same day. Offering to write it yourself means your version becomes the record. 白纸黑字 (báizhǐ hēizì, "black ink on white paper") is the idiom for having it in writing.
火车站	huǒ chē zhàn	railway station
加微信	jiā Wēi xìn	to add on WeChat — 扫一扫 (sǎo yi sǎo) is "scan"; one person shows their QR code (二维码 èrwéimǎ), the other scans. 我扫你 means "I'll scan yours." 拉群 (lā qún) is to create a group chat, the norm for an order with several people on each side.
建筑	jiàn zhù	Architecture, building — Architecture, building. 建筑师 (jiànzhùshī) is an architect, distinct from the 工程师 who makes the building stand up. As a verb, 建 alone means to build: 建一座桥, build a bridge.
节日祝福	jié rì zhù fú	holiday greetings — A short greeting at Chinese New Year and the Mid-Autumn Festival maintains the relationship between orders. 新年快乐 or 中秋快乐 with a line of thanks is enough.
进度	jìn dù	progress — 跟进 (gēnjìn) is to follow up, 催 (cuī) to chase. Ask for photos as well as words; a picture of goods on the line settles more than 快了 (kuài le, "nearly there").
九九表	jiǔ jiǔ biǎo	The multiplication table — The multiplication table: memorized in China as a chant of 45 compressed phrases from 一一得一 (1×1=1) to 九九八十一 (9×9=81). The chant begins with the smaller number, which is why it runs from 1×1 up rather than 9×9 down as in some other traditions.
酒店	jiǔ diàn	hotel
君臣佐使	jūn chén zuǒ shǐ	Ruler, minister, assistant, envoy — Ruler, minister, assistant, envoy: the four functional roles within a compound herbal formula.
科学	kē xu é	science; scientific — 科 kē (branch; category; department) + 学 xué (learning; -ology). Lit. "categorized learning" , the systematic division of knowledge into disciplines. Coined as a translation for Western "science" in the late 19th century.
绫罗绸缎	líng luó chóu duàn	The four major silk fabric types (gauze, thin silk, thick silk, satin) often used together as a set phrase meaning luxury silk goods in general. An elegant way to say "silk" in classical and literary contexts.
农历	nóng lì	the lunar calendar — 农 (farming) plus 历 (calendar). The traditional lunisolar calendar, named for farmers because its solar terms tell you when to plant and harvest. 春节 (chūnjié) falls on 农历 New Year.
朋友圈	péng you quān	circle of friends; WeChat Moments
桥梁	qiáo liáng	bridge; bridging structure — Bridge, in the formal engineering register (spoken Chinese just says 桥). Used figuratively exactly as in English: a person or institution can be a 桥梁 between two sides.
钦天监	qīn tiān jiàn	Imperial Observatory / Bureau of Astronomy — Imperial Observatory / Bureau of Astronomy: the government department responsible for astronomical observation, calendar production, and astrological consultation in imperial China.
确认	qu èr èn	to confirm
入住	rù zhù	to check in — Hotels must register foreign guests' passports, and not every hotel is licensed to take foreigners, so book one that confirms it does. 押金 (yājīn) is the deposit, 退房 (tuìfáng) checkout. For a company invoice, give the 抬头 (táitóu, the invoice name).
软件	ruǎn jiàn	software — 软 ruǎn (soft) + 件 jiàn (a piece). The intangible programs that run on a computer, paired against 硬件 yìngjiàn (hardware), 硬 yìng meaning hard. The soft and hard contrast is a direct calque of the English pair, with 件 supplying the shared unit-of-a-system sense.
闰月	rùn yu è	Intercalary month, the extra month inserted approximately every 2–3 years (7 times in 19 years) to reconcile the lunar and solar cycles. A year with a 闰月 has 13 months.
扫码	sǎo mǎ	to scan a code — 您扫我还是我扫您? (Do you scan me or do I scan you?) is the everyday question at a till.
上网	shàng wǎng	to go online; to use the internet — 上 shàng (to mount; to get onto) + 网 wǎng (net; network; the internet). To mount the internet. The spatial metaphor is the same as getting on a vehicle or into an institution: you step up onto a platform. Formed in the early 1990s as Chinese internet usage began, and immediately adopted the 上 frame.
设计	shè jì	To design; a design — To design; a design. 设计师 (shèjìshī) covers designers from fashion to industrial. In engineering documents, 设计院 (design institute) is the state-descended firm that produces the drawings.
施工	shī gōng	To carry out construction — To carry out construction: "execute the work." The word on every fence and detour sign. 施工中 means "under construction," on streets and, borrowed, on websites.
实验	shí yàn	Experiment — Experiment: to test against reality. 做实验 is to run an experiment; 实验室 is the laboratory; 实验中学 is the "experimental high school" found in nearly every Chinese city.
收到	shōu dào	received, noted — The standard acknowledgement. 好的 (hǎo de) is "okay," 明白 (míngbai) "understood," 收到，谢谢 the polite version. Acknowledging promptly is expected; silence reads as a problem.
手机	shǒu jī	mobile phone — lit. "hand machine" — 手 shǒu (hand) + 机 jī (machine). China's most common word for mobile phone , the machine held in the hand. Completely replaced the earlier 手提电话 and the formal 移动电话 in everyday usage. 玩手机 wán shǒujī = "to play on one's phone" (ubiquitous activity).
数学	shù xu é	Mathematics — Mathematics: number-study. 数学家 (shùxuéjiā) is a mathematician; 数学课 (shùxué kè) is math class, the collocation every student uses daily.
数字	shù zì	Number, numeral, digit — Number, numeral, digit. The everyday word for a figure, whether spoken, written, or on a screen. 数字化 (shùzìhuà) extends it to "digitization," the same move English made with "digit."
司机	sī jī	driver
四大发明	sì dà fā míng	the Four Great Inventions
四气五味	sì qì wǔ wèi	Four temperatures and five flavors — Four temperatures and five flavors: the two-axis system for classifying drug properties. The four temperatures: cold (寒), cool (凉), warm (温), hot (热). The five flavors: sour (酸), bitter (苦), sweet (甘), pungent (辛), salty (咸). Each flavor has specific organ affinities and physiological effects.
酸	suān	Acid; sour — Acid; sour. One word covers the chemical class and the taste, plus muscle soreness (腿酸, sore legs) and the sting of envy (心里酸酸的). Chemistry students never forget which taste an acid has.
算筹	suàn chóu	Counting rods — Counting rods: the predecessor to the suanpan. Small bamboo, bone, or ivory sticks arranged in patterns on a flat surface to represent numbers in a base-10 positional system. Used in China from at least the Warring States period (475–221 BCE) through the Tang dynasty.
算盘	suàn pán	The abacus, literally "calculating tray — The abacus, literally "calculating tray." The standard Chinese bead-frame instrument with 2 upper beads and 5 lower beads per rod, in use in its canonical form since at least the 16th century.
尾号	wěi hào	last digits (of a phone number) — Drivers confirm the passenger by the last four digits of the phone number: 手机尾号多少? Answer with the digits one by one.
物理	wù lǐ	physics; the principles of matter — 物 wù (thing; matter) + 理 lǐ (principle). Literally "the principles of things," the modern name for physics. A clear case of 理 meaning the lawful pattern running through the physical world.
下载	xià zài	To download — To download: carry down. Paired with 上传 (upload). 载 also reads zǎi meaning "year" in classical texts, a polyphone trap for advanced readers.
氧气	yǎng qì	Oxygen (as breathable gas) — Oxygen (as breathable gas): the nourishing 氧 plus 气. 缺氧 (quēyǎng) is oxygen deprivation, used literally at altitude and figuratively for stuffy rooms and dull meetings.
已读不回	yǐ dú bù huí	read but no reply
邮件	yóu jiàn	mail; email; a postal item — 邮 yóu (post; mail) + 件 jiàn (a piece). Originally any item sent through the post. In everyday modern use it most often means email: 电子邮件 diànzǐ yóujiàn (electronic mail) is regularly shortened to 邮件. To send one is 发邮件 fā yóujiàn.
语音	yǔ yīn	voice message — Factory staff often send long voice messages. 转文字 (zhuǎn wénzì) converts a voice message to text inside WeChat (press and hold the message), and the result is easy to paste into a translator. Asking for text is acceptable when phrased as your limitation.
元素	yuán sù	Element — Element: "original plain-stuff." Beyond the lab it means element in the design sense too: 中国元素 (Chinese elements) is the stock phrase for Chinese motifs in fashion or film.
支付宝	Zhī fù bǎo	Alipay
珠心算	zhū xīn suàn	Abacus mental arithmetic — Abacus mental arithmetic: performing suanpan calculations by visualizing the instrument in the mind. Neurologically distinct from verbal mental arithmetic: brain imaging shows practitioners use visuospatial rather than language regions.
