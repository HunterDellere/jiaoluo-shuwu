//角落書屋/Slices/Doing Business
产	chǎn	to produce, give birth, property, output — From birth to bounty: a character that spans biological generation and industrial output, linking the miracle of birth to the logic of production and wealth.
法	fǎ	law, method, the Dharma — Water against the level mark. From that image of fair, flowing measure grows the whole field of 法: the law and the court, the method and the way of doing a thing, and the Buddhist Dharma itself.
告	gào	to tell, to announce, to report — A mouth aimed upward: the character for delivering words to authority, and the root of advertising, accusations, and warnings.
经	jīng	classic, scripture, to pass through, warp threads — The lengthwise warp threads strung on a loom, the structural through-lines that hold a fabric together. From that image grow the canonical classic, the act of passing through, and the running of an enterprise.
品	pǐn	article, grade, character, to taste — Three mouths stacked: a character about multiplicity, evaluation, and the discerning assessment of quality, from product grades to moral character.
业	yè	occupation, industry, undertaking, study — A notched rack for hanging bells and chimes, then the work hung upon it. From that frame grows the whole field of doing: the trade one practices, the industry one builds, the cause one devotes a life to, the coursework one masters, and in Buddhist thought the karma one accrues.
作	zuò	to do, to make, to compose — A person setting something in motion. From the act of beginning grows the whole vocabulary of doing, making, and composing: the job you work, the work you create, and the role you act out.
产品	chǎn pǐn	product, manufactured good — The standard Chinese word for product: what production yields and commerce moves. From factory floor to digital marketplace, 产品 names the output of organized human effort.
生产	shēng chǎn	to produce, production, manufacturing — The foundational act of economic life: and, in China's Marxist political vocabulary, the act from which all social relations flow. Life gives rise to production; production sustains life.
丝绸之路	sī chóu zhī lù	the Silk Road — The term Silk Road was coined by a German geographer in 1877: the Chinese themselves had no single name for the trade network that defined their contact with the world for a millennium.
安居乐业	ān jū lè yè	to live in peace and be content in one's work: a settled, secure life — Lit: peaceful-dwelling, joyful-work. 安居 is to live securely, 乐业 to take pleasure in one's occupation. The classic image of social well-being: a people housed, employed, and at ease. Often invoked as the aim of good governance.
饱经风霜	bǎo jīng fēng shuāng	to have weathered wind and frost: hardened by hardship — 饱 here means having had a full share of something. The image is a face lined by years of exposure; used of people who have lived through long adversity. Common in literary and journalistic prose.
法不容情	fǎ bù róng qíng	the law allows no room for sentiment: justice must not bend to feeling — Lit: law-not-tolerate-feeling. The principle that the law must be applied evenly, without softening for personal ties or sympathy. The level standard of the water radical made into a maxim.
经久不衰	jīng jiǔ bù shuāi	to endure long without fading: standing the test of time — Lit: pass-through long, not declining. Said of a song, a brand, a custom, or a reputation that stays popular and vigorous over many years rather than fading away.
兢兢业业	jīng jīng yè yè	diligent and careful in one's work — A phrase of high praise for someone who does their work with constant care and seriousness, never cutting corners. 兢兢 conveys cautious diligence; 业业 the steady attention to one's 业 (work, duties). Common in performance reviews and tributes, the model of the conscientious worker.
漫不经心	màn bù jīng xīn	careless and inattentive: with no thought passing through the mind — Lit: idly not passing-through the heart. 经心 means to take to heart, to attend to. To do something 漫不经心 is to do it absent-mindedly, paying it no real attention.
为非作歹	wéi fēi zuò dǎi	to do evil; to commit crimes and wrongdoing — Lit: do-wrong-make-evil. 非 (wrong) and 歹 dǎi (bad, evil) pair up to name flagrant wrongdoing. Said of those who run riot doing harm: 为非作歹的歹徒 "evildoers who run amok."
想方设法	xiǎng fāng shè fǎ	to think of squares and devise methods: to try every possible means — Lit: think-method-set-up-method. To rack one's brains for a way through, doing whatever it takes. 方 and 法 here both carry the "method" sense, doubled for emphasis.
业精于勤	yè jīng yú qín	mastery of one's work comes from diligence: skill is born of effort — Lit: work, refined-through, diligence. From Han Yu's essay on study: 业精于勤，荒于嬉, "mastery comes from diligence and is ruined by play." A maxim that one's craft or learning is perfected only through steady, hard application.
一言为定	yì yán wéi dìng	one word and it is settled: a deal is a deal — Lit: one-word-makes-settled. Said to seal an agreement on the spot: a single word given, and the matter is fixed, with no going back. The everyday way to say "deal, it's settled."
引经据典	yǐn jīng jù diǎn	to cite the classics and quote the canon: to argue from authoritative texts — Lit: draw on classics, rely on canon. Praise for someone whose argument is grounded in scripture and the classic texts, though it can also tease a person for being bookishly pedantic.
知法犯法	zhī fǎ fàn fǎ	to know the law and break it anyway: to offend with full awareness — Lit: know-law-violate-law. Said of someone who understands exactly what the law forbids and breaks it regardless, which makes the offense worse, not more excusable.
执法如山	zhí fǎ rú shān	to enforce the law like a mountain: to uphold it firmly and without bending — Lit: enforce-law-like-mountain. Praise for an official or judge who applies the law unshakably, immovable as a mountain, favoring no one and yielding to no pressure.
装模作样	zhuāng mú zuò yàng	to fake a shape and put on a show: to playact, to put on airs — Lit: feign-mould-make-shape. To strike a false pose, going through the motions of sincerity or effort while meaning none of it. A pointed way to call out pretense.
自作自受	zì zuò zì shòu	what you make, you suffer: to reap the consequences of your own actions — Lit: self-do-self-bear. The Chinese counterpart of "you made your bed, now lie in it." Said of trouble that a person has brought entirely upon themselves.
作茧自缚	zuò jiǎn zì fù	to spin a cocoon and bind oneself: to trap yourself by your own doing — Lit: make-cocoon-self-bind. As a silkworm spins the cocoon that encloses it, a person can create rules, habits, or schemes that end up trapping them.
八大菜系	bā dà cài xì	the Eight Great Cuisines — every regional banquet runs on baijiu
白茶	bái chá	white tea — minimally processed; wither and dry
白酒	bái jiǔ	baijiu — the spirit most often paired with a hotpot meal
白切鸡	bái qiē jī	White-Cut Chicken
班	bān	shift — 两班倒 (liǎng bān dǎo) is two rotating shifts; 加班 (jiābān) overtime. Shifts and headcount tell you real capacity better than a capacity figure does.
办法	bàn fǎ	a way; a means; a solution — 办 bàn (to handle; to manage) + 法 fǎ (way). The practical way to get a concrete thing done or to solve a problem at hand: 想办法 "to think of a way," 没办法 "there's nothing to be done."
办公室	bàng ōng shì	office
帮我翻译一下	bāng wǒ fān yì yí xià	please translate this for me — For your interpreter, or for the English-speaking salesperson when the engineer goes too fast. Using an interpreter for one hard passage and Chinese for the rest is common and costs no face.
包间	bāo jiān	private dining room
包装	bāo zhuāng	packaging — 彩盒 (cǎihé) is the retail colour box, 内盒 the inner box, 外箱 (wàixiāng) the master carton, 装箱数 the units per carton. 唛头 (màitóu), from English "mark," is the shipping mark printed on cartons. 跌落测试 (diēluò cèshì) is the drop test.
保密协议	bǎo mì xié yì	confidentiality agreement — The general term for an NDA. NNN协议 is the China-specific form. Ask for it to be signed and sealed before you send drawings, not after.
报价	bào jià	quotation
报价单	bào jià dān	quotation sheet — 报价 (bàojià) is both the verb "to quote" and the noun "quote." Ask for it in writing, line by line: 分项报价 (fēnxiàng bàojià) is an itemised quote that breaks out the product, packaging, tooling, and printing.
毕业	bì yè	to graduate; graduation — 毕 bì (to finish; to complete) + 业 yè (studies). To complete one's course of study: 大学毕业 "to graduate from university," 毕业典礼 "graduation ceremony." A separable verb, so the object slots in the middle: 毕了业 "have graduated."
便宜一点	pián yi yì diǎn	a bit cheaper — The basic request, best softened. 价格有点高 (the price is a bit high) states the problem without accusing; 还有空间吗 (is there still room?) invites a counteroffer without naming one.
表面处理	biǎo miàn chǔ lǐ	surface finish — The finishing family: 抛光 (pāoguāng) polishing, 电解 (diànjiě) electrolytic polishing for liners, 喷涂 (pēntú) spray painting, 喷粉 (pēnfěn) powder coating, 丝印 (sīyìn) screen printing, 镭雕 (léidiāo) laser engraving. 涂层 (túcéng) is the coating itself; 掉漆 (diàoqī) is paint coming off.
不良率	bù liáng lǜ	defect rate — Percentage of defective units; 良品率 or 良率 (liánglǜ) is the yield, its complement. 不良品 (bùliángpǐn) and 次品 (cìpǐn) are defective goods. Agree an acceptable rate in the contract and what happens above it.
不太会喝酒	bú tài huì hē jiǔ	not much of a drinker — Decide before the dinner whether you drink, and say it early with a reason that leaves no one at fault: health, medication, an early meeting, or driving. Once you have drunk one toast, refusing the next is harder.
不谈生意	bù tán shēng yi	no business talk — Price is rarely negotiated at the table, but the boss may ask a soft question about volume or the future. Answer warmly and without numbers; numbers belong in the meeting room the next day.
不锈钢	bù xiù gāng	stainless steel — Grades by number: 201 is cheaper and less corrosion-resistant, 304 is the common food-contact grade, 316 adds molybdenum for better resistance to acids and salt. 材质 (cáizhì) is "material" in the sense of grade and composition.
菜系	cài xì	cuisine school; culinary lineage — Cuisine system , the term for a recognized regional cooking tradition. The 八大菜系 (eight great cuisine systems) are the canonical eight, though culinary scholars recognize anywhere from four to dozens of regional traditions.
蚕丝	cán sī	Silk thread , literally "silkworm thread — Silk thread , literally "silkworm thread." The raw fiber before weaving. 蚕 is the silkworm; 丝 is the thread.
仓库	cāng kù	warehouse — 原材料仓 raw-material store, 成品仓 finished-goods store. A tidy warehouse with labelled batches (标识 biāoshí) is a good sign of traceability.
叉烧	chā shāo	Char Siu — Cantonese BBQ Pork
茶餐厅	chá cān tīng	Cha chaan teng — Cha chaan teng: the quintessential Hong Kong casual restaurant, serving a fusion menu of Cantonese and Western-influenced dishes: milk tea, pineapple buns (菠萝包 bōluóbāo), egg tarts, congee, rice dishes, and Hong Kong-style French toast.
茶黄素	chá huáng sù	theaflavins — the brightness compounds of black tea
产能	chǎn néng	production capacity — Output per day or month. 排产 (páichǎn) is scheduling an order onto the line; 旺季 (wàngjì) is peak season, when capacity disappears. In the months before Chinese New Year lines fill up and many workers leave early, so lead times stretch.
产品认证	chǎn pǐn rèn zhèng	product certification — 认证 rènzhèng (to certify, certification , 认 recognize + 证 proof/certificate). The system by which products are officially verified to meet standards. 3C认证 (China Compulsory Certification) is the mandatory certification for electronics, appliances, and vehicles sold in China.
产品质量	chǎn pǐn zhì liàng	product quality — 质量 zhìliàng (substance + quantity → quality) is the standard word for quality in both the everyday and technical sense. 产品质量 is the fixed phrase for product quality , appearing in consumer protection law (产品质量法), manufacturing standards, and marketing.
产前样	chǎn qián yàng	pre-production sample (PPS) — Made on the production line with production materials, after tooling and before bulk starts. Hand samples can be perfect while line samples are not; insisting on a 产前样 catches the difference before thousands of units exist.
厂房	chǎng fáng	factory building
厂家	chǎng jiā	manufacturer
车间	chē jiān	workshop, shop floor — Named by process: 冲压车间 stamping, 焊接车间 welding, 注塑车间 injection molding, 喷涂车间 painting, 组装车间 assembly, 包装车间 packing. 无尘车间 (wúchén chējiān) is a clean room.
称	chēng	To weigh — To weigh. 称一下 ("weigh it, please") hands the fruit to the vendor. The same character read chèn means "to fit," as in 称心如意, suiting one's heart's wishes.
成本	chéng běn	cost — What the factory pays, as opposed to 价格 (jiàgé), what you pay. 原材料 (yuáncáiliào, raw materials) and 人工 (réngōng, labour) are the two cost lines you will hear cited when a price goes up. 利润 (lìrùn) is profit.
尺寸	chǐ cùn	dimensions, size — Named after two old units of length, 尺 and 寸. 外径 (wàijìng) is outer diameter, 内径 (nèijìng) inner diameter, 壁厚 (bìhòu) wall thickness, 高度 height.
绸	chóu	silk fabric
出口	chū kǒu	to export; an exit; to speak — 出 chū (exit; emerge) + 口 kǒu (mouth; opening; port). Three interlocking meanings from the same compound. As trade: to export , goods going out through the port. As architecture: the exit of a building or tunnel. As speech: 出口成章 (words emerge perfectly formed , to speak with eloquence).
出口退税	chū kǒu tuì shuì	export VAT rebate — The refund of input VAT on exported goods, claimed by whoever exports them. A factory with its own export licence (自营出口 zìyíng chūkǒu) claims it directly; one without exports through a trading company (外贸公司 wàimào gōngsī), which adds a layer of margin.
川菜	chuān cài	Sichuan cuisine — 川 chuān (Sichuan) + 菜 cài (cuisine). The cooking of Sichuan, famous for the numbing-spicy 麻辣 málà of Sichuan peppercorn and chili. 麻婆豆腐 and 回锅肉 are classic 川菜 dishes.
创业	chuàng yè	to start a business; to found a venture; to pioneer — 创 chuàng (to create; to initiate) + 业 yè (undertaking). To found something from the ground up, whether a company or a larger enterprise: 自主创业 "to start one's own business," 创业公司 "a startup." Carries the flavor of building where nothing stood before.
创作	chuàng zuò	to create (art)
打样	dǎ yàng	to make a sample; sampling — The verb for producing samples before an order. 打样费 (dǎyàngfèi) is the sampling fee, often refunded against the first order; 打样周期 is how long sampling takes.
打折	dǎ zhé	to give a discount — 折 counts tenths of the price you pay, not the amount taken off. 八折 means you pay eight tenths, a 20% discount. Learners hear "eight" and think "80% off." In a negotiation 点 (diǎn, a percentage point) is more common than 折 for small moves.
大货	dà huò	bulk goods, the production run — Floor slang for the main order as opposed to samples. 大货生产 is bulk production, 大货样 a unit pulled from it. The word appears in nearly every merchandiser message: 大货已经上线了 means your order is on the line.
代工	dài gōng	contract manufacturing, OEM
单价	dān jià	unit price — The price per piece; 总价 (zǒngjià) is the total, 金额 (jīn'é) the amount on a line. The usual follow-up is what the unit price includes: 包括 (bāokuò) and 不包括 are the words to listen for.
当作	dàng zuò	to treat as; to regard as; to take for — 当 dàng (to equate) + 作 zuò (to make, to do). To regard X as Y: 把困难当作机会 (treat difficulty as opportunity). A near-synonym is 当成 dàngchéng. Note the falling tone, which marks it apart from level-tone dāng.
点心	diǎn xīn	Dim sum — Dim sum: small Cantonese dishes served with tea in the teahouse tradition. The term literally means "touch the heart." Dim sum culture grew from the Cantonese tea-drinking and commercial meeting tradition of Guangdong and Hong Kong and is now the most internationally recognized format of Chinese com…
电汇	diàn huì	wire transfer (T/T) — "Electric remittance," usually just called T/T. 信用证 (xìnyòngzhèng) is a letter of credit (L/C), slower and more paperwork, with the bank standing between the parties.
订单	dìng dān	order
订金	dìng jīn	advance payment
豆腐	dòu fu	tofu — a cross-cuisine staple appearing in nearly every regional school
敦煌	Dūn huáng	Dunhuang
二维码	èr wéi mǎ	QR code
发酵	fā jiào	oxidation (lit. "fermentation") — the enzymatic browning step
发票	fā piào	official receipt: an invoice; a tax receipt — 发 fā (to issue; to send out) + 票 piào (slip; note). The official, tax-registered receipt a seller issues for a purchase. In China the 发票 is a regulated document used for reimbursement and tax, very different from a plain shop receipt (小票 xiǎopiào).
法官	fǎ guān	judge
法国	Fǎ guó	France
法律	fǎ lǜ	law; statute; legislation — 法 fǎ (law) + 律 lǜ (rule; statute). The body of law as a system, the formal rules a state enacts and enforces. 遵守法律 "to obey the law," 法律问题 "a legal question."
法人	fǎ rén	legal representative
法师	fǎ shī	Dharma master; a learned monk or nun — 法 fǎ (Dharma) + 师 shī (master; teacher). A respectful title for a monk or nun versed in the teaching, qualified to expound it to others. Used before or after a religious name.
法院	fǎ yuàn	court of law; courthouse — 法 fǎ (law) + 院 yuàn (institution; courtyard). The court where law is applied and cases are judged. 人民法院 "the People's Court," 上法院 "to go to court."
反馈	fǎn kuì	feedback
反映问题	fǎn yìng wèn tí	to report a problem — The neutral opener. 客诉 (kèsù) is a complaint from your own customers, a useful word because it shifts the pressure to the market rather than to you.
返工	fǎng ōng	rework — Fixing defective goods. Its harder cousins: 报废 (bàofèi) scrapping, 补货 (bǔhuò) replacing goods, 退货 (tuìhuò) returning them. 整改 (zhěnggǎi) is a corrective action on the process itself, so the defect stops recurring.
方法	fāng fǎ	method; means; way of doing something — 方 fāng (square; direction) + 法 fǎ (way). A method or procedure considered in the abstract, the systematic way to approach a task: 学习方法 "study methods," 科学方法 "the scientific method."
非法	fēi fǎ	illegal; unlawful — 非 fēi (not) + 法 fǎ (law). Outside the law, not lawful: 非法行为 "illegal conduct," 非法移民 "illegal immigration." Its opposite is 合法 héfǎ (lawful).
分担	fēn dān	to share (a cost) — The compromise line. Offering it yourself, once fault is clear, buys goodwill for the next order.
封样	fēng yàng	sealed sample; golden sample — "Seal the sample": approved units are labelled, signed by both sides, and kept by each as the binding quality standard for bulk. Also called 标准样 (biāozhǔnyàng) or 金样 (jīnyàng, golden sample). Keep at least one yourself.
佛法	fó fǎ	the Dharma; Buddhist teaching; the law of the Buddha — 佛 + 法 fǎ (law; method; dharma). The teachings of the Buddha as a whole, understood as both doctrine and practice. Also the specific rendering of Sanskrit dharma, which encompasses cosmic law, the teachings themselves, individual phenomena, and moral truth.
佛教	fó jiào	Buddhism (the religion) — 佛 (Buddha) + 教 jiào (teaching; religion; to teach). The standard modern term for Buddhism as a religion or institutional tradition. Entered China officially during the Han dynasty (around the 1st century CE), though oral transmission and merchant routes likely brought it earlier.
佛经	fó jīng	Buddhist scripture; sutra — 佛 fó (Buddha) + 经 jīng (scripture). The sutras, the recorded discourses of the Buddha. Reciting and copying 佛经 is a long-standing devotional practice, as with the much-loved 心经 xīnjīng (Heart Sutra).
服务	fú wù	service (contrasted with product)
负责	fù zé	to be responsible for — Names the owner of an action. Ask it about every open item before the meeting ends. 对……负责 also means "to be accountable for" a result, which makes it the word of quality disputes.
改模	gǎi mú	to modify a mold — Changing the tool after a trial. The crucial question is who pays: changes caused by the factory's error are on them, changes caused by a new request from you are on you. Agree which is which in writing before work starts. 修模 (xiūmú) is repair or maintenance.
盖章	gài zhāng	to stamp a seal
感谢	gǎn xiè	to thank sincerely; gratitude (formal) — 感 gǎn + 谢 xiè (to thank; originally: water overflowing, pouring out one's feelings). Warmer and more sincere than 谢谢. Standard in writing, speeches, and formal situations. Also functions as a noun in 表示感谢.
高硼硅玻璃	gāo péng guī bō li	borosilicate glass — "High boron-silicon glass," resistant to thermal shock. 耐热 (nàirè) is heat-resistant, 温差 (wēnchā) the temperature difference it can survive, 爆裂 (bàoliè) bursting or shattering.
高铁	gāo tiě	high-speed rail — Trains are booked on the official 12306 app or site (it has an English version) or through travel apps, and your passport is the ticket: it is checked at the station entrance and at the gate. 动车 (dòngchē) are slightly slower bullet trains. Arrive early; large stations have security checks.
工厂	gōng chǎng	factory
工程师	gōng chéng shī	engineer — 工程 gōngchéng (engineering; project , lit. "work + process") + 师 shī (master; teacher; professional). Engineer , one of the most respected professional titles in China, associated with the nation's modernization drive. 工程 alone means a large project or undertaking: 三峡工程 = Three Gorges Project.
工人	gōng rén	worker; laborer
工业	gōng yè	industry; the industrial sector — 工 gōng (labor; work) + 业 yè (sector). Manufacturing industry as a branch of the economy: 重工业 "heavy industry," 工业化 "industrialization." Paired with 农业 (agriculture) in the standard rhetoric of national development.
工业区	gōng yè qū	industrial zone
工艺	gōng yì	craft; technique; craftsmanship — 工 gōng (work; skill) + 艺 yì (art; skill; technique). Craft as the intersection of technical skill and artistry. 工艺品 gōngyìpǐn = handicraft item; artisan product. 传统工艺 = traditional crafts. 工艺美术 = arts and crafts (as a discipline).
公差	gōng chā	tolerance — The allowed deviation from a dimension. Tight tolerances cost money; for parts that thread or seal against each other they decide whether the product works. 超差 (chāochā) is out of tolerance.
公斤	gōng jīn	kilogram — 公 gōng (metric) + 斤 jīn (a traditional unit of weight, about half a kilo). The standard kilogram. The 公 again marks the international unit against the older 斤.
公司	gōng sī	company; firm; corporation — 公 gōng (public; common) + 司 sī (to manage; an office). A jointly run enterprise, a body managed in common: 一家公司 "a company." Note the contrast with the homophone 私 sī (private).
公章	gōng zhāng	company seal — A Chinese company binds itself with its red seal more than with a signature. 合同专用章 (hétong zhuānyòngzhāng) is a seal used only for contracts and is equally valid.
供应	gōng yìng	to supply; to provide; supply — 供 gōng (to provide) + 应 yìng (to meet a need). To supply goods or services to meet a demand: 供应商 (supplier), 供应链 (supply chain), 供不应求 (supply falls short of demand). The yìng here is the answering of a need with provision.
供应链	gōng yìng liàn	supply chain
供应商	gōng yìng shāng	supplier
硅胶圈	guī jiāo quān	silicone seal ring — 硅胶 (guījiāo) is silicone; 密封圈 (mìfēngquān) is any sealing ring or gasket. Food-grade silicone should not smell; 异味 (yìwèi, an odd smell) and 发黄 (fāhuáng, yellowing) are the common complaints. 漏水 (lòushuǐ) is a leak.
贵公司	guì gōng sī	your company (honorific) — The polite way to refer to the other side's company; 贵厂 (guì chǎng) for a factory. Your own side is plain 我们公司 or 我们. 贵 is the same character as "expensive," here meaning "honoured."
海上	hǎi shàng	maritime
含税	hán shuì	tax included — 含 is "to contain." The pair 含税 / 不含税 appears on almost every RMB quotation; 含运费 (hán yùnfèi) is freight included, 含包装 packaging included. The same 含 works for anything the price might or might not cover.
汉朝	Hàn cháo	the Han dynasty — 汉 Hàn (the dynastic name) + 朝 cháo (dynasty, court). The dynasty (206 BCE to 220 CE) whose name became the name of a people. A defining golden age of unified empire, remembered as the standard for later eras.

天山	Tiān shān	The Heavenly Mountains — The Heavenly Mountains: the mountain range dividing northern and southern Xinjiang, with peaks reaching 7,443 m. A UNESCO World Natural Heritage Site, the Tianshan is the water source for the oasis cities of the Tarim Basin and one of the great mountain ranges of Central Asia.
条款	tiáo kuǎn	clause, term — 第五条 (dì-wǔ tiáo) is Article 5. 补充协议 (bǔchōng xiéyì) is a supplementary agreement, the usual way to add terms later. 附件 (fùjiàn) is an annex, where specifications and drawings belong.
通过	tōng guò	to pass through; to get past; by means of; via — 通 (to go through) + 过 guò (to cross; to pass). The everyday verb for getting through something: passing through a gate, passing an exam, a motion passing a vote.
图纸	tú zhǐ	technical drawing — 2D drawings with dimensions; 3D文件 or 3D图 (sān-D tú) for the model file, usually STEP. 以图纸为准 ("the drawing governs") is the sentence that settles arguments, so make sure the drawing is the latest version.
退货	tuì huò	to return goods
驼队	tuó duì	camel train
外发	wài fā	to send out (to a subcontractor) — The question the tour exists to answer. A factory that sends out a step is not necessarily hiding anything, but you need to know, because quality on that step is out of its direct control.
违法	wéi fǎ	to break the law; to act illegally — 违 wéi (to violate; to go against) + 法 fǎ (law). To step over the line the law draws: 违法行为 "an unlawful act." The mirror image of 合法, the foot landing outside the level mark.
违约金	wéi yu ē jīn	liquidated damages — A fixed sum payable on breach, such as late delivery. Under Chinese law a court or arbitrator can reduce it if it is far above the actual loss, so set it at a level you can justify. 赔偿 (péicháng) is compensation for actual loss.
维吾尔族	Wéi wú ěr zú	The Uyghur people , the Turkic-speaking Muslim majority population of the Tarim Basin oasis cities, one of China's fifty-five recognized ethnic minorities. The word Uyghur (维吾尔) is a transliteration of the Turkic name for the people, meaning roughly "united" or "allied."
尾款	wěi kuǎn	balance payment — "Tail money." What triggers it is the real negotiation: 出货前 (before shipment), 验货后 (after inspection passes), or 见提单副本 (jiàn tídān fùběn, against a copy of the bill of lading). The later the trigger, the more leverage you keep.
纬	wěi	weft thread; latitude
乌龙茶	wū lóng chá	oolong — also begins with a controlled wither
误会	wù huì	misunderstanding — Frames a disagreement as a communication gap rather than bad faith, which lets the other side correct course without losing face. Use it even when you suspect it was no accident.
西域	xī yù	the Western Regions
消费	xiāo fèi	to consume; consumption (opposite of production)
消费者	xiāo fèi zhě	consumer
效率	xiào lǜ	efficiency
协议	xié yì	agreement
写下来	xiě xià lái	write it down — For technical terms and every figure. Hand over your notebook or phone. Pinyin input is fine; most people will type the characters for you.
辛苦了	xīn kǔ le	thank you for your trouble — Acknowledges effort someone made for you: picking you up at the station, preparing samples, staying late. Factory staff hear it constantly from managers and appreciate it from a buyer. Answer with 不辛苦 or 应该的 (yīnggāi de, "it's what I should do").
行业	háng yè	industry; trade; profession (as a category) — 行 háng + 业 yè (occupation; enterprise). The trade as a category. Used for entire industries: 教育行业 the education sector; 服务行业 the service industry; 各行各业 "every trade, every profession" (the standard phrase for "all walks of life").
幸会	xìng huì	a pleasure to meet you — Formal "fortunate meeting," said with the handshake on first contact. Pairs naturally with 久仰 (jiǔyǎng, "I've long admired you") when you know the person by reputation. Plain 很高兴认识您 is never wrong.
需求	xū qiú	demand; need
玄奘	Xuán zàng	the monk who walked to India — Tang Buddhism's greatest figure — The monk 玄奘 Xuánzàng (602–664 CE) traveled overland to India in 629 CE , without imperial authorization, defying a travel ban , spent sixteen years there studying at Nalanda and other Buddhist centers, and returned to Chang'an in 645 CE carrying 657 Sanskrit texts.
学业	xu é yè	studies; schoolwork; academic work — 学 xué (to learn) + 业 yè (course of work). One's studies taken as a whole: 完成学业 "to complete one's studies," 学业成绩 "academic results." The general term for the body of work a student carries.
严重	yán zhòng	serious — Say plainly how serious it is; understatement is heard as "not urgent." 影响很大 (the impact is large) and 不能接受 (bù néng jiēshòu, cannot be accepted) are firm without being rude.
验货	yàn huò	goods inspection — Inspection before shipment, by your own staff or a 第三方 (dì-sān fāng, third party). 抽检 (chōujiǎn) is sampling inspection, usually by AQL level; 全检 (quánjiǎn) is 100% inspection. 来料检验 checks incoming materials.
样品间	yàng pǐn jiān	sample room
一带一路	yī dài yī lù	Belt and Road Initiative
一国两制	yī guó liǎng zhì	"One country, two systems" , the constitutional principle under which Hong Kong was reunified with China in 1997. Proposed by Deng Xiaoping, it promised Hong Kong fifty years of maintaining its existing legal, economic, and social systems under Chinese sovereignty. The principle expires in 2047.
伊斯兰教	Yī sī lán jiào	Islam — practiced by the 色目人 administrators and merchants of the Yuan
已读不回	yǐ dú bù huí	read but no reply
以中文版为准	yǐ Zhōng wén bǎn wéi zhǔn	the Chinese version governs — A bilingual contract needs a clause saying which version prevails if they conflict (如有冲突 rú yǒu chōngtū). Chinese courts and arbitrators work in Chinese, so the Chinese text is the one that will be read. Have it translated back to you independently rather than trusting that the two versions match.
饮茶	yǐn chá	yum cha; going for dim sum; drinking tea
用法	yòng fǎ	usage; how something is used — 用 yòng (to use) + 法 fǎ (way). The way a thing is properly used, especially a word or a tool: 这个词的用法 "the usage of this word." Built on the same "way of doing" sense as 方法.
优质产品	yōu zhì chǎn pǐn	high-quality product; premium product — 优质 yōuzhì (excellent quality , 优 excellent + 质 substance/quality) is the premium tier modifier. 打造优质产品 (to build premium products) is a common mission statement. Contrasts with 劣质产品 (lièzhì chǎnpǐn, inferior-quality products) , counterfeit or substandard goods.
邮件	yóu jiàn	mail; email; a postal item — 邮 yóu (post; mail) + 件 jiàn (a piece). Originally any item sent through the post. In everyday modern use it most often means email: 电子邮件 diànzǐ yóujiàn (electronic mail) is regularly shortened to 邮件. To send one is 发邮件 fā yóujiàn.
有效期	yǒu xiào qī	validity period — How long a quote holds. Factories tie it to material prices, especially steel, and to the exchange rate (汇率 huìlǜ). A quote with no date can be reopened at any time.
语法	yǔ fǎ	grammar — 语 yǔ (language; speech) + 法 fǎ (rule; law). The "law of language," the system of rules by which a language is built. The same 法 of fixed standard, here governing how words combine.
语音	yǔ yīn	voice message — Factory staff often send long voice messages. 转文字 (zhuǎn wénzì) converts a voice message to text inside WeChat (press and hold the message), and the result is easy to paste into a translator. Asking for text is acceptable when phrased as your limitation.
原因分析	yuán yīn fēn xī	cause analysis — The first thing to ask for. 根本原因 (gēnběn yuányīn) is the root cause; an answer like 工人操作不当 (worker error) usually is not one. Ask why the process allowed the error.
原则上同意	yuán zé shang tóng yì	agree in principle — Signals agreement on direction while keeping the details open. Useful when the boss pushes for a handshake before the numbers are settled.
运费	yùn fèi	freight charge — 海运 (hǎiyùn) is sea freight, 空运 (kōngyùn) air freight, 快递 (kuàidì) express courier for samples. 货代 (huòdài) is the freight forwarder. 整柜 (zhěngguì) is a full container load, 拼柜 (pīnguì) a shared one.
责任	zér èn	responsibility
张骞	Zhāng Qiān	Han diplomat who opened the routes
长期合作	cháng qī hé zuò	long-term cooperation — The phrase that justifies concessions on both sides: the factory gives on price for the sake of future orders, you give on terms for the sake of a stable partner. Invoke it when asking for something; hear it as a request when the other side uses it.
账户	zhàng hù	bank account — 收款账户 (shōukuǎn zhànghù) is the receiving account. Fraudsters impersonate suppliers by email and announce new bank details.
招待	zhāo dài	to host, hospitality — The thank-you at the end names the hospitality: 谢谢款待 or 谢谢您的热情招待.
真空保温	zhēn kōng bǎo wēn	vacuum insulation — Double-wall insulation: the air between liner and shell is pumped out, 抽真空 (chōu zhēnkōng). 保温 is keeping warm, 保冷 keeping cold; 保温测试 is the thermal retention test, and 保温效果 the result.
证据	zhèng jù	evidence
知识产权	zhī shi chǎn quán	intellectual property
职业	zhí yè	occupation; profession; vocational — 职 zhí (post; duty) + 业 yè (work). The line of work a person practices for a living: 职业选择 "choice of profession." As a modifier it means professional or vocational: 职业运动员 "professional athlete," 职业学校 "vocational school."
制品	zhì pǐn	manufactured article; processed product
制造	zhì zào	to manufacture (industrial process emphasis)
质保期	zhì bǎo qī	warranty period — How long the factory stands behind the goods. Pair it with 验收标准 (yànshōu biāozhǔn), the acceptance standard, normally the sealed sample and an AQL level.
质检	zhì jiǎn	quality inspection — Short for 质量检验. 品管 (pǐnguǎn) or 品质部 is the quality department. Ask to see the inspection records (检验记录 jiǎnyàn jìlù) for a recent batch, not only the procedure.
质量	zhì liàng	quality: how good something is (also physical mass) — 质 zhì (substance, nature) + 量 liàng (measure). In everyday speech 质量 is quality, the standard or grade of a product or piece of work: 质量好 (good quality), 提高质量 (to raise quality). In physics the same word is the technical term for mass. Context separates the two cleanly.
仲裁	zhòng cái	arbitration — A foreign court judgment is hard to enforce in China, while an arbitration award can be enforced under the New York Convention. Many contracts name 中国国际经济贸易仲裁委员会 (CIETAC) or the Hong Kong (HKIAC) or Singapore (SIAC) centres. 管辖 (guǎnxiá) is jurisdiction; 适用法律 (shìyòng fǎlǜ) the governing law.
周朝	Zhōu Cháo	the Zhou dynasty
专利	zhuān lì	patent — China has three kinds: 发明专利 (fāmíng zhuānlì) invention patents, 实用新型 (shíyòng xīnxíng) utility models for structural improvements, and 外观设计 (wàiguān shèjì) design patents for appearance.
专业	zhuān yè	major; specialty; professional, specialized — 专 zhuān (to specialize; sole) + 业 yè (field of study). As a noun, the major one studies: 我的专业是历史 "my major is history." As an adjective, professional or specialized: 专业知识 "specialized knowledge," 很专业 "very professional."
转口港	zhuǎn kǒu gǎng	Entrepôt , a port where goods are imported, stored, and re-exported without significant processing. Hong Kong's entire colonial economic logic was entrepôt trade, and it retains this function today as a major transshipment hub alongside its role as a financial center.
赚钱	zhuàn qián	To make money, to be profitable — To make money, to be profitable. The opposite pair is 赔钱 (péiqián) or 亏本 (kuīběn). 这个生意不赚钱: this business doesn't make money, the verdict that ends ventures.
作品	zuò pǐn	a work (of art, literature, music) — 作 zuò (to compose) + 品 pǐn (article; item). A finished creative work: 文学作品 "a literary work," 这是他最好的作品 "this is his finest work."
作为	zuò wéi	to act as; in the capacity of; conduct; achievement — 作 zuò (to do; to act) + 为 wéi (to be; to serve as). Two near-synonyms combined for emphasis on role or capacity. As a preposition phrase: 作为一个老师 (in the capacity of a teacher / as a teacher). As a noun: 他很有作为 (he has accomplished a great deal; he is a person of achievement).
作业	zuò yè	homework; assignment; (work) operation — 作 zuò (to do; to make) + 业 yè (coursework). The schoolwork a student is assigned: 做作业 "to do homework," 家庭作业 "home assignment." In technical contexts it also means a work operation, as in 水下作业 "underwater operations."
作用	zuò yòng	function; effect; role; action upon — 作 zuò (to do; to make; to act) + 用 yòng (function; use). The most abstract 用 compound: not the act of using, but the role or effect that something exerts. 作用 describes what an entity does in a system , its causal contribution.
作者	zuò zhě	author; writer; the one who made it — 作 zuò (to compose) + 者 zhě (the one who). The maker of a work, most often a writer but usable for any creator. Compare 作家 zuòjiā, which specifically means a professional writer or author by trade.
